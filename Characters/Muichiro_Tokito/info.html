Muichiro Tokito (時とき透とう 無む一いち郎ろう Tokitō Muichirō) is a major supporting character of Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba. He is a Demon Slayer of the Demon Slayer Corps and the Mist Hashira (霞かすみ柱ばしら Iwa Bashira) of the Taisho era. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 45 (Page 4). [3]

Quick Answers

What is known about Muichiro Tokito's early life and background?
Born to a woodcutter and his wife, Muichiro Tokito and his twin brother, Yuichiro, experienced the loss of their parents at the age of ten. Later, an encounter with a demon led to Yuichiro's death and Muichiro being grievously wounded while losing his memories. He was found by Amane Ubuyashiki and trained to become a Demon Slayer, eventually becoming the Mist Hashira.
What events lead to Muichiro Tokito's death in the series?
In his battle with Kokushibo, Muichiro sustains numerous mortal wounds as a result of the demon's Blood Demon Art. Despite Kokushibo's death, he succumbs shortly after the battle.
How does Muichiro's character evolve throughout the seasons of Demon Slayer?
Initially, Muichiro is cold, aloof, and solitary. Of few words and with little attachment to the people around him, he regains his memories in the midst of a battle and is able to reconcile with his lost grief and memories. He evolves to be closer with his peers, happier and more open to sharing his feelings.

Appearance

Muichiro is a boy of fair skin complexion, short stature, and a lean-muscular build along with long straight hair of a raven-black color that fades to a cyan color as it approaches his waist. He wears his hair loose with two clumps protruding slightly from each side of his head, a set of marginally shorter hair left down to frame his face, and unruly bangs over his forehead. He also possesses large, empty-looking cyan irises with a teal center. He is identical in appearance to his older twin brother, Yuichiro. He is described as thinner and smaller than Tanjiro but deceptively strong.


Following his meeting with Tanjiro Kamado, and the return of his childhood memories, Muichiro's once empty looking eyes seem to now have a hopeful glimmer that is accompanied with a more upbeat and cheery expression, reminiscent of his former demeanor before the death of Yuichiro.


Muichiro wears the standard black Demon Slayer Corps uniform, which is black in the manga and tinted dark teal in the anime. His uniform includes of a button-up jacket with a stand-up collar, featuring gold buttons, and the kanji for Destroy (滅めつ Metsu) engraved on the back of the uniform, with a shirt pocket on the left breast. Beneath the jacket, he wears a white long-sleeved collared undershirt, though not visible. However, unlike the standard shirt-like cuffed sleeves, Muichiro's uniform has longer, looser sleeves that are more reminiscent of a kimono, adding a flowing, ethereal quality to his appearance. Instead of the typical tattsuke-hakama pants wrapped with kyahan, Muichiro wears his hakama in the umanori style-a more traditional, horseback-riding cut which, combined with his oversized sleeves, gives his entire outfit a loose and oversized silhouette. His uniform is fastened at the waist with a white belt. For footwear, he wears dark teal tabi socks and a pair of white zōri sandals with teal straps.


When Muichiro was young, he wore a light, sleeveless yukata with a cyan mist design, similar to the darker one his brother wore when they were both ten.


Due to Tanjiro's presence around him, Muichiro manifested his Demon Slayer Mark during the battle against the Upper Rank Five Gyokko. Muichiro's mark is blood red colored, resembling mist shaped clouds on both his cheeks and the left side of his forehead.

Personality

Muichiro is an air-headed individual who seesm to constantly wander off in thought and is unable to focus on anything. Extremely impassive - and at times obtuse - he tends to think only for himself and operates on logic alone, never letting emotions guide him, making him seem apathetic and indifferent. However, Tanjiro Kamado sensed that Muichiro wasn't being apathetic on purpose as he didn't emanate any animosity. Despite his mortly uncaring and unserious demeanor, he has been shown to become serious when it is required and is intensely devoted to his position as the Demon Slayer Corps' Mist Hashira. Additionally, he seems to display a cold and arrogant side, as shown by him violently shaking down Kotetsu, calling him stupid, claiming that his time as a Hashira was important, more so than his own, and that all the swordsmiths could do was make weapons. Despite this, Muichiro doesn't seem to be indifferent towards Kagaya Ubuyashiki, most likely due to his immense respect for his master.


However, before the death of his twin brother when the siblings were eleven, Muichiro's personality was that of a much more compassionate and optimistic individual, the polar opposite of his current disposition. Having originally taken after his father, he was shown to display a higher level of kindness and understanding, believing he should aim to do good for others if he wanted good to be done for him. When told by Amane Ubuyashiki that they were descendants to Demon Slayers of old, Muichiro became excited at the prospect of being able to protect people.


After his brother was attacked by a demon and witnessing his final moments and death, Muichiro subsequently lost his memories and has held a perpetual rage deeply suppressed inside of him that inevitably drove his future, intense training. After this memory loss, he started exhibiting the indifferent attitude of his twin brother he is now known for as a Hashira, becoming hostile, aloof, and unbothered by the opinions of others. Following his battle with Gyokko and reuniting with Tanjiro after the latter's battle with Hantengu, he warmly thanked Tanjiro for helping him regain his identity and showed concern for his wellbeing.


During his later interactions with Tanjiro, Muichiro started regaining his memories of his past, causing him to behave more maturely and seriously, even choosing to help Kotetsu when his life was in danger instead of ignoring him. After his memory was fully restored, Muichiro began expressing more emotions and became more confident in himself, even stating to care more for his peers in the Demon Slayer Corps. He also started exhibiting emotions common for someone his age, particularly childishness and immaturity when interacting with other Demon Slayers. Muichiro also appears to be evidently biased towards Tanjiro after his battle with Gyokko in the Swordsmith Village, as his cheerful demeanor when around him was replaced by a cold indifference when around other Demon Slayers. However, he was shown to be more aware of himself and those around him, evident when he realized that Obanai Iguro had a crush on Mitsuri Kanroji, which surprised Gyomei Himejima. His kindness also aided him in building friendships among the Demon Slayers and making happy memories like the ones back when he lived with his family.

Demon Slayer Abilities

Demon Slayer Mark: During his battle against Gyokko, Muichiro's body reached the requirements to unlock the Demon Slayer Mark, which manifested as three, blood red mist-like clouds on both sides of his cheeks, and on his left forehead. While the mark is active, his vitality, speed, and strength are greatly enhanced, to the point where he not only shrugged off the effects of a powerful paralytic poison and used Mist Breathing forms in rapid succession, but also was capable of singlehandedly overwhelming and killing Upper Rank Five of the Twelve Kizuki. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 121 (Pages 14-15). [17] Later on, in his battle against Kokushibo, Muichiro manifested his Mark at will in order to fight the Upper Rank. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 165 (Page 11). [18]


Bright Red Nichirin Sword: During his battle against Kokushibo, Muichiro dyed his Nichirin Sword a bright red color by gripping it with all his might, allowing him to hamper the regeneration of Kokushibo, which later proved to play a pivotal role in his defeat. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 175 (Pages 10-11). [16]


Transparent World: Muichiro obtained the ability to see the Transparent World during the midst of his battle against Kokushibo, which allowed him to see the muscles, blood flow, and joint movement of his opponents, aiding him in landing a hit on the Upper Rank and keeping him in place and creating an opportunity for his allies to kill him. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 173 (Page 15). [10]

Supernatural Abilities

Extrasensory Perception: Muichiro has extremely honed senses able to pick up things outside the normal range of perception, such as discerning the aura of a demon's from a human's. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 102 (Page 7). [27] In his attempt to help Kozo Kanamori and Kotetsu reach the former's work shed for his new blade, Muichiro was able to sense the presence of Gyokko hidden in the bushes without seeing him, which surprised even the Upper Rank and earned him his compliments.


Fighting Style

Master Swordsman: As a Hashira of the Demon Slayer Corps, Muichiro is one of the most powerful and skilled swordsmen in the entire organization, possessing immense raw talent even when compared to his peers; at 11 years old, he would become a full fledged Hashira just two months after joining the Corps and first picking up a blade. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 45 (Page 4). [3] Despite his young age, Muichiro's swordsmanship was superior to many of his older peers, including Tanjiro Kamado, who - despite his own talent and combat experience - could barely keep up with Muichiro during Hashira Training. His skill impressed even the likes of Kokushibo, the strongest swordsman of the current era who had lived for over 30 times longer than Muichiro. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 165 (Pages 9-10). [28] In training, Muichiro could also keep up and even overpower Yoriichi Type Zero, a swordsman doll with six arms which was modeled after Yoriichi Tsugikuni, the strongest swordsman in history.


Breathing Style

Mist Breathing (霞かすみの呼こ吸きゅう Kasumi no kokyū): A Breathing Style Muichiro learnt from a Mist Breathing cultivator. As the Mist Hashira, Muichiro is his generation's most skilled Mist Breathing user. He became so proficient with Mist Breathing that he even created a new Mist Breathing technique, which ultimately enabled him to beat Gyokko. His skill with this Breathing Style impressed Kokushibo, who implied that his proficiency in this swordsmanship style was beyond his expectations. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 165 (Pages 9-10). [28] Moreover, Muichiro wears loose and oversized clothes that hide his limbs from his ememy's line of sight to obscure his movements when using Mist Breathing, further improving the unique strengths of this Breathing Style. Kimetsu no Yaiba First Fanbook. [29] Kimetsu no Yaiba Signs from the Wind. [30]


Techniques

  • First Form: Low Clouds, Distant Haze (壱いちノ型かた   垂すい天てん遠とお霞がすみ Ichi no kata: Suiten Tōgasumi) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga and Anime: Chapter 117 (Page 2) and Episode 51. [31] - The user performs a singular and powerful frontal thrust.
  • Second Form: Eight-Layered Mist (弐にノ型かた   八や重え霞かすみ Ni no kata: Yaekasumi) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga and Anime: Chapter 117 (Page 19) and Episode 51. [32] - The user performs eight slashes in quick succession.
  • Third Form: Scattering Mist Splash (参さんノ型かた   霞か散さんの飛し沫ぶき San no kata: Kasan no Shibuki) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga and Anime: Chapter 120 (Pages 8-9) and Episode 53. [33] - The user performs a powerful and swift circular slash that can blow away incoming projectiles.
  • Fourth Form: Shifting Flow Slash (肆しノ型かた   移い流りゅう斬ぎり Shi no kata: Iryūgiri) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga and Anime: Chapter 106 (Page 8) and Episode 52. [34] - The user takes a low stance before dashing toward the target and slashing them.
  • Fifth Form: Sea of Clouds and Haze (伍ごノ型かた   霞か雲うんの海うみ Go no kata: Garin Gyōbu) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga and Anime: Chapter 119 (Page 16) and Episode 52. [35] - The user charges at their target at blistering speeds in a zig-zag motion while performing a barrage of continuous slashes in front and/or around them.
  • Sixth Form: Lunar Dispersing Mist (伍ごノ型かた   月つきの霞か消しょう Roku no kata: Tsuki no Kashō) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga and Anime: Chapter 120 (Page 7) and Episode 53. [36] - The user leaps into the air and performs a multitude of slashes in a somersaulting motion, decimating dozens of targets at once.
  • Seventh Form: Obscuring Clouds (漆しちノ型かた   朧おぼろ Shichi no kata: Oboro) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga and Anime: Chapter 121 (Pages 8-9) and Episode 53. [37] - The personal creation of Muichiro. A movement-based technique designed to heavily disorient the target, which is done so by fluctuating the user's tempo. When the user appears before the target, they move incredibly slow, but then accelerate to blistering speeds before getting hit, making it seem like they disappeared into a cloud of mist. When done repeatedly, it can disorientate the target to the point of making them think they are being covered in a thick layer of mist.

Game Exclusive Techniques

  • Seventh Form: Obscuring Clouds, Undulation (漆しちノ型かた   朧おぼろ・揺よう曳えい Shichi no kata: Oboro - Yōei) Kimetsu no Yaiba Game: The Hinokami Chronicles 2. [38] - An offensive variant of Obscuring Clouds. As the technique is unleashed, the user constantly bombards their opponent with multiple slashes and cuts from multiple angles stealthily. Finally, the user delivers an incredibly powerful horizontal slash cleaving right through their opponent.
  • Dancing Love Haze (恋こい霞かすみ演えん舞ぶ Koi Kasumi Enbu) Kimetsu no Yaiba Game: The Hinokami Chronicles 2. [38] - A collaboration between a Love Breathing user and a Mist Breathing user. Both strike their opponent simultaneously with a cross-cut, the Mist Breathing user activating Obscuring Clouds, then they dash and slash the opponent twice. The Love Breathing user jumps in with a front flip slash, the Mist Breather dashing in with another slash while the Love Breather delivers more whip slashes with a twirl. The Mist Breather delivers three more slashes before the Love Breather delivers a somersaulting slash that crashes the opponent back, scattering dust. The Love and Mist Breathers then finish their attack by performing their third and fifth forms respectively (Catlove Shower and Sea of Clouds and Haze) while dashing in two directions.

Equipment

Standard Nichirin Katana: Muichiro carries around a standard sized and shaped Nichirin katana that is white in color (being a diamond teal color in the anime). As a Hashira, Muichiro's katana has the words Destroyer of Demons (惡あっ鬼き滅めっ殺さつ Akki Messatsu) engraved on the blade. His katana's tsuba is a rectangular frame on a black core, with smaller hollowed rectangles at each corner, all of which are gold in color. His handle is turquoise blue, wrapped with black cords, and capped with a gold kashira. Along with his katana, Muichiro also carries a standard sword sheath that is black in color.


After breaking his sword during his training with Yorrichi Type Zero, he takes on of the doll's katanas as a temporary replacement before Kozo Kanamori forges a new katana for him using the instructions left behind by his original swordsmith, Tetsuido. This replacement katana had a black blade, an indented gold and brown tsuba, a plain light grey handle, and a gold kashira.
