Tengen Uzui (宇う髄ずい 天てん元げん Uzui Tengen) is a major supporting character of Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba and a major character in the Entertainment District Arc. He was a former member of the Demon Slayer Corps, standing as the Sound Hashira (音おと柱ばしら Oto Bashira) of the Taisho era. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 45 (Page 3). [6]

Appearance

Tengen is a very tall, broad-shouldered, and incredibly muscular man with lightly-tanned skin and white hair. His hair is of an uneven length, the longest strands reaching his shoulders, and is styled with three more notable, shorter clumps arching up to fall as bangs between his eyes and on either side of his face. He has thin, dark fuschia eyes that appear to slant inwards, each with one long eyelash flowing to the far side of his face. He also painted his fingernails and toenails with claret and bright green color. He is considered very attractive as noted by many women and even by Zenitsu; this has led him to use his looks as a tool for his missions whenever persuasion is necessary.


During his service as the Sounds Hashira of the Demon Slayer Corps, Tengen wore a highly customized, sleeveless version of the standard Demon Slayer uniform. His uniform was black in the manga and tinted a deep dark turquoise in the anime. It consisted of a sleeveless button-up jacket with a stand-up collar, gold buttons, and a shirt pocket on the left breast, with the kanji for Destroy (滅めつ Metsu) engraved on the back of the uniform. Underneath, he wore a white sleeveless collared undershirt. The uniform was secured with a white belt at the waist and included matching tattsuke-hakama pants tucked into burgundy cloth wrappings around his calves. On his feet, he wore white zōri sandals with white straps and dark turquoise tabi socks to match his uniform. Tengen is accessorized flamboyantly, wearing two thick, golden rings around his upper arms and a pair of fingerless purple gloves. These gloves were secured with two silver bands around the middle fingers and fastened at the wrists with two thinner golden rings on each side.


As well as this, Tengen sports a white head-wrap that covers his hair, leaving only a thin ponytail visible to fall behind his head from between its layers. Adorning his forehead was a striking pale gray headband, embellished with six pale pink gemstones-three large ones arranged in a horizontal row across the center, and three smaller ones arranged in a triangular formation on the far left side. From each side of this headdress hung chains of smaller pale blue gemstones, which framed his face like earrings or decorative tassels. Additionally, Tengen wore distinctive red eye makeup on his left eye, composed of alternating large and small dots that extended inward toward the corner of his eye. He also wears a pair of two golden cuff earrings on each ear.


After his retirement from the Demon Slayer Corps due to the injuries he has sustained during his fight with Gyutaro, Tengen changes his appearance to coordinate the loss of his left eye and hand. He now wears a long, burgundy colored yukata with a white yuban collar along a light purple obi tied around the waist, and an indigo blue haori. He also replaces his old hear-wrap with a dark eyepatch adorned with what looked to be the remaining gemstones left over from his old headdress, leaving his hair to fall loosely past his shoulders. In addition, a long scar from where Gyutaro's kama sliced his left eye is visible under his eyepatch. He also wears a pair of zōri.

Personality

Tengen is an eccentric and flashy individual, always wanting to be "flamboyant", possessing a need for everything to be flashy and outstanding, even proclaiming that he is the god of flashiness and festivals. This is shown when he gets excited imagining how "flamboyant" it would be if Tanjiro Kamado's blood vessels exploded, and tries to forcefully haul Aoi Kanzaki off to the Entertainment District with him so he can unwillingly help him with a dangerous mission, even spanking her. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga and Anime: Chapter 70 (Page 18) and Episode 34. [7] As a result of his exuberant and flamboyant personality, Tengen is very demanding of others, treating the Demon Slayers very harshly during Hashira Training and insulting them for having no substance when they couldn't survive his stamina training. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 130 (Page 7). [8] Furthermore, he also abhors the idea of unflashy things, as shown when the Hashira openly expresses his hate for plainness. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 71 (Page 11). [9]


He is usually seen talking boastfully, proclaiming that he is "god" in front of Tanjiro, Zenitsu and Inosuke, even calling them trash and declaring that if he tells them to become dogs, they must become dogs, and if he tells them to become monkeys, they must become monkeys. Tengen even demands for the trio to always humor and stoop down to him, imploring him for favors and always doing their best to flatter him. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 71 (Page 7). [10] This was also shown in the initial encounter with Upper Rank Six, Gyutaro, proclaiming that he was in tip-top shape despite being severely poisoned. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 87 (Page 10). [11]


However, Tengen does possess an extremely caring side that is masked under his flamboyant façade, one he only shows to those that are extremely close to him, most notably his three wives, Makio, Suma and Hinatsuru, who Tengen is shown to truly love and care for, praising and encouraging Makio and Suma after their undercover mission Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 80 (Page 4). [12] and assuring Hinatsuru that she has completed her mission and telling her to get out of harm's way. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 77 (Page 4). [13] Furthermore, Tengen also declares that they must value their lives above all others as they are precious to him. This is especially shown when Gyutaro gags Hinatsuru with his hand, Tengen panics and furiously yells at the demon to leave his wife alone. After the events in Yoshiwara, Tengen also becomes more friendly towards Tanjiro, Zenitsu and Inosuke.


Although he can be very boastful and expressive, Tengen also shows a side of modesty and humility. He openly denied Gyutaro's statement that he was blessed with any talent, comparing himself to the other Hashira and especially Kyojuro Rengoku. This implicates a sort of inferiority complex, further proven by the fact that he told himself that he could never become someone as great as Kyojuro. Tengen is also shown to harbor regret over the fact that he has let so many lives slip through his fingers over the years. Kagaya Ubuyashiki praised Tengen for the hard path he chose and for facing forward and fought to protect human lives despite carrying all of his conflicts and contradictions he hides behind his loud and confident façade, which caused Tengen to deeply respect Kagaya from that moment on.


Tengen has exceptional compassion with strong morality, which Kagaya acknowledged. Despite being a shinobu that has traditionally valued completing the mission over all else, Tengen has rejected that ideology after seeing its brutal effect on his family, despising his father for forcing him and his siblings to endure through brutal training and even pitting them against each other in a final lethal test. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 87. [4] He believes that his subordinates, whether they be his wives or the Demon Slayers under his command, should value their lives and the lives of innocents over their missions or themselves. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 75. [14] Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 80. [15] Tengen especially hated the idea of becoming like his brother or father, who were apathetic towards human lives, wanting to desperately abandon what he was taught by them; even then, he chose to simply cut his ties instead of killing them due to his morality. He also chose to be a Demon Slayer to protect innocent people from being harmed by demons.


Following his retirement, Tengen was brought in by the active Hashira to assess fitness and stamina during Hashira Training. Tengen would prove to be a strict teacher, frequently yelling and berating the trainees for their weaknesses and exhaustion, whilst also amusing himself by intimidating them. Despite this, he does genuinely care for his pupils, as Makio mentioned that he did not eat until the last trainee returned, even going as far as to search the mountain to find them should they get lost. Tengen himself claimed that because Kagaya entrusted the trainees in his care, he wasn't going to let them burn out. In addition, according to his wives, Tengen still has a desire to fight and that he calls out in his sleep, blaming himself for relinquishing his position in the Corps. Kimetsu no Yaiba Anime: Episode 58. [16] Despite this, Tengen remained retired and dutifully served as a bodyguard to Nezuko and Kiriya Ubuyashiki alongside Shinjuro Rengoku, where he would go on to give his compliments to the son of his late mentor and friend Kagaya.


Abilities

Overall Abilities

As a Hashira of the Demon Slayer Corps, Tengen is a very powerful and skilled combatant. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 45. [17] He has displayed incredible physical feats of strength and speed, being seen as the second physically strongest, and the fastest runner of the Taisho era's Hashira. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Second Fanbook. [3] He could match the combat proficiency of both Daki and Gyutaro, both Upper Rank demons that have fought and killed numerous Hashira in the past, Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 88 (Page 6). [18] as well as having over a century of combat experience. Tengen was also able to effortlessly decapitate Daki without her even perceiving his movements, which caused him to believe she was no Upper Rank demon at all. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 84 (Page 19). [19] Impressively, he was able to fend off the combined efforts of Daki and Gyutaro while being infected by a poison that would kill someone quickly under normal circumstances, even managing to protect bystanders in the process. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 86 (Page 10). [20]


Tengen has also showcased great skill and precision in using explosives, minimizing damage dealt to the surroundings and maximizing the damage dealt to his opponents. Due to his shinobi training, Tengen has greater tactical skills and sharper senses than the average person, Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 89 (Page 17). [21] most notably his sense of hearing, which helped him greatly during the course of the battle with Upper Six. Even after losing his left hand to Gyutaro, Tengen was able to fight the Upper Rank on even terms due to completeing his Musical Score, allowing him to accurately predict and deflect Gyutaro's attacks, cut off his arm and pin him down at the cost of losing his left eye. Nonetheless, due to the poison circulating in his body, he was unable to attempt a finishing blow and needed Tanjiro to do so. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 93 (Pages 16-17). [22]


Indomitable Will: Tengen possesses an undaunted spirit and immense willpower, never faltering even when faced with disadvantages and difficult circumstances. Despite being poisoned in the beginning by Gyutaro, he boldly proclaimed he could fight just fine, even attacking both Daki and Gyutaro at the same time. He appeared to be ecstatic while deflecting multiple poison blood blades even with a missing hand and numerous cuts in his skin. Even when his left eye gets slashed out, he pushed through all the damage until the demons were defeated. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 87 (Pages 9-11). [23]


Shinobi Training: Before joining the Demon Slayer Corps and becoming a Hashira, Tengen descended from a family of shinobi. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga and Anime: Chapter 70 (Page 11) and Episode 34. [24] Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 87 (Page 6). [25] Tengen received extremely harsh training that killed all of his siblings except for his younger brother, who became cold and heartless. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 87 (Page 7). [26] However, as a result of his intensive shinobi training, Tengen possesses great technical skill in weaponry such as kunai and explosives. His training also made him extremely pain tolerant and poison resistant, which saved him from Gyutaro's poison and allowed him to fight despite suffering numerous injuries and losing his hand.

  • Enhanced Stealt: Tengen is extremely stealthy, moving so silently that he doesn't make a single sound nor disturb the air. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 75 (Page 5). [27] His stealth allowed him to evade the senses of both Tanjiro and Inosuke, both of which possessing a heightened sense of smell and touch respectively. Later, Tengen was able to sneak up on and decapitate Daki, an Upper Rank demon, without her noticing. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 84 (Page 19). [19]
  • Poison Resistance: As a former shinobi, Tengen has been shown to possess high resistance to poison to the point where he could continue fighting while under the effects of a poison from Gyutaro that would have killed someone instantly under normal circumstances. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 87 (Page 6). [25]


As another testament to his abilities, Akaza, the Upper Rank Three and one of the strongest known demons, acknowledged and praised Kyojuro's strength. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 63 (Page 19). [5] He even went as far as to offer to turn him into a demon so he could surpass his human limits and become stronger, something he only offered to swordsmen whose abilities he believed were refined to their utmost peak. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 63 (Page 8). [6] Akaza also complimented his fighting spirit, stating that it was getting close to the "Realm of the Highest".


Indomitable Will: Kyojuro had an undaunted spirit and indomitable willpower, which even impressed the likes of Upper Rank Three, Akaza. Ever since he was a child, he has had a strong will and passion in himself due to his promise to his late mother. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 64 (Pages 13-14). [7] Even when the Upper Rank overwhelmed him with powerful attacks, giving Kyojuro insidious wounds, he remained steadfast and resolute. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 64. [8] After Kyojuro lost an eye and had internal wounds caused by Akaza, he still persisted, vehemently expressing his unwavering will and determination, saying that he will protect the lives of the other innocent people no matter what. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 64 (Pages 6-8). [9] Finally, when Akaza's fist pierced his solar plexus, it triggered a memory reminding Kyojuro of his mother again, giving him the final push to cut the Upper Rank's neck. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 64 (Pages 14-17). [10] In the end, though his efforts were in vain, his incredible determination had a profound effect on Tanjiro, giving the incentive to push on no matter the turmoil he faced. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 66. [11]


Keen Intellect: As a swordsmen who came from a lineage of Flame Hashira, Kyojuro had access to generations of combat knowledge and tactics as well as training from previous Flame Hashira, like his father, Shinjuro Rengoku. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 81 (Page 2). [4] Kyojuro could also master the entire Flame Breathing style just from reading 3 volumes of a book on it. During the battle against Akaza, he displayed keen judgement like after being attacked by Akaza's Destructive Death: Air Type, he realizes that he must fight at close range in order to increase the chances of cutting off his head. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 63 (Pages 17-18). [12] Even when the usually deductive and sagacious Tanjiro Kamado was at a loss of how to kill Enmu, Kyojuro instinctively knew that, no matter what form a demon takes, it will always have a weak spot. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 60 (Page 15). [13]


He also possesses great knowledge in the use of the Total Concentration Breathing (全ぜん集しゅう中ちゅうの呼こ吸きゅう Zen Shūchū no Kokyū), knowing how to plug minor wounds and hemorrhaging and saved Tanjiro from dying of blood loss. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 62 (Pages 21-23). [14] As another testament to his tactical intellect, Kyojuro could instantly read his allies' sign language at indicating the Flute Demon's abilities despite being unable to fully control their movements and execute a countermeasure before the demon's Blood Demon Art befell him.


Physical Abilities

Immense Speed & Reflexes: As a Hashira, Kyojuro possessed tremendous speed and reflexes, so much so that even gifted Demon Slayers like Tanjiro and Inosuke Hashibira were unable to register his clash with Akaza, with the latter stating that he was in a different dimension. Kyojuro could even cause tremors to the ground and nearly launching a train off its tracks by running at such high speeds. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 60 (Page 16). [15] Moreover, Kyojuro could easily move at speeds much faster than the eye can see, decapitating demons before they can even react. His reflexes were so fast that he managed to cut Akaza's arm moments before the Upper Rank was going to hit Tanjiro. Kyojuro also managed to keep up with Akaza's high-speed techniques and incredibly swift movements, which he remarks is faster than the blink of an eye, Kimetsu no Yaiba Movie: Mugen Train. [16] sometimes even managing to out-speed him. This surprised and impressed Akaza himself, who openly expressed his amazement over Kyojuro's skill. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 63 (Page 18). [17]


Immense Stamina & Endurance: Kyojuro displayed superhuman stamina and endurance during his battles over the course of the series. In his battle with the Flute Demon, Kyojuro ruptured his eardrums, yet didn't flinch in the slightest, ignoring the pain and continuing to fight and win. In his battle with Hairo, he suffered dozens of severe wounds and injuries from gunshots and explosions coming from the former Lower Rank Two but continued to stall enough time for all the bombs he planted to be disarmed without showing any signs of pain or fatigue, which shocked Hairo into questioning whether the person he met in the past was really the same person he was fighting now. Furthermore, his battle against Enmu where he fused with an entire train, he was able to keep him at bay in five different train carts without tiring. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 62 (Pages 16-17). [18]


His incredible stamina and endurance was best displayed when he managed to keep up with Akaza, one of the strongest demons in existance, despite physically and mentally exerting himself beyond his limit and sustaining multiple irreparable and fatal wounds, like a smashed eye, shattered ribs and damaged internal organs. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 64 (Page 4). [19] Most impressively, he managed to keep Akaza at bay despite having his solar plexus pierced by the Upper Rank's arm. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 64 (Page 17). [20] Despite all of his fatal wounds, he did not once let go of his hold on Akaza in his attempt to let the dawn's light kill him, eventually forcing the demon to rip off his own arms to finally escape.


Immense Strength: Having spent years perfecting his physical prowess, Kyojuro possessed amazing physical strength, being capable of swinging his katana at speeds faster than the eye can see incessantly with faultless accuracy while engaging in combat and even sending Akaza flying an astonishing distance into a forest with a single blade swing. Kyojuro could also lift and entire train off its tracks by simply dashing forward. Even when he first became a recognized Demon Slayer, he had enough strength to instantly rupture his own eardrums by hitting his ears with his hands. Kyojuro also possessed exceptional grip strength that enabled him to hold down Akaza, one of the physically strongest characters in the series, despite having his solar plexus pierced earlier and sustaining multiple other fatal wounds, to the point which Akaza expressed his disbelief and shock over Kyojuro's strength which he describes as "unbelievable". Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 64 (Pages 16-17). [21]


Supernatural Abilities

Extrasensory Perception: Kyojuro had incredibly honed senses, such as when he was able to sense and perceive Akaza's immense presence and demon aura. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 63 (Page 6). [22] Kyojuro was also capable of sensing demonic presences faster and more accurately than Tanjiro Kamdo, who harbors an acute sense of smell, when he detected two demons right after another on a train before anyone else noticed. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 54 (Page 12). [23] This ability also allowed him to precisely gauge the regenerative abilities and weaknesses of most demons, like Enmu. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 60 (Page 15). [13] Kyojuro was also able to perceive Tanjiro's injuries and torn blood vessel accurately, which allowed him to teach him how to plug up that wound and prevent him from dying of blood loss. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 60 (Page 15). [13] Furthermore, he was able to detect malice in Enmu's spells and prevented himself from getting incapacitated.


Fighting Style

Master Swordsman: As a Hashira of the Demon Slayer Corps, Kyojuro was one of the most powerful and skilled swordsmen in the entire organization. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 45. [24] Additionally, Kyojuro is part of the Rengoku family which has an impressive lineage of powerful swordsmen and Flame Hashira, giving Kyojuro an edge in developing and polishing his swordsmanship. His extraordinary swordsmanship skill has been displayed on multiple occasions across the series.


In his battle against the former Lower Rank Two, Hairo, Kyojuro was able to out-wield the demon's bushido sword style, which Hairo appraised as he was decapitated. Kyojuro's precise blade work could also slow down Enmu in his fused form, which gave him the opportunity to explain his plan to Tanjiro and Inosuke. His mastery of the blade is best showcased during his battle with Akaza, in which he was more than capable of fighting on equal ground with the Upper Rank who had killed countless Demon Slayers and even multiple Hashiras over the last century, even to the point that Akaza constantly praised Kyojuro's swordsmanship.


Breathing Style

Flame Breathing (炎ほのおの呼こ吸きゅう Honō no kokyū): A Breathing Style taught to Kyojuro by the previous Flame Hashira, Shinjuro Rengoku, and later mastered by reading the instruction books left behind by previous Flame Hashira in the Rengoku Family. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 81 (Page 2). [4] As the Flame Hashira, Kyojuro is his generation's most skilled Flame Breathing user. He became evidently proficient in fighting with this Breathing Style, being able to singlehandedly defeat former Lower Rank Two, Hairo and later hold his own against Upper Rank Three, Akaza. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 64. [8]


Techniques

  • First Form: Unknowing Fire (壱いちノ型かた   不知火しらぬい Ichi no kata: Shiranui) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga and Anime: Chapter 54 (Page 15) and Episode 28. [25] - The user dashes towards the target at tremendous speeds, before unleashing a singular horizontal slash directed at the targets neck for a decapitation.
  • Second Form: Rising Scorching Sun (弐にノ型かた   昇のぼり炎えん天てん Ni no kata: Nobori En Ten) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga and Anime: Chapter 63 (Page 5) and Episode 28. [26] - The user unleashes an arcing vertical slash in an upwards motion, often brought up from a tail guard.
  • Third Form: Blazing Universe (参さんノ型かた   気き炎えん万ばん象しょう San no kata: Kien Banshō) Kimetsu no Yaiba Anime: Episode 32. [27] - The user performs an arcing downwards vertical slash, often brought down from a high guard.
  • Fourth Form: Blooming Flame Undulation (肆しノ型かた   盛せい炎えんのうねり Shi no kata: Sei En no Uneri) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga and Anime: Chapter 63 (Page 17) and Episode 32. [28] - The user performs a singular slash or multiple if needed, in a fluid circular motion to defend from incoming attacks and decapitate multiple targets.
  • Fifth Form: Flame Tiger (伍ごノ型かた   炎えん虎こ Go no kata: Enko) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga and Anime: Chapter 63 (Page 21) and Episode 32. [29] - The user dashes forth, bringing their blade into a high guard before performing a series of sword slashes which take the form of a flaming tiger enveloping the user.
  • Ninth Form: Rengoku (玖くノ型かた   煉れん獄ごく Ku no kata: Rengoku) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga and Anime: Chapter 64 (Pages 8-9) and Episode 33. [30] - The most powerful Flame Breathing technique. The user assumes a high stance before performing an extremely high-speed dash towards the target and unleashing a singular, devastating slash. The technique is powerful enough to completely carve out the ground in its wake. In the movie and anime adaptation, this technique seemingly took the form of a flaming Japanese dragon that envelopes the user as they are charging towards the target.

Game Exclusive Techniques

  • Fifth Form: Flame Tiger, Fiery Roar (伍ごノ型かた   炎えん虎こ・咆ほう Go no kata: Enko - Hō) Kimetsu no Yaiba Game: The Hinokami Chronicles 2 [31] - A slightly longer version of Flame Tiger. Kyojuro starts with a left slash to knock the opponent back. Upon connection, Kyojuro charges towards and slashes them several times. Afterwards, he performs Flame Tiger and finishes with an extremely powerful slash with enough momentum to fling his opponent upwards.
  • Hearts Ablaze (心こころを燃もやせ Kokoro o Moyase) Kimetsu no Yaiba Game: The Hinokami Chronicles 2 [31] - A collaboration between a Flame Breathing user and a Sun Breathing user. Both coordinate their attacks to simultaneously strike their opponent with arc-shaped slashes from their respective Breathing Styles from opposite sides. The Flame Breather then readies his stance to use Rengoku and the Sun Breather takes a similar stance to use Sun Halo Dragon Head Dance. The result is both charging at the opponent in an attack that takes the form of a flaming Japanese dragon composed of flames from both styles. Both users yell "Set your heart ablaze!", and their attack ends with an X-shaped slash and a fiery tornado.
  • Fervent Fulmination (爆ばく炎えん響きょう動どう Bakuen Kyōdō) Kimetsu no Yaiba Game: The Hinokami Chronicles 2 [31] - A collaboration between a Flame Breathing user and a Sound Breathing user. Both charge at their opponent and knock them back, and when they catch up, both coordinate their attacks to send their opponent in each other's direction. They Sound Breather then hurls explosive beads at their opponent after they are sent airborne, and is then joined by the Flame Breather who executes Flame Tiger as they jump upward. When they make contact with the target, the result is them being attacked by the blades of both Flame and Sounds Breathing users, as well as being caught in the explosions from the latter's beads. The attack culminates with large fireworks as the users land on the ground.
  • Enthusiastic Educator (熱ねっ血けつ授じゅぎ業ょう Nekketsu Jugyō) Kimetsu no Yaiba Game: The Hinokami Chronicles 2 [31] - A technique exclusive to the Kimetsu Academy version of Kyojuro Rengoku. The passionate History teacher gives his opponent a lecture only to be disappointed when they are disinterested after some time. Kyojuro then rapidly and determinedly writes on the chalkboard so furiously that it causes flames to appear and burn the opponent. Alternatively, if the opponent is actually paying attention, Kyojuro compliments them before offering extra credit, also burning the opponent by writing with his chalk, talking about how interesting history is.

Equipment

Standard Nichirin Katana: Kypjuro carried a standard sized and shaped Nichirin katana. The blade being a deep reddish-black color with red flame-like pattern engraved on both sides, along with the words Destroyer of Demons (惡あっ鬼き滅めっ殺さつ Akki Messatsu) engraved on the side of the blade. The handle of the katana is wrapped in cords of white silk, and capped with an orange kashira with yellow flames. The tsuba of Kyojuro's katana is shaped like a flame, with yellow filling in the middle of the tsuba, a dark red core, and orange border. He also carried a standard sword sheath that was white and grey patterned in color.


In Kyojuro's final stand against Akaza, the demon snaps the blade off its handle as Kyojuro actively tries to behead him, discarding it in a forest as he flees from the sunlight.


In time, the katana's tsuba, normally attached to the blade of a Flame Breather, would eventually come into the ownership of Tanjiro Kamado. Despite his own insistence to keep it within the Rengoku family's ownership, Senjuro had wanted him to keep it as homage to his late brother.

Trivia

  • Kyojuro's surname means "purgatory" (煉れん獄ごく rengoku). His name contains the Kan'yō-on'yomi reading of the kanji for "apricot" (杏きょう kyō), and the Go-on'yomi for both "longevity" (寿じゅ ju) and "son" (郎ろう rō).
  • Kyojuro's rankings in the popularity polls are as follows:
    • Kyojuro ranked 7th place in both the first and second popularity poll with 1,021 votes and 8,000 votes respectively. WSJ Manga Twitter Account [32]
  • Kyojuro was born in the Ebara District in Tokyo. Kimetsu no Yaiba First Fanbook [33]
  • Kyojuro loved to watch Noh, Kabuki and sumo wrestling. Kimetsu no Yaiba First Fanbook [33]
  • Kyojuro's favorite food was miso soup with sweet potatoes. Kimetsu no Yaiba First Fanbook [33]
  • It is revealed in Mount Natagumo Arc movie's Taisho Secret that Kyojuro's favorite lunch food are grilled sea bream and sweet potato rice.
  • Kyojuro was never allowed to have a pet because his father hated animals. Kimetsu no Yaiba First Fanbook [33]
  • During his first mission, Kyojuro ruptured his own eardrums to counter the Flute_Demon's Blood Demon Art. It is unknown but highly unlikely that this permanently affected his hearing. Kimetsu no Yaiba Extra: Special One-Shot [34]
  • Kyojuro has two favorite sayings/mottos: "If you put in your all, you'll stand your ground even in death" (斃たおれて後のちやむ Taorete nochi yamu) and "Nothing's impossible if you put your mind to it." (精せい神しん一いち倒何なに事ごとか成ならざらん Seishin ittou, nanigoto ka narazaran) Rengoku Volume 0 [35]
  • It has been revealed that Kyojuro does have a real-life model and that most of the characters are modeled after people in real life. They're typically normal people who the author happens to know.
  • The Rengoku family has long since had a practice called "Kankagari" (觀かん篝かがり Kankagari). For the duration of their pregnancies/gestation period, every seven days the wives will spend two hours gazing at a large flaming torch. This seems to be what causes the men in the family to have flame colored hair.
    • Conversely, in his Gaiden, Kyojuro states that his very distant ancestor ate a lot of tempuras causing their descendants' and their own hair color to become quite abnormal. However, given that Kyojuro was attempting to diffuse a situation between Mitsuri and an irate mother, it's likely that he was joking to entertain the latter's child who called his hair strange.
  • Kyojuro viewed the other Hashira as follows:
    • "She brews poisons! Isn't that amazing? And she doesn't hesistate to fight despite her disadvantageous size! I respect that!" - Shinobu Kocho
    • "He's more talented that I was at his age! He's young and works hard, which is admirable!" - Muichiro Tokito
    • "He's got sharp senses! And his shoulder, elbow, and wrist joints are more flexible that even Kanroji's!" - Obanai Iguro
    • "I wish I had his bulk! It's amazing how he can easily sling around that ungainly weapon! That takes some muscle!" - Gyomei Himejima
    • "He speaks so softly that I can't hear him! But he works hard!" - Giyu Tomioka
    • "The way he handles a blade is beautiful! He's the first shinobi I ever saw! Talk about stunning! He's got many wives though!" - Tengen Uzui
    • "Her talent is amazing! And her techniques are creative! I consider her an endearing junior corps member!" - Mitsuri Kanroji
    • "He's the guy who took Wind Breathing to the next level! Very admirable!" - Sanemi Shinazugawa
  • Kyojuro's level of openness was ranked 95%.
  • Many of his peers are unable to keep up with Kyojuro since he is "too fast" (He would reach conclusions quickly, he's quick to change his mind, he would eat quickly etc.). Despite this, he was sensitive to other people's emotions and was caring, making most of the Corps members like him.
  • It was revealed in a Taisho Secret in Episode 28 that Kyojuro doesn't have a Tsuguko because of how hard his training is and they end up running away. However, although it was known that Mitsuri was a student of Kyojuro's, she wasn't confirmed to have been his Tsuguko until the Taisho Secret in Episode 63.
  • Kyojuro is one of only four Hashira that use a normal Nichirin Katana. The other three are Giyu Tomioka, Muichiro Tokito and Sanemi Shinazugawa.
  • Kyojuro is one of the only three characters who fought an Upper Rank all by themselves, the other two being Zenitsu Agatsuma and Muichiro Tokito.
  • In Kimetsu Academy, Kyojuro is a history teacher. Because of his passion of teaching and his love of history, he began hurling his students and starting cavalry battles during class. He's loved by his students, and nobody in this academy has terrible grades in history. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Volume 7, (Extra Pages). [36]
