Tengen Uzui (宇う髄ずい 天てん元げん Uzui Tengen) is a major supporting character of Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba and a major character in the Entertainment District Arc. He was a former member of the Demon Slayer Corps, standing as the Sound Hashira (音おと柱ばしら Oto Bashira) of the Taisho era. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 45 (Page 3). [6]

Appearance

Tengen is a very tall, broad-shouldered, and incredibly muscular man with lightly-tanned skin and white hair. His hair is of an uneven length, the longest strands reaching his shoulders, and is styled with three more notable, shorter clumps arching up to fall as bangs between his eyes and on either side of his face. He has thin, dark fuschia eyes that appear to slant inwards, each with one long eyelash flowing to the far side of his face. He also painted his fingernails and toenails with claret and bright green color. He is considered very attractive as noted by many women and even by Zenitsu; this has led him to use his looks as a tool for his missions whenever persuasion is necessary.


During his service as the Sounds Hashira of the Demon Slayer Corps, Tengen wore a highly customized, sleeveless version of the standard Demon Slayer uniform. His uniform was black in the manga and tinted a deep dark turquoise in the anime. It consisted of a sleeveless button-up jacket with a stand-up collar, gold buttons, and a shirt pocket on the left breast, with the kanji for Destroy (滅めつ Metsu) engraved on the back of the uniform. Underneath, he wore a white sleeveless collared undershirt. The uniform was secured with a white belt at the waist and included matching tattsuke-hakama pants tucked into burgundy cloth wrappings around his calves. On his feet, he wore white zōri sandals with white straps and dark turquoise tabi socks to match his uniform. Tengen is accessorized flamboyantly, wearing two thick, golden rings around his upper arms and a pair of fingerless purple gloves. These gloves were secured with two silver bands around the middle fingers and fastened at the wrists with two thinner golden rings on each side.


As well as this, Tengen sports a white head-wrap that covers his hair, leaving only a thin ponytail visible to fall behind his head from between its layers. Adorning his forehead was a striking pale gray headband, embellished with six pale pink gemstones-three large ones arranged in a horizontal row across the center, and three smaller ones arranged in a triangular formation on the far left side. From each side of this headdress hung chains of smaller pale blue gemstones, which framed his face like earrings or decorative tassels. Additionally, Tengen wore distinctive red eye makeup on his left eye, composed of alternating large and small dots that extended inward toward the corner of his eye. He also wears a pair of two golden cuff earrings on each ear.


After his retirement from the Demon Slayer Corps due to the injuries he has sustained during his fight with Gyutaro, Tengen changes his appearance to coordinate the loss of his left eye and hand. He now wears a long, burgundy colored yukata with a white yuban collar along a light purple obi tied around the waist, and an indigo blue haori. He also replaces his old hear-wrap with a dark eyepatch adorned with what looked to be the remaining gemstones left over from his old headdress, leaving his hair to fall loosely past his shoulders. In addition, a long scar from where Gyutaro's kama sliced his left eye is visible under his eyepatch. He also wears a pair of zōri.

Personality

Tengen is an eccentric and flashy individual, always wanting to be "flamboyant", possessing a need for everything to be flashy and outstanding, even proclaiming that he is the god of flashiness and festivals. This is shown when he gets excited imagining how "flamboyant" it would be if Tanjiro Kamado's blood vessels exploded, and tries to forcefully haul Aoi Kanzaki off to the Entertainment District with him so he can unwillingly help him with a dangerous mission, even spanking her. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga and Anime: Chapter 70 (Page 18) and Episode 34. [7] As a result of his exuberant and flamboyant personality, Tengen is very demanding of others, treating the Demon Slayers very harshly during Hashira Training and insulting them for having no substance when they couldn't survive his stamina training. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 130 (Page 7). [8] Furthermore, he also abhors the idea of unflashy things, as shown when the Hashira openly expresses his hate for plainness. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 71 (Page 11). [9]


He is usually seen talking boastfully, proclaiming that he is "god" in front of Tanjiro, Zenitsu and Inosuke, even calling them trash and declaring that if he tells them to become dogs, they must become dogs, and if he tells them to become monkeys, they must become monkeys. Tengen even demands for the trio to always humor and stoop down to him, imploring him for favors and always doing their best to flatter him. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 71 (Page 7). [10] This was also shown in the initial encounter with Upper Rank Six, Gyutaro, proclaiming that he was in tip-top shape despite being severely poisoned. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 87 (Page 10). [11]


However, Tengen does possess an extremely caring side that is masked under his flamboyant façade, one he only shows to those that are extremely close to him, most notably his three wives, Makio, Suma and Hinatsuru, who Tengen is shown to truly love and care for, praising and encouraging Makio and Suma after their undercover mission Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 80 (Page 4). [12] and assuring Hinatsuru that she has completed her mission and telling her to get out of harm's way. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 77 (Page 4). [13] Furthermore, Tengen also declares that they must value their lives above all others as they are precious to him. This is especially shown when Gyutaro gags Hinatsuru with his hand, Tengen panics and furiously yells at the demon to leave his wife alone. After the events in Yoshiwara, Tengen also becomes more friendly towards Tanjiro, Zenitsu and Inosuke.


Although he can be very boastful and expressive, Tengen also shows a side of modesty and humility. He openly denied Gyutaro's statement that he was blessed with any talent, comparing himself to the other Hashira and especially Kyojuro Rengoku. This implicates a sort of inferiority complex, further proven by the fact that he told himself that he could never become someone as great as Kyojuro. Tengen is also shown to harbor regret over the fact that he has let so many lives slip through his fingers over the years. Kagaya Ubuyashiki praised Tengen for the hard path he chose and for facing forward and fought to protect human lives despite carrying all of his conflicts and contradictions he hides behind his loud and confident façade, which caused Tengen to deeply respect Kagaya from that moment on.


Tengen has exceptional compassion with strong morality, which Kagaya acknowledged. Despite being a shinobu that has traditionally valued completing the mission over all else, Tengen has rejected that ideology after seeing its brutal effect on his family, despising his father for forcing him and his siblings to endure through brutal training and even pitting them against each other in a final lethal test. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 87. [4] He believes that his subordinates, whether they be his wives or the Demon Slayers under his command, should value their lives and the lives of innocents over their missions or themselves. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 75. [14] Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 80. [15] Tengen especially hated the idea of becoming like his brother or father, who were apathetic towards human lives, wanting to desperately abandon what he was taught by them; even then, he chose to simply cut his ties instead of killing them due to his morality. He also chose to be a Demon Slayer to protect innocent people from being harmed by demons.


Following his retirement, Tengen was brought in by the active Hashira to assess fitness and stamina during Hashira Training. Tengen would prove to be a strict teacher, frequently yelling and berating the trainees for their weaknesses and exhaustion, whilst also amusing himself by intimidating them. Despite this, he does genuinely care for his pupils, as Makio mentioned that he did not eat until the last trainee returned, even going as far as to search the mountain to find them should they get lost. Tengen himself claimed that because Kagaya entrusted the trainees in his care, he wasn't going to let them burn out. In addition, according to his wives, Tengen still has a desire to fight and that he calls out in his sleep, blaming himself for relinquishing his position in the Corps. Kimetsu no Yaiba Anime: Episode 58. [16] Despite this, Tengen remained retired and dutifully served as a bodyguard to Nezuko and Kiriya Ubuyashiki alongside Shinjuro Rengoku, where he would go on to give his compliments to the son of his late mentor and friend Kagaya.


Abilities

Overall Abilities

As a Hashira of the Demon Slayer Corps, Tengen is a very powerful and skilled combatant. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 45. [17] He has displayed incredible physical feats of strength and speed, being seen as the second physically strongest, and the fastest runner of the Taisho era's Hashira. Kimetsu no Yaiba Second Fanbook. [3] He could match the combat proficiency of both Daki and Gyutaro, both Upper Rank demons that have fought and killed numerous Hashira in the past, Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 88 (Page 6). [18] as well as having over a century of combat experience. Tengen was also able to effortlessly decapitate Daki without her even perceiving his movements, which caused him to believe she was no Upper Rank demon at all. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 84 (Page 19). [19] Impressively, he was able to fend off the combined efforts of Daki and Gyutaro while being infected by a poison that would kill someone quickly under normal circumstances, even managing to protect bystanders in the process. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 86 (Page 10). [20]


Tengen has also showcased great skill and precision in using explosives, minimizing damage dealt to the surroundings and maximizing the damage dealt to his opponents. Due to his shinobi training, Tengen has greater tactical skills and sharper senses than the average person, Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 89 (Page 17). [21] most notably his sense of hearing, which helped him greatly during the course of the battle with Upper Six. Even after losing his left hand to Gyutaro, Tengen was able to fight the Upper Rank on even terms due to completeing his Musical Score, allowing him to accurately predict and deflect Gyutaro's attacks, cut off his arm and pin him down at the cost of losing his left eye. Nonetheless, due to the poison circulating in his body, he was unable to attempt a finishing blow and needed Tanjiro to do so. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 93 (Pages 16-17). [22]


Indomitable Will: Tengen possesses an undaunted spirit and immense willpower, never faltering even when faced with disadvantages and difficult circumstances. Despite being poisoned in the beginning by Gyutaro, he boldly proclaimed he could fight just fine, even attacking both Daki and Gyutaro at the same time. He appeared to be ecstatic while deflecting multiple poison blood blades even with a missing hand and numerous cuts in his skin. Even when his left eye gets slashed out, he pushed through all the damage until the demons were defeated. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 87 (Pages 9-11). [23]


Shinobi Training: Before joining the Demon Slayer Corps and becoming a Hashira, Tengen descended from a family of shinobi. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga and Anime: Chapter 70 (Page 11) and Episode 34. [24] Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 87 (Page 6). [25] Tengen received extremely harsh training that killed all of his siblings except for his younger brother, who became cold and heartless. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 87 (Page 7). [26] However, as a result of his intensive shinobi training, Tengen possesses great technical skill in weaponry such as kunai and explosives. His training also made him extremely pain tolerant and poison resistant, which saved him from Gyutaro's poison and allowed him to fight despite suffering numerous injuries and losing his hand.

  • Enhanced Stealt: Tengen is extremely stealthy, moving so silently that he doesn't make a single sound nor disturb the air. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 75 (Page 5). [27] His stealth allowed him to evade the senses of both Tanjiro and Inosuke, both of which possessing a heightened sense of smell and touch respectively. Later, Tengen was able to sneak up on and decapitate Daki, an Upper Rank demon, without her noticing. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 84 (Page 19). [19]
  • Poison Resistance: As a former shinobi, Tengen has been shown to possess high resistance to poison to the point where he could continue fighting while under the effects of a poison from Gyutaro that would have killed someone instantly under normal circumstances. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 87 (Page 6). [25]


Tactical Intellect: Due to accumulating close to two decades of experience as both a shinobi and Demon Slayer, Tengen has incredible judgement, making decisions on the fly while in battle with Upper Rank demons to protect bystanders and minimize the damage dealt to the surroundings. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 86 (Page 10). [20] Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 86 (Page 16). [28] Even when he was held back by Gyutaro's poison, Tengen was able to properly determine the most efficient strategy to fight off the Upper Rank demons and even fooled Gyutaro into thinking he was dead by stopping his own heart to prevent himself from dying from his poison, which managed to give Tengen an edge against him as he then completed his Musical Score Technique. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 93 (Page 15). [29]


Demon Slayer Abilities

Muscle Control: Tengen can control the physiology of his muscles to a great extent. During his battle with Gyutaro, Tengen used his muscles to stop his heart and temporarily prevent the poison from circulating, which also allowed him to get the drop on Gyutaro, who did not pay him heed until he unexpectedly attacked the demon. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 93 (Pages 15-19). [30]


Physical Abilities

Immense Speed & Reflexes: Tengen is said to be the fastest runner out of all the Hashira, Kimetsu no Yaiba Second Fanbook. [3] and possesses tremendous levels of raw speed and reflexes, often shown when he moves so fast he appears to appear and disappear without a trace. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 80 (Page 6). [31] His speed also far surpasses that of Tanjiro, Zenitsu and Inosuke, who were unable to keep up with, let alone perceive, the Hashira's blisteringly quick movements. Most notably, Tengen was fast enough to behead Daki, an Upper Rank demon, before she could even register what had happened. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 84 (Page 19). [19] Moreover, he could fend off both Daki and the true Upper Rank Six, Gyutaro, despite being held back by a deadly poison that would've killed someone quickly under normal circumstances.


Even after relinquishing his position as the Sound Hashira, Tengen's tremendous speed remained apparent. During a training exercise where he played the role of a demon stalking the trainees, his speed and reflexes gave him a handy advantage as he downed the Demon Slayers one by one without any one of them even able to see him; only Tanjiro, who has previous experience with fighting Upper Ranks, could see him moving and read his attacks to retaliate, leading to a short skirmish between them. Kimetsu no Yaiba Anime: Episode 58. [16]


Immense Stamina & Endurance: Due to his upbringing being raised to become a shinobi and his own father's increased attempts to revive their clan by forcing Tengen and his siblings to train even more harshly and painfully then necessary, Tengen developed inhuman resistance to pain, as seen where he bared little to no reaction at having being caught up in a hail of Kunai knives and being pierced by them without even flinching or showing any form of discomfort or pain, continuing to attack Gyutaro as if nothing had happened. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 89 (Page 17). [21] Later, Tengen was also noted to have suffered other severe injuries during the battle, such as getting his eye and hand slashed off by Gyutaro, Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 93 (Page 17). [32] Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 92 (Page 6). [33] and managed to ignore the pain of such injuries as well. Even the Kakushi were shocked at Tengen's inhuman levels of endurance after the battle against Upper Six, when he was seen standing upright, and walking out of the rubble with minimal help from his wives.


Immense Strength: Tengen possesses tremendous physical strength even amongst the Hashira, the strongest Demon Slayers of the Corps. He was ranked second in an arm-wrestling contest among all the Hashira, with only the hulking Gyomei Himejima surpassing him. Tengen displayed his strength numerous times, as he was shown effortlessly carrying Aoi and Naho, Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga and Anime: Chapter 70 (Page 4) and Episode 34. [34] and soon after knocked Zenitsu and Inosuke unconscious with a single punch to the stomach. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 71 (Pages 11-13). [35] Moreover, Tengen is able to fight at blistering speeds while carrying around his giant Nichirin cleavers, swinging them incessantly to fend off his opponents without much trouble and being able to hold both his enormous cleavers at their tip with only two fingers. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 86 (Pages 16-17). [36] Furthermore, Tengen was able to match Gyutaro in strength with only one arm after his left hand was cut off, and while he was still poisoned. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 93 (Pages 14-19). [37]


In his role in overseeing the first stage of Hashira Training, Tengen once stopped a trainee from moving by just holding his wooden blade outstretched, despite the Demon Slayer attempting to budge with all his might. He was also able to initiate an intense spar with Tanjiro, who had undergone considerable developement between his mission in Yoshiwara and Hashira Training. Kimetsu no Yaiba Anime: Episode 58. [16]


Supernatural Abilities

Enhanced Hearing: Tengen has a heightened sense of hearing similar to Zenitsu Agatsuma. This ability allows him to easily hear noises deep underground, and he uses it to immediately discern that there are channels of underground tunnels and caves directly beneath him when he was looking for Tanjiro Kamado and Daki. He also uses his superhuman hearing to monitor all the activity in Yoshiwara in an attempt to find the location of the demons dwelling in the area. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga and Anime: Chapter 72 (Page 14) and Episode 35. [38] Tengen also used his hearing to discern whether people are still present on a floor in a building. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 86 (Page 16). [28]

  • Echolocation: As an advanced application of his hearing, Tengen also possesses echolocation. When trying to find the location of a battle underground, he analyzed the sounds reflected and resonated through the air by the walls of the caverns, and accurately locates and is even able to guess the number of people inside the tunnels by listening to the sound bouncing from their physical bodies. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 77 (Page 6). [39]
  • Musical Score (譜ふ面めん Fumen): Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 93 (Pages 16-17). [22] A technique Tengen developed using his heightened sense of hearing and battle analysis to work in conjunction with his Sound Breathing, where he analyzes combat maneuvers and deconstructs the rhythm of his enemies' attacks into sound, reading them like sheet music to the point where every fight becomes like a song. This allows Tengen to gain an understanding of his opponent's habits and tactics, as well as their blind spots, giving him the edge against them. While it takes a long time for Tengen to construct the score, once completed, Tengen was able to fight Gyutaro on even terms despite having lost his left hand, but due to the poison he was pushed to the limit and was unable to strike at the Upper Rank's neck. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 93 (Pages 16-17). [22]


Fighting Style

Master Swordsman: As a Hashira of the Demon Slayer Corps, Tengen is one of the most powerful and skilled swordsman in the entire organization. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 45. [40] Unlike most Demon Slayers, Tengen chooses to utilize a dual-wielding swordsmanship style with his Nichirin Cleavers, making use of a nunchucks-esque sword wielding style. Tengen displays his unique and extraordinary swordsmanship skills during his battle with Daki and Gyutaro, being able to adapt to the former's incessant obi slashes and the latter's "mantis-like" fighting style, and go toe-to-toe with them for an extended period of time despite the Upper Rank having over a century of combat experience. Tengen can also use his sword wielding expertise in conjunction with his explosive beads with great synergy, making him all the more effective at fighting demons. He also showed the ability to wield his Nichirin Cleavers with only one arm. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 92 (Page 6). [33] In addition to traditional slashes and swings, Tengen has also been shown to spin his blades by their chain; the whirling motion allowing him to block blows and quickly attack in retaliation, with the blades in constant rapid motion.


Breathing Style

Sound Breathing (音おとの呼こ吸きゅう Honō no kokyū): A Breathing Style that Tengen created himself. Sound Breathing seemingly combines Tengen's heightened sense of hearing, his Musical Score (譜ふ面めん Fumen) technique, his shinobi expertise as well as his dual Nichirin cleavers and his anti-demon bombs.

Both users cut and detonate them, using the explosions to blast themselves down towards their target, creating another powerful explosion when they land.
  • Art is an Explosion! (芸げい術じゅつは爆ばく発はつだ！ Geijutsu wa Bakuhatsu da!) Kimetsu no Yaiba Game: The Hinokami Chronicles 2. [44] A technique exclusive to the Kimetsu Academy version of Tengen Uzui. The school's art teacher orders his opponent to strike a flashy pose so he could paint a portrait of them. However, if Tengen is unimpressed by their attempts, he gets angered and hurls a lit stick of dynamite at them while insulting their lack of panache, becoming much more satisfied after the explosion. Alternatively, Tengen will be just as angered if the opponent does comply with his request and wordlessly stands still, resulting in the same assault.

Equipment

Dual Dual Nichirin Cleavers: Befitting Tengen's flamboyant personality, he carries an equally flamboyant set of weaponry, wielding two gigantic Nichirin cleavers, with the blades being black with smooth wavy, amber outline. The blades each have a notch in the cutting edge and are held together by a metal chain, Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 77 (Page 8). [46] making it very reminiscent to nunchucks. The weight of his blades enables them to generate immense explosive force, as seen when they create a blast upon making contact with Gyutaro's blood blades or when Tengen struck them on the ground to propel himself. It is stated that, until Tengen's fight with Upper Rank Six, no demon had ever survived a blow from them. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 77 (Page 8). [47] The cleavers' tsubas are round with four slight indentations. They are gold in color and decorated with a maroon ring that connects to the center; it has an outer green border. The handles are dark green, wrapped with red cord and capped with an engraved gold kashira; this is also where the chains connect to the cleavers. The blades are engraved with the phrase Destroyer of Demons (惡あっ鬼き滅めっ殺さつ Akki Messatsu). Due to his cleavers' large size, Tengen doesn't use a sword sheath and instead carries them on his back, having them wrapped in cloth.


During the climax of the battle between the Upper Rank Six duo, the chains of his cleavers were broken in pieces.


Explosive Beads: Tengen carries dozens of small black beads that create large explosions and detonate with the slightest amount of friction, capable of disorienting and inflicting damage onto demons of Upper Rank class. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 86 (Page 16). [28] Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 87 (Page 15). [48]


Kunai: Like his three wives, Tengen carries around dozens of standard black kunai, which he used to threaten Omitsu's husband to reveal information about Zenitsu's and Hinatsuru's disappearance and to destroy the Obi sash that captured Hinatsuru. Kimetsu no Yaiba Anime: Episode 38. [49]

Trivia

  • Tengen's clan name contains the Man'yōgana for the kana for "U" (宇う) with the Kan'yō-on'yomi reading of the kanji for "marrow, pith" (髄ずい zui). His given name contains the Kan-on'yomi reading for both "heaven" (天てん ten) and "beginning, origin" (元げん gen). The full term "Tengen" can mean the central black dot on a "Go board" (圍い碁ご盤ばん Igoban).
  • Tengen's rankings in the popularity polls are as follows:
    • Tengen ranked 37th in the first popularity poll with 14 votes.
    • Tengen ranked 13th in the second popularity poll with 3,436 votes.
  • In his initial design, Tengen: Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Volume 11, (Extra Pages). [50]
    • Was less muscular, with a much thinner build.
    • Had half of his face covered in bandages, making it hard for others to see what he looks like.
    • Wore sleeves due to him "not wanting his arms sunburned", according to the author.
    • Has bachelor-ish vibe.
  • Tengen is one of the only known Demon Slayer and Hashira to retire due to being permanently disabled instead of death or age, the other being Jigoro Kuwajima.
  • He and Inosuke Hashibira are the only known Demon Slayers who use two Nichirin Swords instead of one.
  • In a Weekly Shonen Jump spoof 4-koma, Tengen plays the harmonica in the band "Chic-N-Scruffy Democracy", the other members being Zenitsu (shamisen), Inosuke (taiko), and Tanjiro (vocals). "Melancholy of Explosive Sound Skin and Modern Sense of Smell" is their debut single, and it reportedly made everyone who heard it feel dizzy and nauseous. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Volume 11, (Extra Pages). [50]
  • Tengen's family practices polygamy, so when someone turns 15, they must have 3 wives. The clan head is the one who chooses the wives, but they always try to take chemistry into consideration when picking. Kimetsu no Yaiba First Fanbook [51]
  • Tengen had eight siblings, making 9 children in total. Three of his siblings died due to their father's overzealous training. The remaining six was then placed on death battles against each other while being masked. Tengen only realized this fact after killing two of his brothers, which enraged him and caused him to have an argument about his father's foolish ideals. Tengen's remaining brother then killed two more of their siblings, leaving him and Tengen as the survivors. Tengen turned his blade away and left the clan with his wives. Kimetsu no Yaiba Second Fanbook. [3]
    • For some time, it became Tengen's habit to say that he was going to hell. This stopped after seeing his wives' reaction: with Makio getting angry with him, Hinatsuru crying for him, and Suma biting him. Kimetsu no Yaiba Second Fanbook. [3]
    • He would occasionally think of exterminating his entire clan, but he couldn't bring himself to kill his father and brother. Kimetsu no Yaiba Second Fanbook. [3]
