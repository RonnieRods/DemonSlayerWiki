Quick Answers

Why did Tengen Uzui initially want to kill Tanjiro?
Tengen initially wanted to kill Tanjiro because his actions of being a Demon Slayer and caring for a demon was against their rules. Tengen goes as far as to even request to be the one to behead him. It was only because of Kagaya Ubuyashiki's sanction of Tanjiro that he does not.
What was Tengen's plan with Aoi in Demon Slayer?
Tengen Uzui intended to recruit Aoi Kanzaki and Naho Takada for his mission in the Yoshiwara red-light district, requiring the assistance of female Demon Slayers. This plan was thwarted by Kanao Tsuyuri, Sumi Nakahara, Kiyo Terauchi and Tanjiro Kamado. Subsequently, Tanjiro, Zenitsu Agatsuma and Inosuke Hashibira volunteered to join Tengen's mission instead.
How does Tengen Uzui's relationship with Tanjiro evolve throughout the series?
Upon their initial meeting during Tanjiro's Hashira trial, Tengen Uzui is hostile due to Tanjiro's association with a demon. However, their relationship evolves from being strangers with differing views to allies in battle when Tanjiro joins Tengen's mission in Yoshiwara to save his wives. After the battle, Tengen comes to respect Tanjiro and sees him as both an equal and an eventual successor to his position as Hashira.

Relationships

Tanjiro Kamado

Tengen first meets Tanjiro at his trial among the other Hashira. Upon hearing that he's been travelling with a Demon, Tengen initially vouches to kill him since he has disobeyed the rules and also rejects Kagaya's blessing to allow Nezuko and Tanjiro to travel together. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 46 (Page 7). [1]


When they meet again at the Butterfly Mansion, Tanjiro becomes angry and confronts Tengen after witnessing his attempt to kidnap Aoi and Naho. During the intense exchange, Tengen becomes fed up with Tanjiro's attitude and explodes at him for disrespecting him despite being his superior. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 70 (Pages 12-13). [2] However, the exchange eventually cools down when the boys offer to accompany Tengen on his mission instead of the girls.


As they formally begin their mission, Tanjiro's naïvety pleases Tengen, seemingly melting away any tension between them. When Tanjiro struggles to restrain Nezuko following her demonization, Tengen scolds him for his lack of control over his sister given how he testified for Nezuko's innocence in front of the Master. However, rather than choosing to kill her, Tengen instead advises Tanjiro to put her to sleep, showcasing his respect for Tanjiro's wishes. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 85 (Pages 2-3). [3]


As the two work together to take down Gyutaro, Tanjiro becomes concerned over Tengen's deteriorating health due to the poison and constantly tries to protect him from oncoming attacks. Even during situations when Tanjiro is risking his life, Tengen consistently manages to throw Tanjiro out of the way, or protect him before he can be seriously wounded. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 88 (Pages 14-15). [4] Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 91 (Page 8). [5] Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 93 (Page 14). [6]


At the end of the battle, Tengen turns around to warn Tanjiro of Gyutaro's oncoming attack instead of fleeing showing his concern for him. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 94 (Page 18). [7] Despite having horrible wounds himself, Tengen still shows his concern for Tanjiro's well being as he tells him to stay still, if not he would die from his injuries. Following the completion of their mission, Tengen finally acknowledges Tanjiro's abilities by telling the Serpent Hashira, Obanai Iguro, that he "has grown to become strong enough" to fill his vacancy as he retires. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 95 (Page 15). [8] Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 97 (Page 13). [9]


Nezuko Kamado

Initially, Tengen didn't accept the Kamado siblings because Tanjiro Kamado was keeping his sister who was turned into a demon alive. Hearing that he had been traveling with a demon, Tengen vouched to kill both of them for breaking the corps' rules. It is implied that Tengen was at least neutral with Nezuko living after she was tested to see if she'd attack Sanemi after he provoked her but didn't, considering he overlooked her when he went to the Entertainment District with Tanjiro, Zenitsu and Inosuke. He also viewed her not attacking Sanemi as "showing off" in front of the master and was also displeased when she transformed into an advanced demonic state and began to lsoe control of herself, claiming she had no right nor reason to demonize so flashily. What changed Tengen's mind about Nezuko is when he was dying from Gyutaro's poison, Nezuko used her Blood Demon Art to completely burn away the poison from his entire body which at first, confused Tengen thinking if it was even possible. He eventually retires from being a Hashira due to being rendered incapable of fighting.


Zenitsu Agatsuma


When the two originally meet, Zenitsu develops a disdain for Tengen after he attempted to kidnap the girls of the Butterfly Mansion, and due to his flamboyant personality, even referring to him as "god of idiots". Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 71 (Page 4). [10] Tengen's frivolous attitude doesn't sit well with Zenitsu's character and often annoys him. When Tengen tells the boys of his plan to find his wives, Zenitsu becomes enraged at his womanizing antics and mocks him for claiming to have three wives, which results in him getting knocked unconscious by Tengen. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 71 (Page 10). [11]


Tengen's appearance also causes Zenitsu to seethe with jealousy as he appears to draw more attention from the women within the Entertainment District. Zenitsu's dislike for Tengen reaches its peak when he abandons Zenitsu at the Kyogoku House and tells the manager to have him clean the brothel, causing Zenitsu to seethe with rage as he is left to complete his mission. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 72 (Page 4). [12]


Inosuke Hashibira

Tengen initially finds Inosuke to be quite disturbing due to the boar's head he wears. When Tengen introduces himself to the trio of boys, Inosuke uses the opportunity to introduce himself as the "god of the mountain", however his introduction does not appeal to Tengen and calls Inosuke "creepy." Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 71 (Page 4). [10] Inosuke's blunt personality also doesn't sit well with Tengen as seen when he punched Inosuke for carelessly questioning if Tengen's wives are even alive. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 71 (Page 12). [13]


Inosuke doesn't necessarily speak to Tengen with respect as seen when he blamed him for being reckless and allowing the obi to escape from the underground cavern. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 80 (Page 5). [14] However, Inosuke does acknowledge Tengen's advanced abilities and skills to the point where he mimics Tengen's catchphrases. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 75 (Page 5). [15] Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 86 (Page 9). [16]


Obanai Iguro

Despite Tengen's victory against Upper Rank 6, Obanai downplays this significant achievement to instead emphasize the extent of his injuries. Tengen, for his part, brushes aside Obanai's concerns, insisting on retiring and disagreeing with the latter's sentiment that the Demon Slayers were going into decline.


Kyojuro Rengoku

Tengen deeply admired and respected Kyojuro during their time as fellow Hashira, going so far as to say the Flame Hashira was even flashier than he was. During his battle against Daki and Gyutaro, he denies being talented and laments how many lives he's failed to save, acknowledging that Kyojuro was always ahead of him. Later, while wondering if he could handle the battle as Kyojuro would, the Flame Hashira appears before him admitting that he could never be like Tengen either, and asks him to watch over Tanjiro for him.


Makio, Suma and Hinatsuru

Tengen holds a large amount of respect for his wives and cares about them deeply. When Tengen joined the Demon Slayer Corps, he made sure to inform his wives that they needed to priorize their lives instead of the mission, and make sure to return home to him. Tengen wanted to make clear that his wives were his top priority in regards to keeping people safe. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 80 (Pages 1-3). [17]


During their stay in the Entertainment District, Tengen would often communicate with them via letters and decided to search for them once he stopped receiving them. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 71 (Page 10). [11] When Inosuke questions if his wives are even alive anymore, Tengen becomes enraged and punches him unconscious, showing that he chastises any disrespect towards his wives. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 71 (Page 12). [13]


Later on when Tengen discovers Hinatsuru in the Kirimise, he administers medication to her and asks if she's alright. When she starts to apologize to him for failing the mission, Tengen reassures her that she did well and embraces her tightly before instructing her to leave the district for her safety. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 77 (Page 4). [18] However, when Hinatsuru returns to assist in the fight against Gyutaro, she gets captured by the Demon causing Tengen to go into a panic to try and save her. Tanjiro manages to save Hinatsuru in time, allowing Tengen to express his gratitude towards him and mentioning that he "owes him one" for saving his wife. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 90 (Page 19). [19]


When Tengen discovers the underground cavern, he slashes the obi within the vicinity of his wives and apologizes to Suma and Makio for being late. He approaches the women and pats them on the head, reassuring them that they've lived up to his expectations as his wives, causing the two of them to tear up upon reuniting with him. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 80 (Page 4). [20]


Following the events of the final battle, Suma, Makio and Hinatsuru immediately rush in to administer first aid to Tengen, but his wounds appear to be too grave for them to treat, causing them to worry. They each become concerned for his well being as Suma begins to wail, Makio begins to panic and Hinatsuru begins to weep at the sight of his injuries. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 95 (Page 10). [21] However, when Nezuko manages to heal Tengen from being poisoned, the women rejoice at the fact that he's been healed, and wail as they share a relieved hug with him. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 95 (Page 14). [22]
