Kagaya Ubuyashiki (産うぶ屋や敷しき 耀かが哉や Ubuyashiki Kagaya) was a major supporting character of Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba. He was the 97th leader of the Demon Slayer Corps Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 47 (Page 19). [2] known mainly as Oyakata-sama (お館󠄃やかた樣さま lit. "Master") by his subordinates and peers. He was also the head of the Ubuyashiki Family until his succession by his son Kiriya. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 45 (Page 21). [3] Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 46 (Page 5). [4]

Quick Answers

What is the signifiance of Kagaya Ubuyashiki's illness?
The illness on Kagaya Ubuyashiki, the leader of the Hashiras and head of the Demon Slayer Corps, is due to a family curse linked to Muzan Kibutsuji being born in their lineage. This curse weakens the Ubuyashiki offspring and drastically shortens their lifespan.
Who is the leader of the Hashiras in Demon Slayer?
As the head of the Demon Slayer Corps, Kagaya Ubuyashiki is also the leader of the Hashiras. The Hashiras, known for their exceptional strength, remain second in command to him. After Kagaya's death, his son Kiriya Ubuyashiki assumes leadership of the Corps.
What is the relationship between Kagaya and the Ubuyashiki Family?
Kagaya is the 97th leader and head of the Ubuyashiki Family.

Appearance

Kagaya was a man of frail physicality, average stature, and very pale skin complexion. He had shoulder-length black hair that was parted in the middle. Due to his family curse, Kagaya looked as if he was rotting alive, with large portions of his skin appearing with a sickly, shriveled pink hue that began from the left side of his forehead and gradually spread across his body. Additionally, the curse caused him to go blind as it affected his eyes, turned his sclera from white to black (light purple in the anime) and his pupils from a dark purple to a light purple, matching his cursed skin. When he was first formally introduced in Tanjiro Kamado's trial, Kagaya's curse coverd the upper half of his head, but during the last moments of his life only months later, the curse has spread to his entire body, leaving him covered completely with bandages apart from his mouth to speak.


Muzan described him as physically repulsive and pitiful, as well as having the scent of a corpse. Additionally, during their meeting, Kagaya was nearly completely bedridden, having been confined to his household since the battle in Yoshiwara: even the act of getting up caused him great pain and he coughed blood trying to do so. He was also prone to bleeding from his mouth and eyes. Throughout all his appearances, Kagaya sported a gentle and relaxed smile that never wavered, always appearing serene and calm no matter the situation.


While serving as Oyakata, he primarily wore a black yukata, with a white undershirt and tied with a weaved brown obi. Atop this, he wore a white kimono with a flame-like pattern appearing from the bottom and colored with a blend of light-purple and orange. He wore a pair of plain white socks, and a pair of sandals with brown straps. When meeting Muzan during the last moments of his life, Kagaya wore a wavy-striped kimono with a blend of light-puple and grey.

Personality

Kagaya had a calm, dedicated and composed demeanor, showing genuine care about the members of the Demon Slayer Corps, whom he considered as his children. He was also able to handle criticism well and was honest about his weaknesses; one being his inability to become a Demon Slayer despite being their main leader. In order to make up for this, he remembered every single member of the Corps and visited the deceased members at their cemetery on a daily basis until he could no longer move due to his illness.


While he may seem lenient, he only gives due consideration to reason and when a situation was clear of any biases, as shown when he willingly accepted the situation of Tanjiro and Nezuko through a letter from Sakonji Urokodaki, even defending their cause when most of the Hashira respectfully disagreed with his pardoning. His manner of speaking, while generally fluid and gentle, was extremely tactful and deliberate, using words and intonations in a way that the person he talked to would listen and wanted to listen to. His insightful and charismatic speech was even able to make the likes of the initially passive Muichiro Tokito and the impulsive Sanemi Shinazugawa listen to what he had to say without conveying anything that would rile them up.


Towards his own kin, namely his children, Kagaya was rather strict. Due to the curse of their family, he had to prepare them early on to replace him in leading the Demon Slayers. It is also worth noting that Kagaya himself took the position as Oyakata following the suicide of his father at the mere age of four, and he was further plagued by the double-suicide of his siblings and their mother's accidental death in the process. Nevertheless, he shows the same gentle aura and demeanor towards them as he does to everyone.


Beneath his otehrwise kind and gentle personality, Kagaya bore a deep, burning hatred of Muzan Kibutsuji. As the cause for his family's cursed lives, he showed uncharacteristic silent anger towards the demon and addressed him without showing any indication of fear or hesistation. He repeatedly provoked him by mocking his intentions to become an eternal being and boldly stated that he would die, and in his final moments, he bore a smile on his face to lure Muzan into a false sense of security as his trap was activated. Despite his own hatred towards him, Muzan was amazed that Kagaya had hidden his bloodlust during their conversation and was shocked Kagaya was willing to kill his own family from his own perspective, having underestimated the lengths he would go to in order to weaken him.


Abilities

Overall Abilities

Emotion Masking: Kagaya possessed the remarkable ability to concel his emotions and present an composed and controlled outward appearance. He utilized this skill to maintain a calm and serene composure and deceive others' perception of him. When Muzan, the target of Kagaya's deep-seated hatred, confronted him at his home, Kagaya presented himself with the serene smile of a Buddha, effectively masking any trace of murderous intent. By completely erasing his fighting spirit, animosity and hatred, Kagaya successfully concealed his true intentions, luring Muzan into a trap. It was only when Kagaya sacrificed himself by detonating an explosive to ensnare Muzan that the demon realized the magnitude of Kagaya's hidden emotinos; he likened Kagaya's concealed violent intent to a coiled viper lurking within his dark, lifeless stomach, leaving him in awe Kagaya's astonishing ability to veil his true nature. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 138 (Page 10). [5]


Soothing Voice: Kagaya possessed the ability to put others at ease just by the tone of his voice and rhythm of his actions, as he displayed with Tanjiro Kamado andn even the angered Hashira. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 47 (Page 15). [6] In modern times, this attribute is called 1/F Fluctuation, and is said to be possessed by many charismatic people and have the strength of presence to move mass audiences. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 47 (Page 15). [6]


Supernatural Abilities

Extrasensory Perception: Kagaya possesses the ability to perceive the presence of both humans and demons, as well as determine their strength. This became evident during the Hashira meeting held after the events at Mount Natagumo. Despite becoming blind in both eyes due to the progression of his illness, Kagaya immediately sensed the presence of the number of individuals with Hashira-level power upon arrival and expressed his satisfaction that they all made it to the meeting without personnel changes. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga and Anime: Chapter 46 and Episode 22. [7] Furthermore, when Muzan arrived at the Ubuyashiki Estate with the intention to kill him, Kagaya immediately detected Muzan's presence and greeted him, even when his eyes were completely covered by bandages and Muzan was still standing at a distance outside of the estate, showcasing his extraordinary sensory abilities. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 136. [8]


Foresight: Kagaya, as well as the rest of the Ubuyashiki family, possess a heightened level of foresight that helps them avoid disastrous situations and amassed them a great amount of wealth and fortune. Due to this instinct, Kagaya accurately made several guesses about multiple things that were eventually proven to be true and accurate without him knowing any prior information, as seen when he accurately predicted that Muzan's only remaining weakness was the sun and that he could not be destroyed by decapitation, Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 139. [9] that the deaths of Gyutaro and Daki would send waves that would eventually see that the Upper Ranks of the Twelve Kizuki begin losing members for the first time in hundreds of years, and that Muzan will find him and bring about the final wat between the Demon Slayer Corps and the Twelve Kizuki during his last five days of life. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 138. [10]


Trivia

  • Kagaya shares his Japanese and English voice actors with Seijiro Kikuoka from Sword Art Online.
  • As stated by Kagaya, the disease currently ailing him is the curse inflicted on his family due to them birthing the "original demon". The sickness itself is a genetic disorder passed down from generation to generation in their family, it has effectively left the Ubuyashiki family with weak bodies and a lifespan of less than 30 years. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 137 (Pages 6-7). [11]
  • Kagaya's first name contains the kanji for "shine" (耀かが kaga) and the Kun'yomi for "how" (哉や ya); the "ya" in the first character is not included and is instead replaced by the second. His surname contains the Kun'yomi for "bear, give birth to" (産うぶ ubu), "house, shop" (屋や ya) and "spread" (敷しき shiki).
  • Kagaya's father committed suicide when he was 19, unable to bear the thought of having his Corps members die over and over again. Kagaya's siblings also died, leaving him to inherit the position of family head at the age of 4 years old. Kimetsu no Yaiba Second Fanbook. [12]
    • Resentful of his shortened life and to spitefully curse him, Kagaya's younger brother Senri set fire to their home in an act of suicide, forcing their youngest brother Akito to join him. Their mother fell into the flames in a failed attempt to save them. Kimetsu no Yaiba Gotoge Koyoharu exhibition 2021 (via Crunchyroll ) [13]
  • All boys born into the Ubuyashiki Clan will die without surpassing the age of 30. Girls born from the clan must be married off and change surnames by the age of 13, otherwise they will also inevitably die from accidents or illnesses. Kimetsu no Yaiba Second Fanbook. [12]
    • Following Muzan's death, the curse was lifted and the Ubuyashiki Clan enjoyed normal, even prolonged lifespans. His son Kiriya eventually became the oldest living person in Japan by the end.
