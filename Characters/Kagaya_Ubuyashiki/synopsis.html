History

Kagaya was born into the Ubuyashiki clan. As a male born into the family, he was cursed to suffer an illness that slowly afflicted his entire body, due to Muzan Kibutsuji's blood relation with the clan. His father, the previous family head, was a sensitive man who committed suicide at the age of 19 because he could not bear the thought of his troops getting hurt or dying; Kagaya was four when this happened. The children he had were all boys, and Kagaya assumed the role of Oyakata afger his father's death. Kimetsu no Yaiba Second Fanbook. [1] The middle child, Senri, resented dressing like a girl and cursed his family. One day, Senri set fire to the mansion to commit suicide and forced their youngest brother, Akito, to join him. Their mother would try to rescue them but she fell into the flames.


When he was 13 years old, he first met his wife Amane. She was specifically selected by a priest to become Kagaya's wife in order to care for his medical needs, but he only agreed to the marriage on the condition that she cared for him on her own free will. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Volume 16, (Extra Pages). [2]


When Kagaya was 14, he learned of a blind monk who was imprisoned for the deaths of eight children under his care. Having realized that the monk was innocent and that the deaths were the result of a demon attack, he intervened and prevented his execution. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 135 (Page 20). [3] This man, Gyomei Himejima, would later become a Demon Slayer and go on to become one of Kagaya's most trusted confidants and the Stone Hashira.


Kagaya fathered quintuplets, four daughters, and one son, and Amane gave birth to them when he was 15. From eldest to youngest, they were named Hinaki, Nichika, Kiriya, Kuina and Kanata.


Sometime later, Kagaya met with a shinobi turned Demon Slayer named Tengen Uzui, Tengen had left his past behind, unwilling to become a ruthless killer like his father and brother, Kagaya praised Tengen for his decision, citing the great difficulty the young man must have endured to leave everything behind and accept a new direction in life. Touched by his words, Tengen expressed a gratitude and became intensely loyal to Kagaya, later becoming the Sound Hashira. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 87 (Page 6). [4] Sometime before or afterwards, Kagaya met a quiet boy with severe imposter syndrome named Giyu Tomioka, and a young girl who became a Demon Slayer after being saved by Gyomei named Kanae Kocho. They would become the Water and Flower Hashira respectively.


During his first meeting as a member, Wind Hashira Sanemi Shinazugawa harshly insulted Kagaya for sending Demon Slayers under his command to die with an unconcerned smile on his face, angered Gyomei and Tengen, and worrying Kanae. Despite their concerns, Kagaya allowed the Hashira to continue speaking his mind. When Sanemi accused him of being a weak leader to the Demon Slayers, Kagaya apologized to him, shocking the angered young man. He explains that due to his condition, he cannot even swing a blade ten times without his blood pressure becoming dangerous, and if he wanted to become strong, he would but cannot. He apologizes to Sanemi for making him do what is diffucult, the once furious Hashira calmed by his voice and gentle expression.


Kagaya would go on to tell Sanemi that if he believed the Demon Slayers were pawns to him, that he too was one as well since his replacement is already set. Additionally, he explains that he is not above anyone; the Demon Slayers simply treat him with respect but he does not have to. He apologizes for summoning him soon after the death of his partner Masachika Kumeno. In disbelief in hearing his name, Sanemi would be told by Kanae that Kagaya memorizes the names of every single Demon Slayer under his command. The Corps leader gives him Masachika's will to read, causing him to tear up. Since seeing his genuine dedication to the Demon Slayer Corps, Sanemi has also become extremely loyal to Kagaya. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 168 (Pages 8-16). [5]


Sometime afterwards, Kanae was killed by Upper Rank Two Doma, and her younger sister Shinobu Kocho became the Insect Hashira. Later on, Kagaya summoned the Hashira for a meeting, but the depressed current Flame Hashira, Shinjuro Rengoku refused to attend, with his elder son Kyojuro Tengoku coming in his place. After asking about their well-being, Kagaya asks the Hashira to support each other on missions in response to their dwindling numbers. After Sanemi rudely questions Kyojuro's presence, Kagaya chides him and states that he invited Kyojuro so he could explain Shinjuro's condition. Later on, Sanemi attacks Kyojuro after he explains his goal to become the Flame Hashira, but after he expresses his refusal to hit back, Kagaya calms Sanemi by addressing him, and presents Kyojuro with the opportunity of facing off against a Twleve Kizuki Hairo. After Shinobu raises the concern that a Hashira should fight a member of the Twleve Kizuki, Kagaya explains that he needs them to defend the territory covered by missing Hashira and that the demon was located in Shinjuro's former territory. After Kyojuro returned home victorious, Kagaya praised him and offered him the position of Flame Hashira, to which he accepted. Kimetsu no Yaiba Extra: Kyojuro Rengoku's Story: Part 1. [6] Kimetsu no Yaiba Extra: Kyojuro Rengoku's Story: Part 2. [7]


Kagaya encountered the pupil of Kyojuro, a young Demon Slayer who was ostracized for her abnormal strength and unusual traits. Taking pity on her, the Demon Slayer told her that her strength was a beloved gift from the gods and anyone who spoke ill of her was merely envious. The girl, Mitsuri Kanroji, was deeply touched by Kagaya's words, crying and bowing before him in relief and thankfulness. She would later go on to become the Love Hashira of the Demon Slayer Corps. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 124 (Page 1). [8]


During Muichiro Tokito's career as a Demon Slayer, he was visited by Kagaya and Amane during his recovery. Seeing the confused and heavily wounded boy, Kagaya extended words of advice to him, telling Muichiro that one's survival is foremost and that once he survives, lost memories will come back. At a later time, he then asked him not to be so rigid with his perception of the world, telling the boy if he can find his true self, he can become unstoppable. Kimetsu no Yaiba Anime: Episode 53. [9] Muichiro would later become Kagaya's Mist Hashira. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 108 (Page 5). [10]


Before, after, or between the meeting with Muichiro and Mitsuri, Kagaya also met Obanai Iguro, a scarred Demon Slayer with deep emotional and psychological trauma from upbringing. Obanai became the Serpent Hashira, and although he was unfriendly and rude, Obanai became just as loyal to Kagaya as the other Hashira.


Synopsis

Final Selection Arc

Following Final Selection, Kagaya is informed that there were a total of five survivors. Satsified that the number of his "children" has increased, he wonders what kind of Demon Slayers they would eventually become.


Mount Natagumo Arc

During the mission to Mount Natagumo, a Kasugai crow flies from the mountain to the Demon Slayer Corps headquarters to inform Kagaya of the massacre that has occured. Believing the Twelve Kizuki are involved and the deployment of the Hashira to be necessary to rescue the survivors, he sends the Water Hashira Giyu Tomioka and Insect Hashira Shinobu Kocho to intervene.


Rehabilitation Training Arc

During Tanjiro Kamado's trial by the Hashira for harbouring his demon sister Nezuko, Kagaya appears for their semi-annual meeting and comments on the lovely weather. The Hashira bow before him and Sanemi extends greetings to Kagaya. The Wind Hashira then questions him on the circumstances regarding Tanjiro but to the Hashira's surprise, Kagaya has accepted Tanjiro's situation and wishes for them to accept it as well. Gyomei, Tengen, Sanemi, Flame Hashira Kyojuro Rengoku and Serpent Hashira Obanai Iguro disagree due to the nature of demons but Muichiro and Love Hashira Mitsuri Kanroji accept it, while Shinobu and Giyu stay silent. With his answers, Kagaya informs them about a letter from Sakonji Urokodaki, the former Water Hashira, writing that he believes Nezuko to be fully able to resist attacking humans and that he, Giyu and Tanjiro would commit seppuku should it ever happen.


Kyojuro and Sanemi still disagree and claim that it cannot be proven Nezuko won't attack but Kagaya points out that it is also unsure if she would, and citing the fact that 3 people are putting their lives on the line, he asks them to provide a stronger argument. Additionally, he adds that Tanjiro has met Muzan Kibutsuji in the past and has had demons sent to kill him. Though the Hashira realize how important he is, Sanemi attempts to prove Nezuko's demonic nature by stabbing her and taunting the demon with his own blood. He apologizes to Kagaya for his rude intrusion and enters the headquarters to open her box. When Nezuko proves herself capable of resisting his blood, Kagaya, being blind, questions what has happened, to which he is informed that Nezuko turned away from Sanemi.


Believing this to be indisputable proof, Kagaya is pleased and addresses Tanjiro, who notes the floating feeling he experiences hearing his voice. Kagaya informs him that there will be those who would not accept his siter and tells him to prove his worth by killing a Twelve Kizuki. Tanjiro, feeling elated by his acceptance, vows to kill Muzan, though the Master humorously implores him to start small with a Kizuki demon. He then addresses Sanemi and Obanai, gently asking them not to torment those younger than themselves, which they acknowledge. With Tanjiro's trial now finished, Shinobu takes custody of him and Nezuko to live in her estate. Kakushi bow before Kagaya and the Hashira and quickly take the siblings away. However, before Kagaya can continue speaking, he is interrupted when Tanjiro runs back and tries to headbutt Sanemi for stabbing Nezuko, asking for Kagaya's permission. He is quickly knocked down by Muichiro flinging pebbles and taken away again, but before the boy leaves his presence, Kagaya asks for Tanjiro to "give Miss Tamayo my regards", surprising him.


Later that night, Kagaya and the Hashira continued their meeting. All were angered by the growing threat of demons on humanity and noted the declining skill in newer Demon Slayers. Gyomei addressed the issue that since only people who know about demons become Demon Slayers, they cannot bloster their numbers easily. When Kyojuro noted that Tanjiro has encountered a Twelve Kizuki early into his career, Kagaya mentions that Lower Five's drastic actions on the mountain would mean that Muzan was nowhere near, due to always creating a distraction. While disappointed in demons becoming more dangerous than ever before, Kagaya expressed his faith in the Hashira, deeming them the strongest since feudal times and looking forward to his "childrens" success. Later that night, while along in his quarters, Kagaya vowed that they will kill Muzan without fail.


Mugen Train Arc

Sometime later, Kagaya, accompanied by Amane, proceeds to head to the burial of the Demon Slayers who were killed, saying each name of the deceased slayers. When Amane requests that he should rest due to his declining health, Kagaya wonders how long he will cease the visits and believes that those who die deserve their justice and he will mean to bring it to them. He states to Amane that no matter how many lives are taken or beaten down, the human heart will prevail and people will always stand up and fight back.


When he learns that not one of the 200 passengers aboard the Mugen Train was killed in the demon's attack, Kagaya praises the fallen Flame Hashira Kyojuro for his sacrifice. He is not saddened by the Hashira's death, as he remarks that he himself does not have long to live for and will be joining him and the other deceased soon.


Entertainment District Arc

Following Tengen's mission in Yoshiwara that resulted in the deaths of Upper Rank demons Daki and Gyutaro, Kagaya, whose health has visibly deteriorated and worsened, is informed of the news by a Kasugai Crow in his estate. He is ecstatic to learn the accomplishment was performed by Tengen, Tanjiro, Nezuko, Zenitsu Agatsuma and Inosuke Hashibira. Amane assists Kagaya; despite his severe coughing now causing him to bleed, he states that an Upper Rank's death has not occurred in over a hundred years and grabs his wife's hand in joyous realization, telling her that the death is an omen for the coming future changing in their favor.


He remarks that the consequences of such a death will spread far and in time, they can kill Muzan once and for all. His coughing suddenly begins to worsen and Amane orders their children to fetch his medicine, as they worriedly call out to their dying father.


Swordsmith Village Arc

After saving a young swordsmith from a fish-like demon, Muichiro initially moves on ahead to protect the Swordsmith Village, having realized that the demon was actually a Blood Demon Art. The smith, Kotetsu, instead begs him to save two swordsmiths who are alone and fending for themselves from the demons. Though at first he ignores him to go his way, Muichiro suddenly remembers a moment when Kagaya and Amane had visited him following an attack by a demon that left him severely wounded.


Kagaya, noticing the boy's confusion, calmly tells him that his survival is paramount. Should he survive, his lost memories will come back too, telling him to not miss the opportunity, as even trivial events can trigger them to come back. Taking his leader's advice, Muichiro forgoes his initial plans and grabs Kotetsu to rescue Kozo and Hotaru. Though he is initially hesistant to aboandon his post, Muichiro trusts in Kagaya's advice and remarks he can succeed as the Mist Hashira.


During his confrontation with Muichiro, Gyokko, after trapping the Mist Hashira in his Water Prison Pot, told him that one of their objectives is to kill Kagaya, along with destroying the Swordsmith Village, in the hopes of crippling the Corps of leadership and weapons. Kimetsu no Yaiba Anime: Episode 49. [11]


At a later time, after killing Gyokko, Muichiro recalls a past meeting he had with Kagaya, who gently asked him to ease his rigid perception of his surroundings. He tells the Mist Hashira that once he finds his true self, he can become unstoppable. True to Kagaya's advice, Muichiro realizes that by focusing his boiling rage on a target without losing himself, he was able to defeat Upper Rank Five. Kimetsu no Yaiba Anime: Episode 53. [9]


While stunned from Zohakuten's Compressed Sound Waves, Mitsuri reminisces on her past and how she gradually realized that acceptance from others of her unique traits would be nearly impossible. Upon joining the Corps however, Kagaya immediately told her that she was special, and that anyone who speaks ill of her was simply afraid of her strength. Hearing whole-hearted acceptance of her true self brings the once uncertain and unconfident young woman to tears, bowing and thanking Kagaya for his words. Kimetsu no Yaiba Anime: Episode 54. [12]


Hashira Training Arc

After some time in the present day at the Ubuyashiki Mansion as the Hashira Training began, the curse of his illness slowly continued to deteriorate across his entire body due to the fact that he would soon die in result of his illness continuing to develop any further. His health visibly worsened much more clearly everyday; relying on his wife, Amane to tend towards his weakened condition by lending aid with covering his face and arms in bandages in hopes of attempting to slow down the effects of Kagaya's curse. She had also gone to the Hashira Meeting in Kagaya's place to inform the remaining Hashira of a demon who has conquered the sun. Kimetsu no Yaiba Anime: Episode 56. [13] She also took in Kagaya's order of writing a letter to Tanjiro, who was still recovering in the Butterfly Mansion from the events that happened at the Swordsmith Village.


Kagaya's letter to Tanjiro sent by his own Kasugai Crow revealed that Kagaya was stuck into a vulnerable state and became ashamed to admit towards any of the other Hashira of his condition debilitating him to the point where he could no longer move in order to confront Giyu himself, and urgently requested of Tanjiro to encourage him of participating in the Hashira Training by visiting his mansion in order to convince him to join training with the other Hashira, preparing to take appropriate measures before the final battle against. Muzan Kibutsuji.


Outside of the walls of the Ubuyashiki Estate at night, Kagaya senses the entrance gates opening for an infiltrator slowly approaching far away in the direction whilst in his deteriorated condition. Amane held his hand and continued to remain in place to comfort him as she glared towards the sudden sounds of footsteps with determination when Kagaya turned his head towards the direction of the garden's bridge despite having bandages over his eyes and decayed skin, hearing the mysterious figure's footsteps proceeding to heavily grow closer. The clear mist then began to disperse from the ominous shadow as he stopped in his tracks at the back entrance of the Estate; revealing the visitor confronting him to be none other than Muzan Kibutsuji himself, standing confidently in front of Kagaya after he welcomes him to his abode. Muzan then remarks Kagaya's current appearance looking unsightly and hideous. Kimetsu no Yaiba Anime: Episode 62. [14]


It is here that Kagaya reveals to Muzan his connection to his family and how he is the cause of his cursed bloodline. When Muzan rejects this and remains confident in his success to find Nezuko and become immortal, Kagaya assures him of his inevitable failure. In his eyes, the only thing that could be immortal is the feelings of humanity, and notably, the pain Muzan has caused to humans throughout his life. The demon grows tired of Kagaya's lengthy talk and prepares to kill him. With a smile on his face, Kagaya looks at Muzan as the mansion explodes, sacrificing himself, Amane, Hinaki and Nichika to stun Muzan for the arriving Hashira.


Legacy

Kagaya's position as Oyakata was replaced by his only son Kiriya, who staked-out in the Butterfly Mansion to aid and keep track of the Demon Slayers while they were trapped inside the Infinity Castle.


In the immediate aftermath of his death, he was greatly mourned by those who knew him. Sanemi, unaware of his willing sacrifice, catatonically lameneted being unable to protect Kagaya and directed his rage towards slaying demons inside the Infinity Castle. Muichiro, who was told of Kagaya's plan by Gyomei, cursed Muzan for taking his second father-figure and vowed to kill him, with Gyomei sharing the sentiment. Kanata thinking of her deceased parents and sisters began to cry but was told to compose herself by Kiriya.


When Nezuko sensed her brother's peril and fled the Butterfly Mansion, Kiriya panicked but was told by the spirit of his father to leave Nezuko be, making the boy call off any attempts to pursue her. After the final battle which resulted in the ultimate victory for the Demon Slayers, just as Kagaya predicted, Kiriya disbanded the Demon Slayer Corps, and was told by Giyu that his father would be proud of him.
