Kanae Kocho (胡こ蝶ちょう カナエ Kochō Kanae) was a minor character in Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba. She was a Demon Slayer of the Demon Slayer Corps and the late Flower Hashira (花はな柱ばしら Hana Bashira). She was also the older sister of Shinobu Kocho and the adoptive older sister of Kanao Tsuyuri.


She was slain several years prior to the present story by Upper Rank Two, Doma.

Appearance

Kanae was a girl of a slender build, much like Shinobu. She was taller than her younger sister but still comparably petite when standing next to most Demon Slayers, and she had notably pink lips in contrast to her pale complexion. She had long, straight black hair that reached just below her waist, worn down with two shorter clumps over her shoulders, and arched, ear-length bangs on either side of her face. Her eyes were a pale violet color with a darker purple center, and slanted towards the sides of her face, with many notable white sparkles reflecting light off of them.


Kanae wore the standard Demon Slayer Corps uniform, which is black in the manga and tinted dark purple in the anime. Her uniform consisted of a button-up jacket with a stand-up collar and long sleeves, featuring gold buttons, a shirt pocket on the left breast, and the kanji for Destroy (滅めつ Metsu) engraved on the back of the uniform. Beneath the jacket she wore a white long-sleeved collared undershirt, and her outfit was fastened at the waist with the standard white belt. She wote traditional tattsuke-hakama pants matching the dark-purple tone of her uniform. Around her lower legs, Kanae wrapped a strip of black fabric, functioning similarly to kyahan, and wore dark purple tabi socks that matched her uniform's accent. On her feet, she wore zōri sandals with pale purple straps.


Over her uniform, Kanae wore a white butterfly-patterned haori that fades to pale turquoise then pink toward the lower sleeves and the hem, with the cuffs and hem trimmed in black that are decorated with pale turquoise spots, which Shinobu now wears in the present. She also sported a pair of green butterfly ornaments with pink trim on the outer side of the wings, and decorated with white speckled spots adorned on either side of her head-one of which was later used by her adoptive younger sister Kanao as a memento.

Personality

Kanae was shown to be a good-natured girl with an easy-going personality; in both Shinobu and Kanao's flashbacks, she was almost always seen with a gentle smile on her face. Kimetsu no Yaiba Anime: Episode 25. [2] Kimetsu no Yaiba Extra: Bonus Manga. [3] When Sanemi Shinagawa lashed out against their leader, Kagaya_Ubuyashiki, Kanae showed concern for Kagaya and gently informed Sanemi how their master truly cares for the Demon Slayers under his charge. In a Volume extra depicting what happened after his meeting, while Sanemi was sternly lectured by the Stone and Sound Hashira, Kanae was not mad at him and simply relayed her disappointment in him for his harsh words against Kagaya. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Volume 18, (Extra Pages). [4]


She was also shown to be somewhat of a whimsical character, as she once responded to a tirade by an angered Shinobu that she would prefer to see her smile instead of her frown, with her own smile and lack of clear reasoning serving to annoy her more. Kimetsu no Yaiba Anime: Episode 25. [2] Kimetsu no Yaiba Extra: Bonus Manga. [3] Additionally, her initial reasoning as to why she had decided to give the emotionally broken Kanao a coin to help make choices was because she was cute, which also did not soothe Shinobu's stance on the issue at hand; however, she later clarified with her belief and hope that someday, the girl's heart would heal and open in time. Kanae even had a dream of someday being able to live peacefully alongside demons despite all the suffering and death they have inflicted on humans. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 50 (Pages 7-12). [5]


She was also extremely kind to demons, as Kanao mentioned that; despite being fatally wounded and on the verge of death by Doma, she still felt sorry for the demon due to his emptiness, even as she was dying. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 157 (Pages 10-13). [6] Anecdotally, several demons had commented on what it was like to be slain by Kanae, and hty all noted that she would say kind words as they died, as if to send them to Heaven. Kimetsu no Yaiba First Fanbook. [7] However, Kanae had a much stricter side, as shown when Shinobu was on the verge of defeat during her battle with Doma. Kanae's spirit ordered her to stand up and face her opponent, brushing away the excuses forming in her mind that she was too delicate to take on demons. Kimetsu no Yaiba Movie: Infinity Castle - Part 1: Akaza Returns. [8] Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 142 (Pages 11-13). [9] Despite this, Kanae was deeply shaken by Shinobu's death, as seen through a Volume Extra page depicting her with a forlorn expression and shedding tears. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Volume 17, (Extra Pages). [10]


Abilities

Overall Abilities

During her time as a Demon Slayer, Kanae was skilled enough to hold the position of the Flower Hashira, a position only reserved for Demon Slayers who have slain one of the Twelve Kizuki or have successfully battled and slain at least 50 demons, further implying her incredible skill. An additional testament to Kanae's strength is that she was confident enough to directly challenge Upper Rank Two, Doma. Kimetsu no Yaiba Movie: Infinity Castle - Part 1: Akaza Returns. [8] Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 142 (Pages 12-13). [11]


Keen Intellect: Kanae was rather observant, seeing through Doma's acting and lies, and deducing that he was in fact emotionless and empty. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 157 (Pages 10-13). [6]


Physical Abilities

Enhanced Stamina: As a Hashira, Kanae had attained tremendous levels of stamina and endurance due to years of harsh training. Even after being fatally wounded by Doma, Kanae still held on long enough to describe his appearance and traits to Shinobu before succumbing to her wounds. Kimetsu no Yaiba Movie: Infinity Castle - Part 1: Akaza Returns. [8] Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 141 (Pages 1-4). [12]


Enhanced Strength: Though she was similarly dainty in frame, unlike her younger sister Shinobu, who lacked the physical strength to cut off a demon's head and had compensated by using a uniquely-designed Nichirin Sword and new fighting style, Kanae wielded an ordinary-looking blade and was capable of beheading demons.


Fighting Style

Master Swordswoman: As a Hashira, Kanae was, at one point, one of the most powerful swordswomen in the Demon Slayer Corps organization, which was supported by the fact that she was confident enough in her skill to challenge the second strongest Upper Rank in the Twelve Kizuki.



Breathing Styles

Flower Breathing (花はなの呼こ吸きゅう Hana no kokyū): A Breathing Style Kanae mastered well enough to give her the title as the Flower Hashira and battle Upper Rank Two, Doma. In addition, her adopted sister Kanao learned how to utilize Flower Breathing herself by watching Kanae perform them. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Volume 19, (Extra Pages). [1]


Techniques

  • Second Form: Honorable Shahow (弐にノ型かた   御み影かげ梅うめ Ni no kata: Mikage Ume) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 158 (Page 5). [13] - The user releases several rotating sword slashes that deflects incoming physical attacks.
  • Fourth Form: Crimson Hanagoromo (肆しノ型かた   紅べに花はな衣ごろも Shi no kata: Beni Hanagoromo) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 143 (Page 14). [14] - The user unleashes a single sword slash that curves and twists gracefully.
  • Fifth Form: Peonies of Futility (伍ごノ型かた   徒あだの芍しゃく薬やく Go no kata: Ada no Shakuyaku) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 158 (Page 4). [15] - The user unleashes a flurry of nine consecutive attacks that flow and weave in on themselves.
  • Sixth Form: Whirling Peach (陸ろくノ型かた   渦うず桃もも Roku no kata: Uzumomo) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 158 (Page 7). [16] - A technique used after or during evasion, the user spins around moving their body weight to deliver an attack.
  • Final Form: Equinoctial Vermillion Eye (終ついノ型かた   彼ひ岸がん朱しゅ眼がん Tsui no kata: Higan Shugan) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 162 (Page 18). [17] - This is a focusing technique that raises the user's kinetic vision to its maximum as a last resort. When unleashed, this technique turns their sclera red, and the user perceives the world as if it were in slow motion. Usage of the technique, is highly dangerous however, as the tremendous strain on the eyes could cause blood vessels to rupture and lead to partial or permanent blindness. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 162 (Page 19). [18] This technique is still usable even if the user only has one fully functional eye. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 202 (Pages 16-17). [19]

Equipment

Standard Nichirin Katana: Kanae wielded a standard sized and shaped Nichirin katana that is pink in color. Her katana's tsuba seems to be the same as her younger sister, Shinobu's: a four-petaled aqua-colored flower, trimmed with an orange border. As a Hashira, Kanae's blade has the words Destroyer of Demons (惡あっ鬼き滅めっ殺さつ Akki Messatsu) engraevd on the side. The handle is in an orange color, wrapped with a turquoise cords and capped with an orange kashira.


Her katana broke during her encounter with Doma.

Trivia

  • Kanae's surname is composed of the Kan-on'yomi reading of the kanji for "butterfly" (胡こ蝶ちょう kochō). Kanae's given name is spelled with three katakana characters; which differs from Shinobu, her biological sister, and is similar to how Kanao's name is spelled.
  • Kanae shares her English voice actress with Rui's human mother, Bridget Hoffman.
