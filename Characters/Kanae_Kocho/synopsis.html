History

The Kocho sisters lived a peaceful, privileged life with their wealthy and loving parents, who continuously doted on and cared for them. Growing up wanting for nothing, the two sisters were content with their life together. However, this all came crashing down when a demon attacked their home. Their parents tried to protect the two but were slaughtered before their daughters' eyes. They would have been next, but they were saved by the timely arrival of Gyomei Himejima, who killed the demon immediately before it could reach either of them. After these traumatic events, the two sisters made a promise to both join the Demon Slayer Corps to stop the demons from inflicting the same horror and pain they experienced on others. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 143 (Pages 1-3). [1] Months later, the sisters made their way to Gyomei's house to thank him for saving their lives. Kanae had also asked about the Demon Slayer Corps; both Kanae and Shinobu ask if they can join it only to be told to 'go home' by Gyomei. The Stone Hashira wanted the girls to just live long happy lives, something Shinobu loudly protested, causing Kanae to apologize on her younger sister's behalf.


Eventually, Kanae had been stubbornly talking about both her and Shinobu's determination to kill every demon they encounter so no one will suffer what they both had suffered. Gyomei was angry about it and himself for not accepting it. A few days go bu of it turning into a routine as Gyomei wanted them to leave but both of them were stubborn. After 3 days had gone by, he admitted defeat. In the following morning, he led them to the back of the house with a huge boulder. He told them if they can move it then he will introduce them to trainers to become Demon Slayers. However, both Shinobu and Kanae would be introduced to entirely different trainers. They were upset by it but fixed their composures quickly. In the afternoon, he informed both Kanae and Shinobu he'd be off while both of them threw their body weights to move the boulder with no luck. Gyomei believed the wouldn't move the boulder; however, on return, he saw both Kanae and Shinobu were horribly worn out and exhausted. Gyomei was shocked to see both Kanae and Shinobu were still at the boulder. He had taken notice it had changed. It was far from a block but the boulder had indeed been moved.


Shinobu and Kanae had dug up a hole near the rock and pushed in a lever stick to move the boulder. Gyomei sighs and decides to honor his promise. The next morning, they set out to go to their retrospective trainers. Kimetsu no Yaiba: One-Winged Butterfly. [2] Both of them soon became successful Demon Slayers after completing their training and developing their own Breathing Styles.


At some point down the line, Kanae became the Flower Hashira. She was present at Sanemi's first Hashira meeting, right next to him and looking at him with a concerned expression when he rudely confronted their leader, saying that he didn't really care what happened to the Demon Slayers. When Kagaya mentions Masachika Kumeno by name, Kanae informed the surprised Sanemi that Kagaya made a point to memorize the names and the backgrounds of each Demon Slayer that died in combat. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 168 (Pages 9-16). [3] After the meeting ended, she joined Gyomei and Tengen, the Stone and Sound Hashira respectively, in scolding Sanemi who sheepishly listened. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Volume 19. [4]


One day while traveling through a village, Kanae took notice of a little girl being dragged along with a leash by a man. Even though the girl was nameless, dirty, and didn't speak, Kanae approached her and attempted to convince the man who purchased her to let her go. When he demanded money in compensation, Shinobu flung money in the air, grabbed the rope he released, and they ran off with the girl. They then took her to the Butterfly Mansion, adopted her, and named her Kanao, a name chosen by Aoi Kanzaki, who already lived there. When the girl proved unresponsive without orders, Kanae gave her a coin to flip to help her make decisions until she could make decisions on her own.


Some years later, one night Kanae ran into the Upper Rank Two of the Twelve Kizuki, Doma. She was fatally wounded during the encounter as a result of being slashed across her torso by his razor-sharp fans, but he was forced to flee from the rising sun before he could devour her. Kimetsu no Yaiba Movie: Infinity Castle - Part 1: Akaza Returns. [5] Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 142 (Pages 12-13). [6] As she laid in a hysterical Shinobu's arms, she tried to convince her younger sister to leave the Demon Slayer Corps to live a normal life, but her sister was dead set on avenging her and begged to know the demon's appearance. With the last of her strength, Kanae described Doma's physical appearance and traits to Shinobu before succumbing to her wounds. Kimetsu no Yaiba Movie: Infinity Castle - Part 1: Akaza Returns. [5] Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 141 (Pages 1-4). [7]


Synopsis

Rehabilitation Training Arc

During Tanjiro Kamado's training to become stronger with Rehabilitation Training, Shinobu met the boy and one night commended him for his spirit and will to push on. To Shinobu's shock, Tanjiro unexpectedly asked her if she is angry, despite having a smile on her. Her expression saddened as she stated that she had always been angry at demons and the pain they caused humanity, unlike her beloved older sister Kanae. She, like Tanjiro, saw sympathy in demons, and when she was killed, Shinobu attempted to maintain a friendly and cheery façade in rememberance of her sister but she couldn't hold the same dreams, seeing demons as irredeemable. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 50 (Pages 7-12). [8]


Infinity Castle Arc

After being pulled into the Infinity Castle along with the rest of the Demon Slayer Corps, Shinobu happened upon the temple of Doma himself. He eventually recognized Kanae's haori and recollected how he missed out on eating her, Kimetsu no Yaiba Movie: Infinity Castle - Part 1: Akaza Returns. [5] Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 141 (Pages 12-13). [9] enraging Shinobu. When she was on the brink of defeat, Kanae's spirit appeared before her and commanded her to continue fighting. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 142 (Pages 11-13). [10] After Doma's death at the hands of Kanao and Inosuke Hashibira, Shinobu and Kanae's spirits gently patten their exhausted adopted sister on the head, commending her for doing well.


Shinobu and Kanae are seen in kimonos, run hand in hand towards their parents as they all ascend to Heaven. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 163 (Pages 18-19). [11] Kanao, who previously internalized her emotions due to the abuse she suffered during her childhood, apologized to Kanae because she could not cry during her funeral, unlike the other residents of the Butterfly Mansion, despite at the time, no one blamed her. She saw her own hair pin, which was formerly Kanae's, broken and found Shinobu's pin in the lotus pond after Doma disintegrated, before clutching the memoirs of her sisters to her chest and crying for the first time, mourning Kanae and Shinobu. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 163 (Pages 13-19). [12]


Legacy

Kanae would be reincarnated in the modern day as a student in Sekirei Women's Academy, and is seem happily walking alongside Shinobu's reincarnation. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 205 (Page 9). [13]
