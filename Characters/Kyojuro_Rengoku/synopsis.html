Quick Answers

What is the significance of the title 'Flame Hashira' that Kyojuro Rengoku held?
The 'Flame Hashira' is a prestigious title in the Demon Slayer Corps, denoting the most skilled Flame Breathing user of their generation. Kyojuro Rengoku, a descendant of the first Flame Hashira, Chojuro Rengoku, held this title. He demonstrated his mastery of Flame Breathing, a skill he honed through studying instructions from previous Flame Hashira in the Rengoku family.
In which episode does Kyojuro Rengoku, the Flame Hashira, die?
Flame Hashira Kyojuro Rengoku meets his end in the 33rd episode of Kimetsu no Yaiba. In a fierce battle with Upper Rank Three Akaza, Rengoku sustains lethal injuries. Despite his valiant efforts and powerful Flame Breathing techniques, he is ultimately defeated and succumbs to his wounds.
What led to the change in behavior of Kyojuro Rengoku's father after his wife's death?
The death of his wife and retirement from the Demon Slayer Corp led to a significant change in Shinjuro Rengoku's behavior. He became distant, apathetic, and even abusive, showing little interest when Kyojuro became the Flame Hashira. His grief and the discovery of Hinokami Kagura's superiority to other breathing styles further fueled his negative transformation.

History

Kyojuro was the eldest son of Shinjuro Rengoku. His father was knwon as the Flame Hashira of the Demon Slayer Corps until his unexpected retirement. Kyojuro also had a younger brother who trained alongside him until their father ceased to teach them. His mother, Ruka Rengoku, passed away after battling an untreatable disease some time after giving birth to Senjuro. His father on the other hand became negligent and abusive, due to a mixture of grief from his wife's death and disillusionment after discovering the existence of Hinokami Kagura, and it's superiority to all other breathing styles. Kyojuro was still close to his brother and helped encourage his pursuit in swordsmanship, even when his brother lacked the talent to do so.


During his tenure as a Demon Slayer, he trained a young, abnormally strong girl. Although the two were very fond of each other, she couldn't master Flame Breathing, instead opting to use a style derived from it, known as Love Breathing. He also encountered a demon who was part of the Twelve Kizuki, Lower Rank Two: Hairo. The demon mistook him for his father, whom he held a grudge against, but Kyojuro, with the aid of his protégée, defeated the demon. For this achievement, he was promoted to being the Flame Hashira, like his father before him, although he reacted with nihilistic apathy.


Synopsis

Rehabilitation Training Arc

Kyojuro is present with his fellow Hashira when Tanjiro Kamado awakens. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 44 (Pages 21-22). [1] Shinobu Kocho notes that Tanjiro is on trial, for which Kyojuro sees no need for as he was protecting a demon, which is a clear violation of conduct; he states that it was a matter they could deal with themselves. He then agrees with Gyomei Himejima's idea to kill Nezuko Kamado, witnessing shortly afterward Tanjiro's coughing fit, and remains silent when his former student, now Love Hashira, Mitsuri Kanroji asks if they should be dealing with their subordinate's trial without Kagaya Ubuyashiki's input. After Tanjiro headbutts Sanemi Shinazugawa, Kyojuro looks at Mitsuri when she laughs with a neutral expression. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 45 (Pages 1-19). [2]


Mugen Train Arc

Kyojuro is sent to investigate a series of brutal attacks plaguing a small village, believing it to be related to the incident surrounding the disappearance of forty passengers aboard the Mugen train. He meets with another Demon Slayer at a ramen shop and they discuss their next plan of action, to which he is informed the Mugen Train is being held at a maintenance facility after being put out of service. After finishing their meal, they set off to a nearby train station. They encounter a young girl, Fuku, saddened by the lack of customers buying her bento boxes as the news of the attacks is scaring everyone into staying inside after dark. Kyojuro unabashedly tells her he is looking for a demon, to which she angrily hurls her bean bun and retorts that demons don't exist. However, he is unfazed by her actions. After being calmed by her grandmother, Fuku apologizes and asks if he would like to buy some bento boxes. He happily buys them all and gives them to his companion, telling him to return to the Demon Slayer Corps, as the bentos will make great gifts.


Kyojuro, carrying a crate of bento boxes, boards a train headed for maintenance, believing he will be taken to the same facility as the Mugen train. After being confronted by a train conductor and told the Mugen train's actual location, he leaps off the train and heads to the train maintenance facility. There, he sees the train and senses the faint scent of a demon, confirming a demon is responsible for the disappearances. He is met by the chief engineer, to whom Kyojuro claims he was sent to the facility to give the workers the bento boxes. As the workers eat, Kyojuro is told the train is being repaired and will be put back into service again, unnerving him. Suddenly, a scream is heard, forcing Kyojuro to confront the situation. A marked demon appears, responsible for the attacks reported, clutching a young worker in his claws and causing him to bleed heavily. Kyojuro tells the culprit to surrender him, but the demon tells him human food disgusts him now, stepping on a bento box, to which he adds that he only eats humans he finds palpable and kills the one he doesn't.


The demon activates his Blood Demon Art, enabling him to speed around the Hashira and taunt him. Kyojuro acknowledges his speed but warns him to not get cocky facing him. He informs the demon the woman he attacked is slated to make a full recovery, angering him. Seeing the destroyed bento box, the demon decides to kill the bento vendors to spite Kyojuro. He tries to kill the worker, but Kyojuro slices off both his hands and forces him to retreat. After treating the boy's injuries, he is met with other demon slayers, telling them to take charge here while he confronts the demon. Kyojuro heads towards the demon's trail and follows the train tracks, using his Total Concentration Breathing to accelerate him to incredible speeds. The demon finds Fuku and tries to kill her but is momentarily distracted by Fuku's grandmother. Fuku tries to escape but he catches her outside and tries to crush her throat. Kyojuro arrives in time, slices the demon's achilles and saves Fuku.


He confronts him again as he tries to kill Fuku's grandmother, repeating his words not to get cocky. The demon tries to challenge him over who is truly faster; if he is able to kill him before he kills her. To which Kyojuro responds that the challenge is unnecessary. He mocks the demon for being slow and rushes forward instantly, decapitating the unsuspecting demon with his Flame Breathing, First Form: Unknowing Fire. The scene reminds Fuku's grandmother of when she herself was saved by the Flame Hashira, recognizing Kyojuro's haori and manner of sheathing his blade. To this, Kyojuro states that it was likely his father Shinjuro who saved her; he is simply following in his footsteps to save people from demons. As Kakushi arrive on the scene, Kyojuro confirms the demon is dead and the Mugen train will be repaired for service the next day. When his companion expressed relief that the situation is finally over, the Hashira adds that it was likely a more powerful demon that what he just faced that is responsible for the train's disappearance.


As the sun sets, Kyojuro boards the Mugen train for his investigation when he meets Fuku and her grandmother again. They offer beef pot bento boxes in graditude for his heroic actions, to which he happily accepts and takes Fuku's entire stock. The two bid him good luck on his mission and the train leaves the station.


During the journey, while devouring his eleventh bento box while shouting "delicious", Tanjiro, who was told to meet him, introduces the Hashira to Inosuke Hashibira, Zenitsu Agatsuma and Nezuko, who he recognizes during Tanjiro's trial with the Master. After he finishes his food and Tanjiro explains his question about Hinokami Kagura, Kyojuro energetically answers that he doesn't know what Hinokami Kagura is and that this is his first time hearing about it. He says that he's delighted that Tanjiro applied his father's dance to his fighting style, then swiftly changes the subject and tells Tanjiro that he should become his Tsuguko. Tanjiro tells him to slow down and asks if he can at least try and help a little more to answer his question. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 54 (Pages 6-7). [3]


Kyojuro says that Flame Breathing has a long history, and says that there has always been a Hashira in every age who uses Water Breathing and the one who uses Flame Breathing. He explains that the other Breathing Styles were derived from either those two or Wind Breathing, Stone Breathing or Thunder Breathing. Kyojuro then asks Tanjiro what color his Nichirin Sword is, to which the latter responds that it's black, and Kyojuro laughs out and says that that must be unfortunate. Tanjiro explains why that is, and, as the train starts to take off, Kyojuro explains that he's simply never seen a swordsman with a black blade become a Hashira before, and that he's heard people whose blades turn black don't even know which Breathing Style to master. Seeing Tanjiro's face, he tells him not to worry and that he'll train him up back at his house. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 54 (Pages 8-9). [4]


The Train Conductor then comes over to the four of them to stamp their tickets. Tanjiro smells something unusual, and Kyojuro appears to sense it too as he asks the conductor to step aside, simultaneously preparing his sword. A large-bodied demon appears at the other end of the carriage, and Kyojuro wonders if it was hiding with its Blood Demon Art unbeknownst to him, before darting forward to behead it with the Flame Breathing, First Form: Unknowing Fire. The demon's dead body falls to the floor, only for Kyojuro to add that a second one is nearby, prompting Tanjiro and Inosuke to follow him. As the frightened crowd flee to another carriage, they find the demon cornering a single passenger, leading Inosuke to charge ahead and ignoring the two. When he is nearly hit by its longs arms, Kyojuro rushes forward to save Inosuke and the passenger, before using his Flame Breathing, Second Form: Rising Scorching Sun to kill it. An amazed Tanjiro, Inosuke and Zenitsu all declare they would love to be his Tsuguko, leading him to laugh and state that he would gladly train them all.

Kyojuro gets up and walks out of the house, out into the garden, his little brother, Senjuro, coming to greet him and asking if their father was happy about the news and wondering if he would acknowledge him too if he also becomes a Hashira. Legacy

Kyojuro's colleagues were very fond of him and as such were deeply troubled by his death. He would not be forgotten though, as the late swordsman would be remembered by Sound Hashira Tengen Uzui during his fight with Upper Six, Gyutaro, and Mist Hashira Muichiro Tokito after defeating Upper Five, Gyokko. In addition, his young protégée, Tanjiro Kamado, would be particularly devastated by the loss of his mentor and friend, becoming catatonic for months, and even going as far to begin to wish that he had died instead of Kyojuro. During his fight with Upper Four, Hantengu, Kyojuro was among those Tanjiro thought of as needing to be repaid; Kyojuro's hand was also among the ones on Tanjiro's back during the fight. His younger brother Senjuro reacted with grief when the news reached him. However, when Tanjiro visited the Rengoku household to deliver Kyojuro's last words and read the notes Shinjuro Rengoku kept, the jaded former Hashira drunkenly insulted his deceased son and claimed that his death was due to a lack of worth and talent. After a physical altercation with the boy, Shinjuro was given his elder son's final words to him by his remaining son. Not being able to hide behind his wall of denial and apathy anymore, Kyojuro's father broke down in tears, a starting point for the man to finally turn his life around.


Tanjiro would adopt his sword guard and use it for his newest sword. Before it was attached however, it ended up saving a young boy's life by protecting him from a near-fatal laceration from a fish demon that Gyokko summoned. When Tanjiro once again encountered Akaza, alongside the Water Hashira, Giyu Tomioka, Kyojuro was brought up several times by the Upper Rank demon. Akaza, now witnessing Tanjiro's newfound strength, claimed to have now understood what Kyojuro meant by rebuking the claim that he was weak, and later went on to say that he was glad Kyojuro had died because of his foolish mindset and believed that he wouldn't have gotten any stronger. Tanjiro was infuriated at what he perceived as an insult to his late mentor, but Akaza claimed that he was actually praising him.


Later on, when Tanjiro was turned into a demon after the defeat of the Demon King, Muzan Kibutsuji, he was among the many hands that pushed him back towards being a human alongside many of his colleagues whom had also fallen in battle, when the spirit of Muzan attempted to sabotage the serum administered to cure Tanjiro's condition.


Several years into the future, Tanjiro's final sword, still donning Kyojuro's tsuba, would be hung in the Kamado household as an antique.
