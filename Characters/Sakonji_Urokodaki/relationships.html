Relationships

Giyu Tomioka

Urokodaki was Giyu's master, training him alongside Sabito, teaching them both Water Breathing. Giyu was the first of his disciples to survive the Final Selection, eventually earning the title of Water Hashira, just like his former master before him.


Urokodaki has a lot of affection and trust in Giyu, which can be seen in his actions. When he received a letter from Giyu explaining Tanjiro and his sister's situation and asking to be trained, he immediately went to find him and accepted him as his new disciple. He also took care of Nezuko despite knowing she was a demon, even accepting the risk with Tomioka of committing seppuku if she ever attacked a human. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 46 (Pages 9-10). [1]


Tanjiro Kamado

Initially, Urokodaki hesitated to train Tanjiro, noticing his slowness in making important decisions. Nevertheless, he took him and his sister to his home and then put him to the test, telling him he had to descend Mount Sagiri, which was riddled with traps, before dawn. Tanjiro successfully completed the challenge just before the time limit, earning Sakonji's acceptance as his new disciple.


He trained Tanjiro for over a year, improving his physical abilities and swordsmanship, as well as teaching him Water Breathing, all while caring for both him and Nezuko at his home. Despite this, after the deaths of thirteen of his students, he only allowed Tanjiro to participate in the Final Selection if he could first cut a massive boulder in half with his sword. Tanjiro wasn't the first of his disciples to be given this test, but he was the one given the largest boulder. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 7 (Page 20). [2] After months of training, Tanjiro successfully cut the boulder, exceeding Urokodaki's expectations. He congratulated him on his performance and allowed him to participate in the Final Selection to become a Demon Slayer, but not before giving him a mask to protect him from bad luck.


After seeing Tanjiro return from Mount Fujikasanae, Sakonji, along with Nezuko who had just woken up, greeted him with a hug, happy to see that he returned alive. Time passed, until one day Haganezuka arrived to give Tanjiro his Nichirin Sword, surprising Urokodaki when he saw the blade turn black. Right after this, he was called on his first mission as a Demon Slayer in the Northwest Town. Before he left, Sakonji made it clear how much he trusted him, and then gave him a wooden box he had made himself, in which he could carry Nezuko without worrying about her being exposed to sunlight. Kimetsu no Yaiba Anime: Episode 6. [3]


During the night in which the Demon Slayers were dragged into the Infinity Castle, he spoke of the Kamado siblings as cogs that fit perfectly into a great machine, wishing that Tanjiro would not lose. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 147 (Pages 8-9). [4]


Nezuko Kamado

From the beginning, he knew she was a demon, but, trusting Tomioka's words, he took her in along with Tanjiro. Nezuko remained asleep for over a year after arriving at Urokodaki's home, leading him to believe that Nezuko recovered by sleeping, rather than by eating humans, noticing that she was different from the rest of the demons. Despite hardly interacting during that time, it's clear that they both have a great deal of affection for each other.


Sakonji has a lot of trust in the Kamado siblings, so much so that he and Tomioka swore that they would commit suicide if Nezuko ever attacked a human.


When Nezuko became immune to sunlight, she was hidden from Muzan Kibutsuji by the Demon Slayer Corps, and later took Tamayo's drug to become human again. Sakonji stayed by her side, taking care of her during Hashira Training, Kimetsu no Yaiba Anime: Episode 59(Pages 8-9). [5] until the night of the final battle. After Muzan's defeat, the two meet at the Butterfly Mansion and they warmly embrace upon seeing each other. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 204 (Page 23). [6]


Sabito and Makomo

Sakonji had several disciples besides Giyu Tomioka and Tanjiro Kamado, the most notable being Sabito and Makomo. Sabito was Giyu's best friend, seemingly possessing greater potential than Tomioka himself. Unfortunately, all of them were killed during the Final Selection to become Demon Slayer, which led Urokodaki to increase the difficulty of his training with each new disciple.


Urokodaki cared deeply for each of his disciples, crafting custom Kitsune Masks for them all and imbuing them with a protective spell with the intention of keeping them safe. Unbeknownst to him, this was precisely what allowed the Hand Demon to identify his disciples during Final Selection.


Although Urokodaki doesn't know it, the spirits of all his former students reside on Mount Sagiri, near his home, due to the deep affection all the children have for him. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 5 (Pages 12-13). [7]


Hand Demon

Sakonji captured the Hand Demon during the Edo Period of the Keio era, at a time when he was the Water Hashira, to imprision him in Mount Fujikasanae to serve as an obstacle demon in the Final Selection. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 7 (Pages 3-4). [8]


Although to the former Water Hashira he was nothing more than a simple demon, the Hand Demon harbored a deep grudge against him, taking it upon himself to kill all of Urokodaki's disciples he encountered, being responsible for Sakonji's greatest losses without his knowledge. It was not until his confrontation with Tanjiro that he was finally defeated, thus bringing peace to the spirits of his master's former disciples.
