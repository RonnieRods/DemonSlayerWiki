History

Sakonji was a capable Demon Slayer trained with the style of Water Breathing, and was solely responsible for capturing the Hand Demon and imprisoning him on Mount Fujikasanae. He would then go on to become the Water Hashira for an undisclosed period of time, before retiring to become one of the cultivators of the Water Breathing style to aspiring Demon Slayers.


Sakonji had several disciples whom he trained to face the Final Selection but unfortunately, the vast majority of them died without ever passing it. Among them, the most notable were Makomo Sabito and Giyu Tomioka. The latter two were trained together, becoming very close friends during their training, however only Giyu survived the Final Selection, becoming the only one of his students to become a Demon Slayer, and over the years, a Water Hashira.


Synopsis

Final Selection Arc

Having received a letter from Giyu Tomioka requesting that he train Tanjiro Kamado, explaining the boy and his sister's circumstances, Sakonji set forth from his home to find the two. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 3 (Pages 2-4). [1] Upon finding them, he stops Tanjiro from attempting to kill a demon with his knife, stating that he will be unable to kill the creature with such a weak weapon. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 2 (Pages 24-25). [2] Admonishing the youth for his question of how to properly defeat the beast, Sakonji watches as Tanjiro struggles to come up with a way to dispatch the creature. Sniffing the youthm Sakonji deems him unworthy to be a Demon Slayer, as he still has the scent of kindness even when confronted by a vile demon. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 3 (Pages 4-6). [3]


While Tanjiro continues struggling to kill the demon, Sakonji buries the victims of the creature, afterwards kneeling by their graves with his hands together in prayer. After Tanjiro's hesistation leaves the demon to die in the sunlight, he then introduces himself to Tanjiro, asking if he is the one Giyu spoke of in his letter. Getting an affirmation to his inquiry, Sakonji goes on to ask the boy on what he will do if his sister decides to feast on a human, slapping him when he hesitates to answer. He admonishes him on his slowness to answer, stating that he lacks determination, and stresses that he must not allow his sister to harm another human under any circumstance. Getting an answer of conviction from Tanjiro, the trainer agrees to test the boy to see if he is worthy of becoming a Demon Slayer, telling him to bring his sister and follow him. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 3 (Pages 8-12). [4]


Sakonji then swiftly makes for his home, with Tanjiro struggling to keep up with his pace. Arriving at his hut, Sakonji tells the exhausted youth that the test begins now, instructing him to hike up the mountain. He travels with Tanjiro part way up the mountain before suddenly telling him that he must make his way back to his home before dawn. He then vanishes into the heavy fog surrounding them. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 3 (Pages 12-16). [5] Later, having made his way back down the mountain, Sakonji drapes a blanket over Nezuko, before noticing a noise at his door. A heavily exhausted and injured Tanjiro opens the door, prompting Sakonji to recognize his abilities and his passing of the test. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 3 (Pages 22-23). [6]


Sakonji explains his profession as a cultivator to Tanjiro, as well as how to join the Demon Slayer Corps: surviving Final Selection. To prepare him for this trial, and to see if he is worthy of even being able to participate in it, Sakonji begins to train Tanjiro in the way of the sword. He explains and drills him in how to descend the mountain successfully and repeatedly, how to breathe properly to gain power, the intricacies of the weapon and the forces behind it, sparring with Tanjiro to improve his endurance and stance using only his bare hands, and hetting him used to harsh conditions. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 4 (Pages 4, 7-9). [7]


When Nezuko continues to fall asleep without waking up, Sakonji calls for a doctor, who cannot find anything wrong with her. Finally, after a year has passed, the elder tells Tanjiro that he has nothing more to teach him, instructing him to prove himself by improving the basics he has been taught. He then leads the boy to a huge boulder, telling him that if he can cut it in half, he will be permitted to go to Final Selection. When Tanjiro hesitates, overcome by doubt, Sakonji leaves without a word, his hand in the boy's training completed. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 4 (Pages 9-13). [8]


Six months later, after Tanjiro finally succeeds in slicing the boulder in half, Sakonji explains his reasons for not wanting Tanjiro to go to Final Selection, complimenting the boy on his accomplishment. He then wraps Tanjiro in a hug, urging him to come back alive from the Demon Slayer Corps' test. Before Tanjiro leaves, the trainer gives him a mask to ward off bad luck. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 6 (Pages 3-4). [9] Additionally, Sakonji explains that demons increase in power by consuming humans, and that Tanjiro will be able to figure out how many humans a demon has consumed if he increases the sensitivity of his nose. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 7 (Page 6). [10] When Tanjiro wishes him farewell, asking that he look after Sabito and Makomo, Sakonji wonders aloud how the boy knew the names of his two dead apprentices. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 6 (Page 5). [11]


After Tanjiro returns from Final Selection, Sakonji and Nezuko embrace him, the old trainer weeping tears of joy over the long-awaited return of a student of his. Fifteen days after this, Haganezuka arrives with Tanjiro's new sword, but ignores the youth's attempts at hospitatlity, prompting Sakonji to note to himself that the swordsmith's behavior had not changed. He then agrees with Haganezuka when he notes that Tanjiro's blade may turn red, watching shortly after that as it instead turns black. Nothing this, the elder watches as Haganezuka throws a fit over this unexpected development. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 9 (Pages 8-17). [12]


Sakonji then watches in silence as Tanjiro buttons up his uniform, and subsequently shows him a box made out of "Mist Cloud Fir", for which he can hide Nezuko from the sun with. He speculates that Tanjiro's sister may have fallen into her coma because she was attempting to regain strength through sleep, rather than through consuming humans. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 10 (Pages 3-4). [13] He goes on to tell Tanjiro that while she was sleeping, he gave Nezuko some suggestions to her subconscious to defend humans as her family from other demons. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 11 (Page 19). [14] He also informs his apprentice about Blood Demon Arts Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 10 (Page 15). [15] and the sole demon responsible for the creation of all other and the likely murderer of Tanjiro's family, Muzan Kibutsuji. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 11 (Pages 2-3). [16]


Rehabilitation Training Arc

After Nezuko's existence was discovered by the Demon Slayer Corps, Sakonji sends a letter to Kagaya Ubuyashiki. In it, he requested Tanjiro Kamado be forgiven for harboring a demon and explained Nezuko's unique circumstances. He further wrote that if Nezuko were to ever kill a human, he, Tanjiro and Giyu will all commit seppuku to repent. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 46 (Pages 9-10). [17]


Hashira Training Arc

During Hashira Training, after Tanjiro had finished training with Muichiro Tokito, the Hashira noted his optimistic attitude for the future and asked him why he was so confident in Nezuko being safe. Tanjiro remarked he was assured that Nezuko would be protected as she was under the watchful eye of a former friend, revealing that Sakonji was tasked with looking after Nezuko after she fell unconscious.


Final Battle Arc


Infinity Castle Arc

While Tanjiro and the remaining Hashira engaged Muzan and the remaining Kizuki in the Infinity Castle, Sakonji continued to watch over and protect Nezuko, whose newfound resistance to sunlight Muzan now seeks. He also gave her the cure developed by Tamayo, which would turn her back into a human and thus ruin Muzan's plans. Sakonji grows nervous, as the words "Final stage" crosses his mind again, resulting in his heart pounding, and his body trembling; he never thought he'd live to see the day when the long battle would finally conclude. While tending to her as she fell ill as a result of taking the cure, Sakonji thinks about everything that happened since he met her and Tanjiro and tells both of them not to lose. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 147 (Pages 4-8). [18]


Sunrise Countdown Arc

While watching over Nezuko, she suddenly wakes up and leaves the Demon Slayer Corps. Despite Sakonji attempting to bring her back, Nezuko leapt off a cliff, preventing the former Hashira from going after her. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 185 (Pages 2-3). [19] After Muzan's defeat, Urokodaki and Tomioka encounter the Kamado brothers who run towards them to greet them. Upon meeting Nezuko, she immediately greets him with a hug.
