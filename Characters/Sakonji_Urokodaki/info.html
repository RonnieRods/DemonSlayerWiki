Sakonji Urokodaki (鱗うろこ滝だき 左さ近こん次じ Urokodaki Sakonji) is a minor character in Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba. He is a retired member of the Demon Slayer Corps, having held the position of the previous Water Hashira (水みず柱ばしら Mizu Bashira). He is a cultivator of the Water Breathing style, having trained Makomo, Sabito, Giyu Tomioka and Tanjiro Kamado Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 46 (Page 9). Appearance

Sakonji is a reasonably tall elderly man with fair skin tone, as noted by the countless number of students he has trained, along with his sliver-colored hair. His face has never once been revealed or depicted throughout the entire completed manga; he is always seen wearing a tengu mask that covers his entire face although Tanjiro states through a Taisho Secret that Sakonji's face is very gentle and calm.


He is always seen wearing a blue, cloud-patterned jinbei kimono with navy colored pants which are fastened at his shins by white kyahan guards. He also wears traditional black tabi socks, and sandals similar to Tanjiro, except his sandals have blue straps. On the occasions when Sakonji travels, he wears a white cloth that covers his head and wraps around his neck.


When he was younger, his hair was black and fashioned into a topknot.

Personality

As a trainer and former Demon Slayer, Sakonji is pragmatic, strict and vigilant, while simultaneously sympathetic and understanding. When first meeting Tanjiro, he was quick to criticize him for his weak resolve and innate kindness, harshly telling him the solution to when Nezuko eats someone is to kill her and then himself. He possessed a no-nonsense demeanor while training Tanjiro, repeatedly kicking, beating and harshly instructing him to get his bearings together and get things quickly and properly; he even began to utilize traps armed with knives and spikes when Tanjiro began to improve his descent of his mountain. That said, he became incredibly proud of the Demon Slayer Tanjiro would become.


As a cultivator who had lost so many students over the years, Sakonji was haunted by his association with the Hand Demon, realizing it had been the demon to kill his past 13 students. He flatly told Tanjiro that he had no intention of sending him to Final Selection and believed the giant boulder he had set on the boy was too big to cut. Taken aback by his student's accomplishment, he hugged him and told him to come home. Upon seeing that Tanjiro would indeed return from Final Selection, Sakonji embraced him and cried in relief. He also commended him for being the one to kill the Hand Demon.


He showed great dedication to Nezuko, attempting and successfully, to implant suggestions within her to protect humans and kill demons. He wrote to Kagaya Ubuyashiki his belief that Nezuko was completely able to withstand her hunger of humans, even putting his own life on the line by promising to commit sepukku should the occasion ever arise.


Abilities

Overall Abilities

As the former Water Hashira, he was once one of the Demon Slayer Corps' most powerful fighters. He singlehandedly captured and imprisoned the Hand Demon on Mount Fujikasanae, a notable feat considering the presumed challenge of disabling the powerful demon without killing it. As the cultivator of Water Breathing, he is both incredibly knowledgeable and an accomplished teacher, having trained at least 14 students in the art. Even in his old age, a young and physically fit Tanjiro barely kept up with him while running and Sakonji was strong enough to flip him over and fend his attacks off without exertion.


Enhanced Stealth: Sakonji possesses a light footstep while moving, which was noted by Tanjiro during his attempt to follow him back to Mount Sagiri. Even while running at a considerable pace, his feet barely made a sound Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 3 (Page 12). [3]


Hand to Hand Combat: Sakonji has shown to be highly skilled in hand-to-hand combat, which he used to toss and throw Tanjiro during his training, despite Tanjrio being armed with a sword and charging at him with full speed.


Physical Abilities

Enhanced Speed: Sakonji is shown to be incredibly fast; during his confrontation of the Hand Demon, he was able to catch the demon off-guard and slice it several times, despite it already having consumed several victims. He was ultimately able to appear behind it and end the battle with its capture.


Enhanced Strength: As a former Hashira, Sakonji has incredible physical prowess which he retained in old age, as Tanjiro described him as "ridiculously strong" depite being younger and going through extensive training under him.


Supernatural Abilities

Enhanced Smell: Sakonji has a heightened sense of smell, revealed when Giyu Tomioka writes to Sakonji asking for him to train Tanjiro, citing that he also possesses the same ability as the cultivator. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 3 (Page 3). [4] This was shown during his first meeting with Tanjiro during his battle against the Temple Demon, where Sakonji was able to smell out Tanjiro's emotions and detect the scent of sympathy and consideration even when considering to kill the demon. Later, he also informs Tanjiro that should his sense of smell sharpen as he trains, he can determine the number of victims a demon has eaten.


Fighting Style

Master Swordsman: As the former Water Hashira, Sakonji is a full fledged master swordsman of Water Breathing. After retiring, he still retained his knowledge of this swordsmanship style for killing demons, which he effectively passed down to his students, the best being Giyu, Sabito, Makomo and Tanjiro.


Breathing Style

Water Breathing (水みずの呼こ吸きゅう Mizu no kokyū): Sakonji is the primary instructor of this Breathing Style. He has taught ten breathing techniques to both Tanjiro Kamado and the successor of his former position as Water Hashira, Giyu Tomioka. The user can increase the amount of oxygen in one's blood by controlling their breathing pattern. Doing this increases his strength and agility to equal a demon's.


Techniques

  • First Form: Water Surface Slash (壱いちノ型かた   水みな面も斬ぎり Ichi no kata: Minamo Giri) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 7 (Page 21). [5] - The user generates enough momentum to create a powerful single concentrated slash.
  • Second Form: Water Wheel (弐にノ型かた   水みず車ぐるま Ni no kata: Mizu Guruma) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 6 (Page 18). [6] - The user leaps and vertically spins forward in the air while releasing a flowing attack in a circular motion.
  • Third Form: Flowing Dance (参さんノ型かた   流りゅう流りゅう舞まい San no kata: Ryūryū Mai) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 16 (Page 22). [7] - The user swings and bends their blade in a winding motion along with their body and dances in a flowing pattern, slicing everything in its path.
  • Fourth Form: Striking Tide (肆しノ型かた   打うち潮しお Shi no kata: Uchishio) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 6 (Page 13). [8] - The user makes multiple consecutive slashes while twisting their body and sword in a flowing fashion to deliver multiple blows simultaneously.
  • Fifth Form: Blessed Rain After the Drought (伍ごノ型かた   干かん天てんの慈じ雨う Go no kata: Kanten no Jiu) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 31 (Page 19). [9] - The user changes the grip on their sword and decapitates the opponent in a single flowing strike that causes little to no pain. It is a "sword strike of kindess" used when the enemy willingly surrenders.
  • Sixth Form: Whirlpool (陸ろくノ型かた   ねじれ渦うず Roku no kata: Nejire Uzu) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 12 (Page 11). [10] - The user fiercely twists their upper and lower body, creating a whirlpool of air that cuts anything caught in it. It's effect and efficiency is enhanced underwater, where the user can draw water around their weapon to enhance their slashes.
  • Seventh Form: Drop Ripple Thrust (漆しちノ型かた   雫しずく波は紋もん突づき Shichi no kata: Shizuku Hamon Tsuki) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 16 (Page 10). [11] - The user performs a precise, fast and accurate thrust with their blade, blocking any attacks from any opponent as numerous ripples emerge in front of them.
  • Eighth Form: Waterfall Basin (捌はちノ型かた   滝たき壼つぼ Hachi no kata: Takitsubo) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 10 (Page 19). [12] - The user cuts the target vertically in a flowing motion which is most effective when they are falling downward.
  • Ninth Form: Splashing Water Flow, Turbulent (玖くノ型かた   水すい流りゅう飛沫しぶき・乱らん Ku no kata: Suiryū Shibuki - Ran) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 25 (Page 10). [13] - The user changes their footwork in a way that minimizes the landing time and surface needed when landing, allowing the user to move without limits. Ideal when fighting in a place with no solid foodhold.
  • Tenth Form: Constant Flux (拾じゅうノ型かた   生せい生せい流る転てん Jū no kata: Seisei Ruten) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 39 (Page 12). [14] - The user performs a continuous flowing attack which seemingly takes the form of a water dragon that increases in strength and power with each rotation due to momentum.

Game Exclusive Techniques

  • Eighth Form: Waterfall Basin, Destruction (捌はちノ型かた   滝たき壼つぼ・壊かい Hachi no kata: Takitsubo - Kai) Kimetsu no Yaiba Game: The Hinokami Chronicles. [15] - The user leaps forwards towards their enemy in a spinning motion and slashes them as they pass. They then turn around and leap behind the enemy to slash them with a curved strike, before uppercutting them while leaping high into the air and then falling down with great force in a overhead strike, smashing the enemy to the ground and unleashing multiple gargantuan waterfalls that pulverize their opponent.
  • Master's Wisdom (育そだ手ての知ち恵え Sodate no Chie) Kimetsu no Yaiba Game: The Hinokami Chronicles. [15] - The user sets a trap on the ground that releases sharpened bamboo shoots protruding from the ground once the opponent walks on it.
  • The Essense of a Water Hashira (水みず柱ばしらの神しん髄ずい Mizu Bashira no Shinzui) Kimetsu no Yaiba Game: The Hinokami Chronicles 2. [16] - A collaboration requiring two exceptionally strong Water Breathing users, very similar to Fourth Form: Striking Tide, Twin Torrents. They corrdinate their attacks and strike their opponent several times; when the two are on opposite sides of each other, they perform Constant Flux, creating a powerful eruption of water when the two dragon heads collide.

Equipment

Standard Nichrin Katana: Sakonji had a standard sized and shaped Nichirin katana with a deep blue blade as is typical of Water Breathers. His tsuba is oval-shaped, colored light-gray, and decorated with ornate patterns. The handle is blue, wrapped in white cords, and it ends with a gray kashira. When Tanjiro Kamado trains with Sakonji, the cultivator lends him a similar katana, with the only difference being a generic gray blade and a dull gray oval shape with four indentations and a dull gray color. In Hinokami Chronicles, Sakonji uses the latter katana which now has a deep blue blade. Strangely, the stock art depicts Sakonji's handle as being wrapped in black cords instead of white. Trivia

  • Sakonji was ranked in 14th place as of the first character popularity poll with 213 votes.
  • Sakonji's surname, Urokodaki, contains the Kun reading of "fish, scales" (鱗 uroko) and the rendaku reading of "waterfall, cascade, rapids" (滝 daki). His given name, Sakonji, consists of the kanji for "left" (左 sa), the Kan'yo-on reading of "near, close" (近 kon), and the Kan'yo-on reading of "order, sequence" (次 ji).
  • Sakonji doesn't drink alcohol and has a very low alcohol tolerance. Kimetsu no Yaiba First Fanbook. [17]
  • In the spinoff, Kimetsu Academy, Sakonji is the school janitor. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Volume 3, (Extra Pages). [18]
  • The only type of masks he makes are fox masks. This is apparently simply because he likes foxes. Kimetsu no Yaiba First Fanbook. [17]
  • Sakonji started wearing his mask because demons used to make fun of his gentle face. Kimetsu no Yaiba First Fanbook. [17]
  • Sakonji receives money from Kagaya Ubuyashiki, but he never spends it and prefers to live a self-sufficient lifestyle. He did, however, use it to pay for the nutritious food he gave to Tanjiro while he was staying with him, and for the traps he had his hunter friends set up. Kimetsu no Yaiba First Fanbook. [17]
  • Sakonji resembles real life swordsman Miyamoto Musashi. Like Sakonji, Musashi lived out his twilight years meditating in remote mountains, described his dueling style in a memoir titled The Book of Water, and most damningly, also had a long line of prodigies who met unfortunate ends before he himself passed.

Quotes

  • (To Tanjiro Kamado about Nezuko) "Your sister must never take the life of an innocent person!" Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 3 (Page 11). [19]
  • (To himself regarding Tanjiro and Nezuko Kamado) "Tanjiro... when I think about it, ever since you brought your younger sister, who had become a demon I've felt as though large cogs have been turning. If this war is a giant machine then when the two cogs that are you and Nezuko fitted into place, it caused a stagnant situation to suddenly begin moving. Don't lose, Nezuko. Don't lose, Tanjiro. You must not lose." Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 147 (Pages 8-9). [20]
