History

Sanemi was the eldest son of seven children and lived with both of his parents. Their father was abusive, constantly lashing out at the children and their mother until one day, he was stabbed and killed by someone with a grudge against him. Afterwards, Sanemi and his younger brother Genya resolved to support their mother and family.


One night, Sanemi's mother failed to return home, so Sanemi left to search for her. At some point, he found her and realized she became a demon, but couldn't stop her from returning home and killing five of his siblings. Just before she could kill Genya, however, Sanemi tackles her through the window to the street below with a knife in hand. In the ensuing fight, she gave him the two large scars across his face, and his marechi blood — which disorients demons and makes them act intoxicated — gave him an opening to cut her down.


When Genya runs into the street to find a doctor for his grievously-injured siblings, he finds Sanemi with their mother lying daed at his feet. Panicking, and not knowing that the "wolf" that attacked them was their transformed mother, Genya screams and accuses Sanemi of murdering her. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 115 (Pages 1-13). [1]


After that, Sanemi abandoned Genya and began to hunt demons on his own with suicidal devotion. Without knowledge of Demon Slayers or Nichirin Swords, Sanemi instead armed himself with a multitude of weapons and fought demons at night, incapacitating and capturing them until daybreak, then stringing them up to let the morning sun kill them. Despite this crude and reckless battle style, he managed to survive thanks to sheer luck and the unique aroma of his blood. His unorthodox method caught the attention of the Demon Slayer Corps.


While wondering the land hunting demons, he eventually met a legitimate Demon Slayer, Masachika Kumeno, who helped him and introduced him to a proper trainer. Eventually, during their career as Demon Slayers, the two of them would encounter and defeat Ubume, the previous holder of Lower Rank One of the Twelve Kizuki. However, Kumeno lost his life in the battle, and only Sanemi was promoted to the rank of Wind Hashira. At the time of his promotion, Gyomei Himejima, Kanae Kocho, Tengen Uzui and Giyu Tomioka were already Hashiras.


Bitter over Kumeno's death, Sanemi takes his anger out on the Corps' leader Kagaya Ubuyashiki, accusing him of happily giving orders from above while the Demon Slayers bleed and die for his cause. To his surprise, Kagaya takes the criticism gracefully before mentioning Kumeno by name; Kanae then informs him that Kagaya has memorized the names and backgrounds of every single Demon Slayer who has died since he became the leader. This discovery, and reading Kumeno's will given to him by Kagaya, gives Sanemi a newfound respect for Kagaya. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 168. [2] Afterwards, he is scolded by his fellow Hashiras Kanae, Gyomei and Tengen with Giyu just watching them from a distance. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Volume 19, Extra Pages. [3]


Synopsis

Rehabilitation Training Arc

Sanemi arrives at the Demon Slayer Headquarters, where Tanjiro has been put on trial. Coming in holding Nezuko's box, he is finally introduced to the latter. Sanemi mocks Tanjiro for carrying a Demon around with him and proceeds to draw his blade before stabbing the box. Enraged, Tanjiro charges at him and manages to headbutt him. However, before they could continue the fight, the leader of the Demon Slayers arrives to begin the trial. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 45. [4]


Kagaya Ubuyashiki; the leader of the Demon Slayer Corps has just arrived, and he begins by greeting everyone that's present. His arrival initiates the Hashira meeting, which is held once every six months. Upon seeing him, Tanjiro immediately notices the scar on his face, which he deduces to be from an illness. However, before he is able to react, he gets knocked down to his knees by Sanemi to forcefully bow and show his respect to the leader.


Sanemi's tone immediately changes as he greets Kagaya politely; introducing Tanjiro who is being put on trial for traveling with a Demon. To everyone's surprise, Kagaya explains that he had already accepted the Kamado siblings, and wanted everyone to accept them as well. Sanemi along with Gyomei, Gengen, Obanai and Kyojuro immediately refuse to accept the decision.


Even after, Kagaya instructs one of his daughters to read aloud the letter that he received from Sakonji Urokodaki stating that he asks for forgiveness on Tanjiro's further explaining that Nezuko doesn't eat humans, Sanemi still remains unconvinced. Further deliberating with the Hashira about the situation, Kagaya mentions that Tanjiro has come into contact with Muzan Kibutsuji, shocking the Wind Hashira. He then proceeds to immediately bombard Tanjiro with questions of his whereabouts and abilities with the other Hashira since no one has encountered him. Tanjiro being unable to answer the questions, is left overwhelmed resulting in Kagaya raising his finger to silence the Hashira.


He proceeds to question that something unexpected must have happened to Muzan, but Sanemi refuses to understand. He pulls out his blade and cuts his arm, causing his blood to drip. Sanemi proudly proclaims that he will prove their leader wrong and proceeds to pour his blood onto Nezuko's box, trying to entice her to attack him. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 46. [5]


Obanai reminds Sanemi that the Demon won't come out in the sunlight, so he quickly moves into the building behind Kagaya; drawing his sword, prompting Tanjiro to attempt to come to his sister's aid.


Sanemi stabs Nezuko's box multiple times, prompting her to leave it. Seeing the Hashira's wounded arm, she is transfixed by the sight of his blood. Distracted from the temptation before her, Nezuko remembers her order not to hurt humans, as well as her family. With much effort, she turns away from a shocked Sanemi, prompting Kagaya to ask what had transpired. After a few words to all those present, Kagaya then reprimands Obanai and Sanemi, and informs everybody present that the issue of Tanjiro and Nezuko is over, and that the Hashira Meeting can finally begin. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 47. [6]


Mugen Train Arc

When he receives the news about Kyojuro Rengoku's death, Sanemi becomes enraged and reiterates his promise to exterminate demons from the world.


Swordsmith Village Arc

During his battle with Upper Rank Four Hantengu, Genya realizes that the demon's manifestation of anger, Sekido, is behind him and is preparing to deliver a fatal blow. Unable to dodge the attack and believing he will die, he reminisces on his childhood, including the moment Sanemi had killed their mother and being rebuked by him in his grief. Genya tears up believeing he will never have the chance to apologize for denouncing him and his subsequent indifference towards him as a Demon Slayer.


Hashira Training Arc

In a remote forest, accompanied by two lower rank Slayers, Sanemi and Obanai stalk an abandoned castle, searching for a demon that had abducted a woman. Sanemi rebukes them for their lesser skill allowing the kidnapping to occur when Obanai spots a demon below their position. They see him carrying the missing woman and give chase. The four enter the castle but unbeknownst to them, it is crawling with numerous demons; one pounces on a Demon Slayer but it is quickly killed by Obanai, while another is beheaded by Sanemi. As the demons begin to attack, the Hashira are able to kill them but the weaker Demon Slayers struggle to fight back. Obanai orders them to retreat while he continues the mission with Sanemi; when one insists on staying to help, Sanemi dismisses them as hindrances and repeats the order, causing them to retreat.


Obanai notes that something is amiss and Sanemi confirms his suspicions; they have never seen demons collect in a swarm before. He then hears the woman cry for help and leaps onto an upper floor, housing a large horde of demons. Using Wind Breathing, Second Form: Claws - Purifying Wind, Sanemi eviscerates the horde in one ripping attack. More demons converge on him when Obanai then appears, performing Serpent Breathing, Fifth Form: Slithering Serpent and beheading them in a single, fluid slash. Sanemi expresses annoyance at his assistance when they see the kidnapper holding the woman. The demon flees upon realizing his pursuers are the Hashira as Sanemi demands his surrender, killing the demons in his path. They follow the kidnapper to outside the castle and are surrounded by more demons. Wondering where they are coming from, Sanemi allows Obanai to take over as he goes for the woman, killing several demons with Serpent Breathing. Sanemi corners the kidnapper and notices him surrounded by lesser demons. The demons attack but Sanemi kills them all with Wind Breathing: Fourth Form: Rising Dust Storm, showering himself in blood.


The kidnapper then surrenders the woman by throwing her off the roof but she is caught by Obanai. The Hashira chase the demon as he falls back into the castle, but when they leap inside, they instead see a vast, endless space of wooden halls and lamp-lit rooms, crawling with even more demons. The Infinity Castle then collapes in on itself as they fall inside; Sanemi attempts to kill the kidnapper but only manages to stab the ground as the alternate dimension vanishes. Obanai questions where the demons are now but Sanemi crudely responds that he is as unsure as he is.


Sanemi is present at the Hashira Meeting held at the Ubuyashiki Estate in the aftermath of the attack on the Swordsmith Village alongside the other remaining Hashiras. When the Love Hashira Mitsuri Kanroji attempts to explain how she got the mark he looks on at her in annoyance as she cannot properly explain the phenomenon but when the Mist Hashira Muichiro Tokito describes how he felt when the mark appeared on him, Sanemi scoffs and says it is quite simple to achieve, though Giyu Tomioka rebuffs him, much to his anger. After it is decided that there will be a giant training session amongst all the members of the Demon Slayer Corps to help as many Demon Slayers to manifest their Demon Slayer Marks as possible, Sanemi is excited at the prospect. However, he gets into an argument with Giyu who refuses to engage with the other Hashiras due to his long-standing belief that he was unworthy of his position. Sanemi is unable to read Giyu's feelings due to him not explaining them and assures that the Water Hashira is being arrogant. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 129. [7] Giyu attempted to leave, making the Wind Hashira angrier, but before anything broke out, Gyomei cowed the Hashira into silence with a powerful clap, and claimed that he had a proposition.


Sometime later during this meeting, the topic of Hashira bodyguards for Kagaya was raised, by Sanemi but was quickly shut down, due to Gyomei pointing out that Kagaya refused to have the Hashira serve as bodyguards, claiming they were too precious to expend on him alone. Shinobu adds in that the Ubuyashiki's haven't had bodyguards for generations, leaving the Hashira concerned as Sanemi scoffs. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga and Anime: Chapter 138 and Episode 1. [8] Kimetsu no Yaiba Anime: Episode 63. [9]


Sanemi's section of Hashira Training was known as Infinite Striking Training; a beatdown session where Sanemi relentlessly attacks the trainees to build resilience, with no resting until participants either vomit or faint. He was the most impatient with his trainees, frequently degrading and dismissing them as useless. At some point, he called upon Obanai for a private meeting; Sanemi agrees with his notion that their trainees will not be useful in battle against the Upper Ranks. Despite their woes, they know it is Kagaya's will. Obanai then asks why he had called him at this time and their conversation turns to their discovery of the Infinity Castle. Sanemi mentions that he had investigated the castle several times to no avail. Obanai adds that at the same time, demon activity in the surrounding city has diminished, which Sanemi reasons is proof they are targeting Nezuko. Obanai said that the lessened activity is an omen of misfortune for the Corps, though Sanemi disregards his unease by claiming he will be the one to end it all.


When Obanai remarks that he believes that the relative peace is the calm that will be followed by a major storm, Sanemi dismisses his premonition, believing it is futile to be certain of the future. Afterwards, Obanai then claims he has a suggestion for him, Sanemi interjects, saying he will state his suggestion first. He challenges the Serpent Hashira to fight him, to which Obanai remarks that he had coincidentally been thinking the same. They eventually made sparring sessions a regular nightly activity in their free time. Kimetsu no Yaiba Anime: Episode 58. [10]


A couple of days later, Sanemi and Obanai were engaging in their nightly sparring session, although the Hashira surmised that they were evenly amtched. They are then joined by Muichiro, who asks that he be allowed to join them. Sanemi, noticing he has been coming to them every night, asks about how his training has been to which the Mist Hashira responds that while training is enough for the other Corps members, it is insufficient for him. Sanemi laughs at his similar experience and remarks that for him and Obanai, true Hashira Training is sparring with one another. Obanai and Sanemi then agree to spar with Muichiro, who jokingly complains about the unfairness of fighting two on one.


With his Mist Breathing, he rushes past Obanai's attempt to strike, before he maneuvers around Sanemi to attack him. The Wind Hashira bends back to evade his blade but narrowly misses his own slash at Muichiro, who then swings at him from the side. Sanemi reacts by blocking his blade with his foot, using it to launch himself away as Obanai lunges at the Mist Hashira, leading to an intense skirmish that envelopes the courtyard with dust clouds and debris. From nearby, Muichiro's Kasugai Crow, Ginko, encourages him to beat the two Hashira, to which Sanemi angrily responds by aiming an attack at her. After the clash, Sanemi reflects on what he believes was a difficult training session and says that had either of them lost focus for a second, they would have gotten injured. Obanai concurs with him, and Sanemi reasons that training the Corps members during the day and performing combat training at night will be enough to prepare them. When Obanai asks Muichiro about how his training went, he replies that Tanjiro had come to his place, surprising both Hashiras, given their dislike of him. After Muichiro claims that he should be reaching their section of training soon, Sanemi expressed a desire to humble Tanjiro to prove that he is still no match for a Hashira no matter how much more powerful he's gotten. Kimetsu no Yaiba Anime: Episode 59. [11]


Sanemi's strict and abusive behavior terrified Zenitsu Agatsuma to the point where the young Demon Slayer ran away from his training, only to be stopped by Tanjiro Kamado's arrival. He attempts to explain his situation to Tanjiro, but is stopped by Sanemi who grabs him by the head, and gives Zenitsu the ultimatum of either returning to training or dying. Zenitsu grabs onto Tanjiro and pleads for mercy, only to be chopped in the neck and knocked unconscious by Sanemi who then orders Tanjiro to carry him back. On route to his dojo, Sanemi attempts to intimidate Tanjiro and says that he hasn't accepted the young Demon Slayer yet to which Tanjiro responds by saying that he has not accepted Sanemi either since he was the one who stabbed Nezuko. Watching as Tanjiro walks off, Sanemi stares at him before angrily remarking on his nerve to reply back. During his training, Sanemi ends up being the most rough and vindictive against Tanjiro, beating him so hard that the boy receives several bruises on his face.


During one of the few breaks, Tanjiro walks in on Genya Shinazugawa attempting to speak with his brother but Sanemi angrily rebukes him by saying he has no brother and to stop bothering him or else he would shred him to pieces. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 131. [12] He continues and says that Genya should leave the Corps as he is unable to use Breathing techniques and walks off. Genya is devastated at his brother's words and calls out to him, saying he had always wanted to apoplogize to him but Sanemi brushes his words aside, telling him to leave and that he did not care on what Genya had to say. When Genya then reveals that he overcame his lack of Breathing techniques by eating demons to fight, the Wind Hashira stops in his tracks, angrily demands he repeat himself, stomping down his foot, and frightening Genya and Tanjiro with his rage. He then vanishes, and as Genya grows confused at his sudden disappearance, Tanjiro bursts forth to reach him as Sanemi viciously attempts to gouge out his eyes. Tanjiro grabs Genya and pushes him away, causing a cut across his face from his fingers as the two punch through the wall.


Yelling at the Hashira's unseemly behavior towards his brother and a fellow member of the Corps, Tanjiro asks if he is trying to kill Genya to which Sanemi responds that that would be against the code of the Corps and so he was aiming to leave his brother incapable of regenerating, so that he would be forced to leave the Corps. But he relents that should Genya leave the Corps right then and there, he would finally forgive him and leave him unharmed. Tanjiro objects to this, saying Sanemi had no rights to do such things to his brother due to disowning him, and claiming that without Genya, they wouldn't have defeated an Upper Rank without him, and he will not let the Hashira harm him. Hearing this, Sanemi instead decides he will cripple Tanjiro first before taking a fighting stance. With tremendous speed, he lunges forward and punches Tanjiro in the chest, lifting him up and shocking Genya and Zenitsu. To his surprise, he finds the Demon Slayer has stopped his fist and kicks him in the head into letting go. The two drop to the floor and after Tanjiro orders Zenitsu to get Genya to safety, Genya warns him just as Sanemi attempts to kick him in the head. He narrowly misses, but his foot cuts across Tanjiro's ear. Sanemi claims that Tanjiro's actions must be part of his death wish, and he would be happy to kill him. The scuffle devolved into a large brawl of all the trainees attempting to stop Sanemi's rampage. This continued until the evening and Tanjiro received a scolding from the higher ups, in addition to being banned from approaching Sanemi, having to progress to Gyomei Himejima's training session instead. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 132. [13] Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 133. [14]


Tanjiro would complete Gyomei's training, and sometime before Tanjiro arrived at Giyu's residence, Sanemi and Giyu had engaged in a sparring session, which the young Demon Slayer witnessed firsthand. Unlike the ones he had with Obanai and Muichiro however, Sanemi was fueled by a vendetta against the Water Hashira instead of the camaraderie he had beforehand. Sanemi uses Wind Breathing, First Form: Dust Whirlwind Cutter, performing a fast rotating charge that chips Giyu's bokutō. Though Tanjiro is stunned by the Wind Hashira's speed, he soon realizes he is able to see and perceive their moves. As they do, Sanemi taunts the Water Hashira with his own words about being different to the other Hashira, leaving Tanjiro dismayed since he know the true reason behind his remarks. Giyu blocks his incoming blow and attempts to catch Sanemi with Water Breathing, Fourth Form: Striking Tide, trying to follow his movements. His opponent derides him for being too slow as he goes for an attack; Giyu uses Seventh Form: Drop Ripple Thrust to meet Sanemi's Fifth Form: Cold Mountain Wind, and the resulting clash destroys both of their blades.


Sanemi then enthusiastically proclaims they can now kill each other with their bare hands, only for Tanjiro to jump in and stop him. The Hashira angrily reminds the Demon Slayer of the restraining order and accuses him of spying on them, only for Tanjiro to ask if they are fighting over ohagi, claiming he can make enough for them both. Provoked, Sanemi demands to know if he is joking but Tanjiro assures him he is not. He mentions smelling the scent of mochi and red bean paste at his residence during training, as well as the scent of green tea whenever he appeared. As Giyu asks Sanemi if he does indeed like ohagi, Tanjiro tries to engage him with small talk, asking if he enjoys it coarse or fine and mentioning that he enjoys his grandmother's recipe, only to be punched in the face by the embarrassed Wind Hashira. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 136 (Pages 6-13). [15]


During the late evening, Sanemi descends down a set of stairs by himself as he bemoans the absense of both Obanai and Muichiro, claiming if he only trains with lesser-skilled Demon Slayers, he will grow rusty. Recalling Tanjiro's earlier remarks, Sanemi grows more annoyed as he complains about his interruption of his spar, cursing him out loud. As he continues grumbling about the Demon Slayer's behavior, he stops in his tracks to grab at something moving behind him. He opens his palm to find a demonic eye marked with the daiji for 4, sporadically twitching before it burns to ask, causing him to realize that the Corps has been infiltrated. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 136 (Pages 15-17). [16] Kimetsu no Yaiba Anime: Episode 62. [17]


Guided by his Kasugai Crow Sorai, Sanemi rushes to the Ubuyashiki Mansion in a desperate attempt to save Kagaya from an intruder. As he reaches near the Ubuyashiki Mansion, Sanemi remains hopeful that he can reach it in time, but is left horrified when the building is suddenly engulfed in a fiery explosion, knowing that Kagaya and Amane were inside and most certainly perished. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 138 (Pages 2-4). [18] Despite this, Sanemi presses forward where he encounters Gyomei fighting a shirtless, black-haired demon surrounded by thorned tendrils. Not knowing that Kagaya, his wife, and two of his elder daughters participated in a suicidal play to ambush Muzan, Sanemi furiously asks the intruder what happened to Kagaya. Following Sanemi, the rest of the current Hashira and Tanjiro who accompanied Giyu joined the scene.


Gyomei quickly announced that the demon was Muzan Kibutsuji himself, whilst also revealing that he would not die if beheaded. After getting over the initial shock of finally encountering the King of Demons in person, Sanemi quickly sprung into action and was about to use Seventh Form but before he could even swing his sword, Muzan opened the Infinity Castle, forcing Sanemi and the rest of the Hashira and Tanjiro to plummet inside. Sanemi ends up falling by himself, and he growls in frustration at the dramatic turn of events. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 139. [19] Kimetsu no Yaiba Anime: Episode 63. [9]




Infinity Castle Arc

Sitting by himself in the Infinity Castle, Sanemi mourns Kagaya's death and laments his inability to save his mentor and friend. As a group of demons attempt to attack him, Sanemi effortlessly kills them all with a single backhand swing, before getting on his feet and vowing to kill more demons, with a smile on his face, and tears in his eyes. The Wind Hashira then went on a one-man rampage slaughtering any demons in his path, furiously demanding that he fight an Upper Rank.


Later on he encountered Upper Rank 1, Kokushibo. The demon had not only severed Muichiro's hand and pinned him to a podium with his own sword, but had just sliced off Genya's arms before cutting him in half horizontally. As Kokushibo readied his sword to decapitate Genya, as he figured out Genya's pseudo-demon biology, Sanemi deflects the demon's Moon Breathing using Fourth Form: Rising Dust Storm. Kokushibo identifies Sanemi as the Wind Hashira, to which Sanemi then turns his attention towards his brother, berating him before proclaiming that the reason why he killed their mother was to protect him, before ranting that Genya should've married, had children, and lived well into his old age. As Kokushibo remarks of the nostalgia of Demon Slayer brothers, Sanemi furiously yells at the demon for dicing up his brother, calling it an unforgivable act. He then dodges an attack by Kokushibo and responds using First Form: Dust Whirlwind Cutter. As their blades meet, Sanemi draws attention to Kokushibo's multi-eyed flesh sword and insults it.
