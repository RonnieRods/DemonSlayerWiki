Quick Answers

What event led to Shinobu Kocho becoming a Hashira?
The transition of Shinobu Kocho to the role of Insect Hashira was triggered by the demise of her elder sister, Kanae Kocho. Kanae, the previous Flower Hashira and a Demon Slayer, was slain by Doma, an Upper Rank Two demon. This tragic event propelled Shinobu into Hashira Training and her subsequent position.
Who saved Shinobu and her sister from a demon attack?
The person who saved Shinobu Kocho and her sister Kanae from a demon attack was Gyomei Himejima. He arrived in time to defeat the demon that had killed their parents.
Who is responsible for Shinobu's death in the manga?
In the manga, Shinobu Kocho is killed by Doma, who absorbs her. Anticipating this, she had been ingesting wisteria poison for over a year, with Tamayo's assistance during Hashira Training, to weaken Doma if he consumed her. Her death is later avenged by Kanao Tsuyuri and Inosuke Hashibira.
What is Shinobu's role within the Hashira?
As a Hashira in the Demon Slayer Corps, Shinobu Kocho is highly skilled and powerful. She employs a unique fighting style, using sword thrusts and piercing techniques to inject poison into demons. Despite lacking the physical strength typical of a Demon Slayer, Shinobu's creativity and ingenuity allows her to be a formidable fighter. Her swift and powerful sword thrusts can penetrate boulders and surpass the speed of the Water Breathing's Drop Ripple Thrust form.

History

Past

Shinobu was born as the youngest daughter within the Kocho household, living alongside her mother, father, and older sister Kanae Kocho. Her parents worked in compounding medicine, a reason why Shinobu is also very talented with medicines. Kimetsu no Yaiba First Fanbook. [1] She was raised in a financially stable, loving and caring household, until her parents were slaughtered by demons right before her and her sister's eyes.


Just when the demon was about to take the lives of her and her sister as well, they were saved by the Hashira, Gyomei Himejima, who slayed the demon. Now more determined as a result of the pain and suffering they endured, Shinobu and Kanae both promised to become Demon Slayers to protect each other, as well as prevent other people from enduring the same losses they had to experience. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 143 (Pages 1-3). [2] Shinobu later states that if a demon is slain, tens of lives would be saved. She mentioned that defeating an Upper Rank will save hundreds of lives. Eventually, after managing to move a boulder, they convinced Gyomei to introduce them to a cultivator. Kimetsu no Yaiba: One-Winged Butterfly , Chapter 1. [3]


Later in her life, while accompanying her sister Kanae on a trip, the sisters encountered a young girl being led away as a slave on a bridge. Intriged by her, Kanae took an interest and decided to take the girl back to the Butterfly Estate. Despite Shinobu's doubts about raising an emotionally stunted child, she purchased the girl, whom the sisters helped develop a name for, Kanao Tsuyuri, from her master and trained her within the Butterfly Mansion to become the next Tsuguko.


At the age of 14, Shinobu became the master of the Butterfly MAnsion in Kanae's place, since her sister died at age 17. She coddled the orphaned girls of the Butterfly Mansion as if they were her own little sisters. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Volume 19, Extra Pages. [4] They included one teenager who was too fearful of fighting despite being trained in Water Breathing, and 3 prepubescent girls. In addition to Kanao, Shinobu had at least 3 other girls train as her Tsuguko. However, all were killed by demons, further adding onto her hatred of demons and intense anger which she belied with a cheerful persona that she adopted following Kanae's death.


Giyu Tomioka's Story: Part 1

Upon becoming a Hashira, she frequently spoke with the stoic and introverted Water Hashira, Giyu Tomioka, whom she liked to tease. She accompanied Giyu on a mission where they went on a search for another demon in the snow mountains that reportedly killed multiple people. Shinobu arrived first and made small-talk with the locals, where she was informed of a man eating bear, much to her intrigue. They then encountered an irritable young woman named Yae, a matagi out for revenge against the aforementioned bear who had supposedly killed her father and his matagi friend. Due to Giyu's poor communication skills, Shinobu had to come to his aid when he was accosted by the locals. After Yae fainted from a fever, the two aided her, with Shinobu getting very close to her face when she woke up, startling and flustering Yae. Shinobu then gave the woman some tea, and revealed the existence of demons and the Demon Slayer Corps, inquiring information about the bear that attacked her home to confirm that it was a bear and not a demon. When Yae asked if they killed all demons, she responded claiming that they only kill the bad ones, and that if a demon chose to die of starvation, she'd care for it till the very end. She offered to help Yae kill the bear, but she angrily rejected her offer and stormed off into the mountains once again. Afterwards, Shinobu changed into her Hashira attire and confided with Giyu that Yae was in trouble.


Giyu Tomioka's Story: Part 2

The bear turned out to have been a fabricated story by Yae, and the only casualty was her matagi friend, who was killed by her father, Matazo, who transformed into a demon. The two Hashira were able to save the woman before she was killed by her demonized father, and after a brief skrimish, Giyu was able to behead Matazo. Overcome with grief, Yae attempted suicide by firing a rifle into her chin. Shinobu attempted to intervene but noted that she wouldn't reach in time. Fortunately, the rifle jammed and Shinobu tackled Yae into the ground. Shinobu then gave the woman words of comfort and told her to be strong the way they once were, before silently departing. The Hashira then made a rendezvous at a noodle shop, where she claimed that Giyu had changed and was less cold than before. After Giyu is served salmon and radish, he begins to smile, much to her discomfort.


Synopsis

Mount Natagumo Arc

Shinobu is present when Kagaya Ubuyashiki receives news of the situation unfolding at Mount Natagumo, and is given the order to travel there and assist the lower-ranked swordsmen. Acknowledging this, she states that it would be great if everyone could get along, regardless if they are human or demon, and asks Giyu if he agrees. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 28 (Pages 18-19). [5]


On Mount Natagumo, Shinobu appears before a heavily injured Zenitsu Agatsuma, greeting and asking him if he is alright. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 28 (Pages 18-19). [6] She then suddenly appears behind the daughter member of Rui's Spider Family, inquiring if the strings came out of her palms, wishing the creature a good evening, and commenting on the beauty of the moon that night. Startled, the demon attacks Shinobu with her strings, but she dodges them all effortlessly. Drawing her sword, Shinobu dryly comments that this attack means that the demon doesn't want to get along with her. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 41 (Pages 8-10). [7]


The daughter demon quickly begs Shinobu for mercy, stating that she would be killed if she disobeyed Rui, Shinobu, sympathizing with the creature's plight, says that she will cooperate with the demon if she answers a few of Shinobu's questions, the first of which being how many people she had killed. The demon's answer is five, but Shinobu assures her that she doesn't have to lie, complimenting her string technique from eariler, and the demon corrects her answer to eighty humans. Despite the creature's stubborn denial, the swordswoman notes that both of them came from the west side of the mountain, and, on that side, she had found forty of the demon's cocoons. Shinobu adds that she isn't angry, but only trying to be as accurate as possible. When asked what the point of checking this information is, Shinobu tells the elder daughter demon that in order for them to be friends and to satisfy those who have died, she will have to endure punishment, explaining in gruesome detail what she had in mind for this, and adding that the creature won't die from it, so there will be no aftereffects. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 41 (Pages 10-15). [8]


Enraged, the demon attacks Shinobu again, but the Demon Slayer quickly bypasses her, simultaneously landing her Insect Breathing, Butterfly Dance: Caprice attack on the creature. She laments the demon's poor choice, turning and subsequently watching as the creature collapses. Shinobu tells the creature not to let her guard down just because she can't cut the demon's head off since, she explains, that she utilizes poison as her weapon instead. Shinobu then introduces herself as the Insect Hashira of the Demon Slayer Corps, stating that, although she may be the only one among her order to not possess the ability to cut off a demon's head, poison that can kill the creatures is still a potent tool. She then apologizes, realizing that the daughter demon is already dead and could not hear her explanation. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 41 (Pages 15-19). [9]


Shinobu then proceeds ot cut open the demon's silk cocoon, freeing Murata. She asks him if he is alright, and he asks what happened to the older sister demon, to which she answers that she had killed the demon with poison from wisteria, commenting afterwards on how lucky he is for only having his clothes melted off by the creature's attack. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 42 (Pages 2-3). [10]


She later attempts to attack Tanjiro Kamado and Nezuko Kamado, but her attack is deflected by Giyu. She questions his interference, noting that if he continues this course of action, everyone would hate him. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 43 (Pages 18-19). [11] The Insect Hashira asks her fellow Demon Slayer to get out of the way, apologizing to him when he replies that he isn't hated. She then turns her attention to Tanjiro, telling him that he is protecting a demon and that he should get away from her. The young Demon Slayer exclaims that Nezuko is his sister, causing Shinobu to take pity on her and plan to kill her with a toxin that inflicts no pain. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 44 (Pages 4-6). [12]


Giyu then instructs Tanjiro to run, which Shinobu notes to be a violation of the Demon Slayer's conduct. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 44 (Page 7). [13] Later, while being restrained by Giyu's arm, she laments that she had been in the right to come kill demons and therefore had broken no rules, but noted that Giyu had, asking him what his intent was. Her fellow Hashira remains quiet, prompting her to angrily ask him to say something. Giyu replies that it had all started two years ago, causing Shinobu to note that the length of time was a problem and wonder if he was holding a grudge because she had said he was hated. She then extends a blade from her right shoe and prepares to attack Giyu, only to be stopped by an important message from a crow which said that Tanjiro and Nezuko are to be restrained and brought to headquarters. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 44 (Pages 15-19). [14] Later, at the headquarters of her order, the Demon Slayer stood alongside her fellow Hashira in front of a restrained Tanjiro. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 44 (Page 22). [15]

Mugen Train Arc

Shinobu and Kanao were informed by a Kasugai Crow of the death of Kyojuro Rengoku. Like all the Hashira, Shinobu was fond of Kyojuro and as such was troubled by his death.


Hashira Training Arc

After Kanao came back from a mission, she was greeted by Shinobu who informed her of Tanjiro's mission in the Swordsmith Village, as well as Nezuko gaining immunity to the sunlight. She then attends a meeting of Hashira in the Ubuyashiki Mansion attended by Amane Ubuyashiki, who was filling in for her husband due to Kagaya's familiar curse leaving him bed-ridden. She listened as Amane informed them of the Demon Slayer Marks. After the meeting concluded, Giyu attempted to immediately dismiss himself, much to the ire of Sanemi and Obanai. In response, Shinobu, uncharacteristically stern, asked Giyu to explain himself, causing him to respond like he wasn't like the rest of them. Before the situation could escalate any further, Gyomei made a powerful clap to cow them into silence, and proclaimed he had a proposition.


The plan was to. instead of solely training their Tsugukos, a mass Hashira Training program would be exercised in order to both improve all Demon Slayers' physical abilities and overall health, bolstering the members of the Demon Slayer Corps significantly, and to improve the individual strength of each Hashira as well as practice coordinating their attacks together. Sometime during this meeting, the topic of Hashira bodyguards for Kagaya was raised, but was quickly shut down, due to Gyomei pointing out that Kagaya refused to have the Hashira serve as bodyguards, claiming they were too precious to expend on him alone. Shinobu pointed out that the Ubuyashiki's haven't had bodyguards for generations.


Like Giyu, Shinobu did not participate in Hashira Training, although unlike Giyu, she made it clear that she was in full support of the proposition. Instead she was tasked with collaborating with a demon doctor named Tamayo to come up with a plan to defeat Muzan Kibutsuji by Kagaya. Due to her extreme hatred of demons, she was rather hesitant and mistrustful about it and asked her late sister for help to suppress her emotions. She is later approached by Kanao who announces that she is leaving to go train with the Wind Hashira before asking if she could train her after completing her section with the Stone Hashira. Shinobu responds that she's not participating in the training, and when Kanao asks why, Shinobu invites her in for a private conversation. Kanao meekly admits her desire to train with her older adopted sister, causing Shinobu to smile warmly and express happiness that Kanao is learning to speak what's on her mind more clearly. She then decides that now is the right time to reveal the demon that killed Kanae and how to defeat it.


When Tamayo and her demon assistant Yushiro arrived, relations were tense. Shinobu was highly paranoid and inwardly hostile to the demons, and the notoriously overprotective Yushiro could sense it, causing him to be on edge, much to Tamayo's unease. However, they learned to work past their differences and created an Anti-Kibutsuji Drug. When she was alerted by a Kasugai Crow that an intruder had trespassed into the Ubuyashiki Mansion, she ran to the aid of her master and his family as fast as she could, alongside her fellow Hashira. However, before they could arrive, Kagaya detonated a large amount of explosives, not only decimating the Ubuyashiki Mansion, but killing both himself, and his wife along with his two elder daughters, who all refused to leave his side.


Shinobu looked on in horror but pressed forward, where she encountered Gyomei fighting a shirtless, black-haired demon. Gyomei quickly announced that the demon was Muzan Kibutsuji himself, whilst also revealing that he would not die if beheaded. After getting over the initial shock of finally encountering the King of Demons in person, Shinobu quickly sprung to action and was about to use Butterfly Dance, but before she could swing her sword, Muzan opened the Infinity Castle, forcing Shinobu and the rest of the Hashira and Tanjiro to plummet inside.


Infinity Castle Arc

Inside the Infinity Castle that Muzan Kibutsuji had created after Kagaya's suicidal move, Shinobu enters a room and discovers Doma eating a group of his own female disciples. When he finally notices her, Doma reacts with delight at having another meal walk right into his room, and thanks Nakime. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 140 (Pages 18-20). [30] One of his disciples, still alive, begs Shinobu to save her, and Doma, irritated by this, attempts to finish her off, only for Shinobu to jump in and save her. She asks the young woman if she is alright, but before she can answer Shinobu's question, the young woman explodes and dies right in front of her eyes. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 141 (Pages 2-6). [31]


Angered by this, and Doma's insistence that he saved the girl by killing her, Shinobu notices a pair of sharp fans that Doma uses as his main weapon, and angrily inquires if he remembers the haori that originally belonged to her elder sister. Dome finally remembers her by the butterfly wing design haori that Kanae once wore, and playfully expresses his disappointment at not being able to eat Kanae due to the sun rising, Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 141 (Pages 12-13). [32] which further angers Shinobu. The Insect Hashira swiftly launches an attack at him using her Insect Breathing, Dance of the Bee Sting: True Flutter to inflict a powerful stab directly in Doma's right eyeball, which impresses him since he couldn't block it. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 141 (Pages 11-14). [33]


Although he is initially dismissive of her, since he correctly guessed that she lacks the physical strength to cut off a Demon's head, he becomes excited upon learning that Shinobu's tru strength lies in poisoning her opponents. Shinobu hopes that the poison will be effective enough to kill him, but then learns, much to her disappointment, that Doma had already received information regarding her poison from her battle against the Spider Family of Lower Rank Five. Doma manages to break down and render her poison non-lethal, healing himself from her attack. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 141 (Pages 15-18). [34] She is further backed into a corner due to Doma now rapidly developing antibodies and adjusting his body against her poison so that, after a mere five slashes and injections of poison, he is now entirely resistant to it. Also, due to his Blood Demon Art of frozen mist, it is becoming increasingly difficult for Shinobu to breathe, due to the necrosis in her lungs and alveoli, as both parties note. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 142 (Pages 4-5). [35]


Shinobu's nose begins to bleed, which she wipes off. Now hedging her bets on injecting a large dose to kill him by using a six stab attack, Shinobu strikes him using Insect Breathing, Dance of the Dragonfly: Compound Eye Hexagon and successfully hits him. However, as she attacks him, he simultaneously attacks her, cutting both her collarbone and ribs as well as puncturing one of her lungs. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 142 (Pages 6-7). [36] Finally near her wits' end and almost losing any hope of victory, Shinobu attempts to draw strength from replaying her older sister's words of encouragement in her head.


Shinobu then prepares to strike at Doma again, despite her serious injuries and the immense pain she was in, and attacks him using Insect Breathing, Dance of the Centipede: Hundred-Legged Zigzag, to confuse him. Doma is shocked at this feat, noting the destroyed bridge from the impact of her feet; he tries to slash her in response, but is unable to make contact, only managing to slice her haori. She strikes Doma directly through his neck, impaling him and sending him flying towards the ceiling, break the ceiling in the process. Exhausted and wounded, Shinobu is triumphant upon seeing Doma being poisoned and seemingly dying from her attack. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 142 (Pages 8-17). [37]


However, Doma recovers almost instantly from her attack, by which Shinobu is further enraged. Now exhausted, fatally injured, and dangerously near death, Shinobu is caught by Doma as they fell down from the ceiling. He embraces her in a bear hug and begins mocking her and calling her entire attempt to defeat him a wasted effor, assuring her she will live peacefully as a part of him. He playfully asks for her last words, and she spitefully curses him. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 143 (Pages 1-5). [38]


As her disciple Kanao arrives, Shinobu passes her knowledge of Doma's abilities through hand signals, but the demon notices and crushes her spine, killing her. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 143 (Pages 6-8). [39] Her death causes Kanao to go berserk, but she is repelled by an irritated Doma, who tells her not to interfere when he's in the middle of devouring someone. He successfully absorbs Shinobu's body into himself, leaving only her butterfly-shaped hair piece and sword behind. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 143 (Pages 9-16). [40]


Shinobu's death was reported to Tanjiro and Giyu by a Kasugai Crow, with both being very shaken up by the news. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 144 (Pages 3-4). [41] After Kanao, later joined by Inosuke Hashibira, fight to avenge Shinobu's death, it turns out that she anticipated the possibility she would lose and be consumed by Doma. Thus, she had been taking and absorbing wisteria poison regularly for over a year, with Tamayo helping the process along during the Hashira training, in the hopes that if Doma consumed her, he would also devour thirty-seven kilograms worth of wisteria poison, nearly seven-hundred times the lethal dose for a normal demon. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Volume 19. [42]


She hoped this would be enough to kill the Upper Rank or at least weaken him enough that her allies could slay him later. Ultimately, Shinobu's plan works out, as Kanao and Inosuke manage to sever Doma's disfigured head from his body, fulfilling her revenge at last. In the afterlife, Shinobu and Doma converse with each other, with Doma asking her if her name is Shinobu or Kanae, and she says that it doesn't really matter because he makes her sick and she doesn't want him to say her name anyway. Doma going on to compliment her for her poisoning skills and express his surprise at not noticing it had circulated through him before it was too late, and she smiles and explains that a Demon called Tamayo helped her make it.


Shinobu says she is frustrated that she couldn't kill him herself and the fact that she can no longer help the other Demon Slayers defeat Muzan, but that she is still satisfied with the result, and that one of her comrades will defeat him. Doma gives her a look, and Shinobu asks what's wrong, to which the Demon says that he feels like his heart is beating and wonders if he is in love with Shinobu. He then proposes that they go to hell together, but Shinobu vulgarly declines with a smile on her face. Shinobu is last seen with her sister, Kanae, giving their blessings to Kanao, before happily reuniting with their parents in the afterlife. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 163 (Pages 5-8). [43]


Legacy

When Tanjiro was turned into a demon after the defeat of the Demon King, Muzan Kibutsuji, she was among the many hands to push him back towards being a human again alongside many of her colleagues whom had also fallen in battle, when the spirit of Muzan attempted to sabotage the serum that was administered to cure Tanjiro's demonization.


In the modern era, Shinobu reincarnates with her sister Kanae as high school girls, both of whom are seen attending an all girl's school.
