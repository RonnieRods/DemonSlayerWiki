Quick Answers

What is the nature of the relationship between Giyu and Shinobu?
As Hashiras of the Demon Slayer Corps, Shinobu Kocho and Giyu Tomioka are paired for missions. Despite Shinobu's perception of Giyu as taciturn and aloof, they find common ground in their duties as Demon Slayers.
How do Shinobu and Kanao interact within the Demon Slayer Corps?
Shinobu Kocho and Kanao Tsuyuri, as adoptive sisters, share a deep familial connection. They became part of the Demon Slayer Corps following the death of their parents a the hands of a demon. Shinobu also has a nuanced relationship with Giyu Tomioka, her fellow Hashira in the Demon Slayer Corps. Despite their contrasting personalities and initial conflicts, they frequently undertake missions together and maintain a cordial interaction.
Is there any romantic interest between Shinobu and Giyu?
Shinobu Kocho and Giyu Tomoika, both Hashiras in the Demon Slayer Corps, frequently collaborate on missions. They initially have misunderstandings due to their contrasting personalities, but eventually develop mutual respect. Shinobu desires more communication from Giyu, who in turn admires her diligence and expresses concern for her wellbeing. Both show concern for the Kamado siblings. No explicit romantic interest is indicated between them.
How does Shinobu's relationship with Tanjiro evolve?
Shinobu Kocho's bond with Tanjiro develops from initial confusion to mutual respect. She mentors Tanjiro during his recovery, initially puzzled by his determination to safeguard his Demon sister, but ultimately grows fond of him. Shinobu finds Tanjiro less vexing than Zenitsu and Inosuke, as he is serious about his training from the outset.
What are the conflicts between Giyu and Shinobu?
Shinobu Kocho and Giyu Tomioka, both Hashiras in the Demon Slayer Corps, often conflict due to their differing personalities. Shinobu views Giyu as absent-minded and clumsy, leading to misunderstandings during their joint missions. This tension escalates when Shinobu becomes frustrated with Giyu for interfering with her attack during a mission at Natagumo Mountain.

Relationships

Kanae Kocho

Shinobu was noted to have a very close and loving sisterly relationship with her biological older sister, Kanae having been seen to be very close to each other since early childhood and having spent their childhoods happily together with their parents. Their sisterly bond was increasingly deep and caring, as seen where the two of them both took an oath to protect others from demons and ensure no one else goes through their tragedy upon the two of them surviving the Demon attack that killed their parents.


The sisters developed a unique familial dynamic, where Kanae would frequently make lighthearted, whimsical decisions without thinking first, which then causes Shinobu great expiration and worry due to her elder sister's behavior. But Shinobu is still shown to trust her sister deeply and support her sister's decision despite not initially agreeing with her at first, as seen when the two adopted and made off with their adoptive little sister Kanao Tsuyuri.


Kanae's deep love for her sister was truly shown during her last moments after fighting Doma, where Kanae begged Shinobu to leave the Demon Slayer Corps and simply live her life free and happy, stating that the only thing that would make her happy is knowing that Shinobu would be able to live a full happy life and die peacefully as an old woman, showing just how much she loved her younger sister. Shinobu in turn showed her own immense love for her elder sister by swearing that she couldn't possibly live like that and begged her sister to tell her which demon killed her, swearing eternal vengeance against whoever killed her sister.


Kanao Tsuyuri

Although Shinobu was the one to impulsively buy her adoptive sister Kanao out of slavery, she quickly became frustrated when Kanao could not make her own decisions, declaring that she was dangerous to herself and others. However, Shinobu soon warmed up to her and developed a strong, sisterly bond. During Kanae's funeral, Shinobu comforted Kanao and was understanding of her inability to cry.


Shinobu eventually made Kanao her chosen Tsuguko (successor) due to her considerable talent and exceptional skills as a Demon Slayer. Shinobu instructed Kanao to cut off a demon's head without thinking, though she was worried whether or not Kanao was truly following her own will. But when Kanao begins opening up and expresses her wish to train beside her specifically, Shinobu was very happy.


Shinobu holds great trust in Kanao, as she entrusted her with avenging Kanae's death while using herself as a sacrifice. She is also noted to be protective towards Kanao; Inosuke mentions that Shinobu gets "really mad" when Kanao gets hurt, and Shinobu expresses her worry about Kanao using the Flower Breathing's Final Form and thus possibly losing her sight, even when she was the one losing her life. After Shinobu died at the hands of Doma, a furious Kanao successfully avenged her sisters with the help of Inosuke. In the aftermath, the spirits of Shinobu and Kanae gave their blessings to their younger sister before departing to the afterlife. This event causes Kanao to break down in tears for the first time. Kanao also fished through the water to find Shinobu's hairpin as a keepsake of her departed sister and continues holding her in high regard.


Aoi Kanzaki

Shinobu is Aoi's mentor in the field of medicine and likely oversaw some or all of her demon hunter training as well. Aoi thinks highly of Shinobu, like all the other girls in the Butterfly Mansion. Shinobu cares for Aoi and feels sympathy for how her family was killed by demons. She also seems to feel some anger on Aoi's behalf, remarking that none of the girls would have been killed if it weren't for demons during her fight with Doma. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 143. [1] Furthermore, Shinobu happen to have shown interacting with Kanao, and the Butterfly Girls in the flashbacks, when Tanjiro reminiscences of Shinobu, after receiving the news of her death in the battle against Doma. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 144 (Pages 3-4). [2]


Mitsuri Kanroji

Shinobu and Mitsuri get along quite well due to them having been the only two female Hashira after Kanae's death. Kimetsu no Yaiba First Fanbook. [3] However, Shinobu has expressed jealousy towards Mitsuri's strong body before. Kimetsu no Yaiba: One-Winged Butterfly, Chapter 3. [4] Nevertheless, they are good friends and have expressed a fondness for each other. Mitsuri sometimes teaches Shinobu western cooking. Kimetsu no Yaiba First Fanbook. [3]


Giyu Tomioka

Giyu and Shinobu are both Hashiras of the Demon Slayer Corps and they are usually assigned on missions together. They have opposite personalities and initially seem to be at odds with each other, as she appears to be under the impression that he is an airhead and a klutz, assuming he is spacing out in front of a demon as she is trying to save him. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Volume 5, Extra Pages. [5] After he deflects her attack, she is angry at him for getting in the way and proceeds to remark that people dislike him as a result. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Volume 5, Extra Pages. [5] However, she does wish he would talk more while he compliments her work ethic and hints at concern for her physical wellbeing. Furthermore, Gyomei notes that the two enjoy talking with each other. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: First Fanbook. [6] Later, both Shinobu and Giyu come to care about the Kamado siblings and are visibly upset by the hostile treatment of the other Hashiras towards them. When Giyu later hears about Shinobu's death, he is visibly shocked at the news and suddenly recalls her smiling face.


Tanjiro Kamado

Shinobu first meets Tanjiro after she attempts to kill Nezuko, and tells him it is dangerous to be so close to a Demon. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 44 (Page 5). [7] Later on, during the Hashira's trial, she is the only one trying to hear Tanjiro's side of the story, and has a sad expression on her face as Tanjiro desperately tries to explain that Nezuko has not and will not hurt anybody. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 45 (Page 12). [8] She comes to care about both Tanjiro and Nezuko and is visibly concerned about them, even letting them rest at her estate so they are able to recover from their injuries. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 48 (Page 2). [9] Tanjiro is visibly sad when he hears about Shinobu's death.




Nezuko Kamado

Shinobu initially assumes that since Nezuko was a demon she was a threat to others, though she decides to make her death swift and painless when she realizes that Tanjiro was her brother. She leaps out from afar and attempts to stab the demon girl with her sword, only to be blocked by Giyu. However, at the Hashira Meeting, she doesn't object to the master's decision to accept her into the Corp. Over time, Shinobu grows a bit fond of Nezuko and takes care of her in the estate. When Nezuko is turning back into a human by Tamayo's medicine, she remembers when Shinobu showed her goldfish to her.


Zenitsu Agatsuma

Shinobu meets Zenitsu during the Natagumo Mountain incident and treats his wounds after he has slain one of the spider demons. To motivate him to continue his recovery training, Shinobu cheers for the boy.


Inosuke Hashibira

Inosuke meets Shinobu while recovering at the Butterfly Estate. To motivate him to continue his recovery training, she condescendingly mocks his inability to do simple things, prompting him to angrily train harder. As time passed, she continued to care for Inosuke's injured state and he harbored affection for Shinobu, vowing to avenge her death when he learned that Doma absorbed her. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 159 (Pages 5-7). [10] Shinobu's sweet demeanor and use of a "pinky promise" with Inosuke inadvertently recalling deep subconscious memories of his mother. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 160 (Page 11). [11] It is also noted that Shinobu is one of few people whose names Inosuke never gets wrong.


Genya Shinazugawa

Although the two are never shown interacting, Genya mentions that Shinobu had examined his unique demon-consuming condition after being introduced by Gyomei. He gripes that she had given him sour looks and lectures, to which Tanjiro reassures that it's out of concern instead of disdain.


Murata

Murata first meets Shinobu at Natagumo Mountain when she rescues him from the Older Sister Spider. As she has seen Murata naked, he has been avoiding her since then.


Tamayo

Upon being requested and offered by Kagaya Ubuyashiki to help the Demon Slayer Corps to kill Muzan once and for all and Tamayo accepting his offer, she was immediately introduced and set to work with Shinobu due to her own immense skills in drugs and poisons working well with the Insect Hashira's own. Initially, there was little trust between the two, with Tamayo being initially scared of trusting a Demon Slayer, especially a Hashira due to her status as a demon, and Shinobu's own paranoia against her for being a demon. However, eventually through their work together in creating the humanization drug and the anti-Kibutsuji concoction, the two developed a genuine sense of admiration and respect for each other, with Tamayo being genuinely impressed by Shinobu's talent in poisons and Shinobu being touched by the demoness's attempts to make up for her past actions and her being impressed by the latter's immense skills as a doctor. It was also revealed that Tamayo had a hand in personally modifying Shinobu's body in preparation for her dual and eventual death against Doma in order to fulfill her vengeance for her older sister Kanae, showing just how much she had come to trust Tamayo; to the point of allowing her to personally alter and modify her body.


Doma

Shinobu holds a strong resentment and hatred for the Upper Rank Two Doma, due to him killing her older sister, Kanae. This hatred and disgust becomes even stronger when she actually meets him and comes to understand and see first-hand his twisted personality and ideas about giving his female disciples "salvation" by killing and eating them. Later, during their battle, she continuously curses him due to him not being affected by her poisons and sword techniques again and again, until she is defeated and devoured by him. However, thanks to Shinobu's body being heavily saturated with poison, Doma is severely weakened and is eventually and successfully slain by Kanao and Inosuke.


Inside of Limbo, Doma, having heard and been touched by Shinobu's strong faith and belief in her comrades and fellow Demon Slayers' ability to defeat the demons and slay Muzan, seemingnly begins to develop genuine feelings of romantic attraction towards her. He begins to doubt his initial belief that neither heaven or hell nor paradise exist, and happily asks her if she wanted to go to hell with him for a date, to which she responds with sharp rejection while having the usual kind and calm smile on her face as a final act of spite towards the Demon.
