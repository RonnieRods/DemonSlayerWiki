Shinobu Kocho (胡こ蝶ちょう しのぶ Kochō Shinobu) was a major supporting character of Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba. She was a Demon Slayer of the Demon Slayer Corps and the Insect Hashira (蟲むし柱ばしら Mushi Bashira) of the Taisho era. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 41 (Page 19). [2] Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 45 (Page 7). [3] She was also the younger sister of the former Flower Hashira Kanae Kocho, and the adoptive older sister of Kanao Tsuyuri.

Appearance

Shinobu was a petite, young woman of slender build and pale skin complexion, which sharply contrasts her pink lipstick. She had large eyes that lack pupils, only a haze of gradient purple that blends with a lighter purple to a darker purple at the bottom, along with white highlights on the upper irises, making her eyes appear similar to those of insects. Her eyes are framed with black eyelashes. She had raven-black wavy hair that fades into a dark purple hue at the ends, styled at the back of her head into a flat yakai-maki bun fastened with a white butterfly ornament with a light purple trim on the outer side of the wings, along with turquoise speckled spots. She had center-spli, ear-length, voluminous curtain bangs that flow down to frame the sides of her face, with two slightly thinner chin-length locks of hair in front of her ears below them. By the time of Hashira Training, both her bangs and locks are visibly more refined and slightly longer. She stood otu among the more physically imposing Hashira due to her diminutive stature and small frame, which was later reinforced by her mentioning that she does not possess enough physical strength to decapitate a demon as the others do. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 142 (Pages 9-10). [4]


Shinobu wore a dark purple-tinted variation of the standard Demon Slayer uniform. It consists of a button-up jacket with a stand-up collar and long sleeves, featuring gold buttons and a shirt pocket on the left breast, with the kanji for Destroy (滅めつ Metsu) engraved on the back of the uniform. Beneath the jacket, she wore a white long-sleeved collared undershirt. Her long, saggy tattsuke-hakama pants are also tinted dark purple and are fastened at the waist with a white belt. Her legs were wrapped with a pair of unique white kyahan with butterfly-patterned design that fades from pale turquoise to pink gradient around the lower legs, and tied securely with white ribbons. Her tabi socks match the dark purple color of her uniform.


Over her uniform, she wore a distinctive white haori that once belonged to her late sister, Kanae. The haori features a butterfly wing pattern inspired by the Monarch Butterfly, with a color gradient that fades from whtie into pale turquoise and pink on the lower sleeves and hem, with the cuffs and hem having a black trim, decorated with pale turquoise dots, resembling butterfly wing edges. For footwear, she wore white zōri sandals with purple straps, and she conceals a hidden blade in the right sandal—used as a last resort weapon.


When Shinobu was fourteen, her appearance was altered slightly. Her chin-length strands of hair in front of her ears were tucked in and she had a different Demon Slayer uniform consisting of silver buttons and cloth a darker shade of purple. She also sported a plain white haori over it.

Personality

Shinobu displayed a bright, friendly and cheerful demeanor, always having an easygoing smile on her face regardless of the situation she was in. However, her outward behavior was shown very quickly to be a façade. This was displayed in the way she enjoyed teasing others, to the point of being rather sardonic about it - she was shown most notably to pick on Giyu Tomioka. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 44 (Page 4). [5] Despite her relaxed exterior and her initial claim of wanting to get along with demons, she could be rather cruel towards them, which was seen when she killed Daughter after giving her false hope of helping her, and immediately attempting to kill Nezuko Kamado without giving her brother Tanjiro a chance to prove that she was harmless to humans. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 45 (Page 14). [6] Later on, she was shown to have a more compassionate side towards the Kamado siblings after hearing their full story. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 47 (Page 7). [7] Shinobu was shown to be unabashed, as she paid no heed to Murata's lack of clothes despite his own embarrassment. Kimetsu no Yaiba Anime: Episode 20. [8] Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 42 (Pages 2-3). [9] She also showed to be very strict when she needed to, as she scolded Inosuke when he broke the window, Kimetsu no Yaiba Anime: Episode 56. [10] Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 129 (Pages 15-19). [11] and burned revealing uniforms tailored by Masao Maeda, who intended them to be worn by her and other girls. Mitsuri's Uniform [12]


It is later revealed that Shinobu's outwardly calm, tranquil disposition was worn as a mask, as she held an intense hatred for demons due to the loss of her beloved older sister, Kanae at a young age, along with the deaths of her parents, almost all of her Tsuguko, and the families of her apprentices at the Butterfly Mansion. This hatred was so strong that she was in a near-constant state of extreme anger, Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 143 (Page 6). [13] which was sensed by Tanjiro when the two held a conversation. Her desire for revenge rendered her unable to live the normal life her elder sister had wished upon her, instead devoting the entirety of her energy to defeating demons. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 141 (Pages 2-3). [14]


Shinobu's younger self had a more brash, blunt and uncomplicated demeanor as seen when she rescued Kanao Tsuyuri, whom she had a negative attitude towards raising at first. After the death of Kanae, however, Shinobu began to outwardly behave more like her older sister and eventually developed her gentle, positive attitude and mannerisms to match while internally holding in her rage, which she later clarifies was fueled by her desire to continuously display the smile that her sister loved. Kimetsu no Yaiba Anime: Episode 24. [15] Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 50 (Pages 5-12). [16] Though never shown onscreen, she has a notable temper, namely in the way that makes even the loud and brash Inosuke Hashibira take notice. Kimetsu no Yaiba Anime: Episode 56. [10]


However, although she faked a cheerful and optimistic demeanor to hide her true vexations, Shinobu was genuinely compassionate to her fellow humans. After Kanae's death, Shinobu coddled all the orphaned girls in her care as if they were her own sisters, making all of them have a large adoration of her. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Volume 19, Extra Pages. [17] Kimetsu no Yaiba Movie: Infinity Castle - Part 1: Akaza Returns. [18] Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 144 (Pages 3-4). [19] In addition, despite her stubborn attempts to kill Nezuko, after being ordered to give the Kamado siblings a trial, Shinobu was willing to allow Tanjiro to defend himself in stark contrast to most of her colleagues who opted to execute them on the spot. Shinobu also aided Tanjiro by giving him water laced with anesthetic when he was in pain, and told Obanai that he was being too rough when he pinned Tanjiro to the ground with his elbow. Kimetsu no Yaiba Anime: Episode 22. [20] Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 45 (Pages 11-14). [21]


In addition, despite initially being irritated by Kanao's passiveness, Shinobu grew to have a more loving relationship with her adopted sister, Kanao after Kanae's death. She comforted Kanao during Kanae's funeral despite Kanao not shedding tears, being perfectly understanding of her reasoning for not expressing emotions, Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 163 (Pages 13-19). [22] and expresses concern for Kanao's well-being, as shown when she warned Kanao about Flower Breathing: Final Form's grievous effect. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 162 (Page 19). [23] Shinobu was also pleased when Kanao grew to be more expressive and talkative over time. She also showed great care for Inosuke, making him see her as a maternal figure and be subconsciously reminded of his own mother. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 160 (Page 11). [24] Moreover, after intercepting Yae's suicide after she witnessed her demonized father be dispatched by Giyu, who later makes a tasteless comment after trying to solace her, Shinobu, concerned for the girl's wellbeing, stays behind for a moment to comfort Yae with sincerity, despite Giyu telling her to remain on duty. Kimetsu no Yaiba Extra: Giyu Tomioka's Story: Part 2. [25]


During her battle against Doma, Shinobu also revealed that she suffers from an inferiority complex due to her inferior physical abilities compared to her fellow Hashira, having been jealous of them for possessing taller or more muscular bodies than her, and cursing her own body for not developing further to make her more effective in combat. Kimetsu no Yaiba Movie: Infinity Castle - Part 1: Akaza Returns. [18] Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 142 (Pages 9-10). [4]

Fighting Style

Master Swordswoman: As a Hashira of the Demon Slayer Corps, Shinobu was one of the most powerful and skilled swordswomen in the entire organization. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 45. [61] Her techniques were unique to her alone, but was as effective as any of the traditional swordsmanship styles. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 142 (Pages 9-10). [4] Her swordplay specialized in thrusting and piercing techniques in order to inject poisoin into demons. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 41 (Pages 16-19). [29] Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 42 (Pages 8-10). [32] Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 44 (Page 2). [30] Her sword thrusts are so fast and powerful that they can pierce boulders and even out-speed Water Breathing's Drop Ripple Thrust form, the fastest thrusting technique in that Breathing Style. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Volume 16, Extra Pages. [28] However, the structure of her sword made it ineffective for blocking attacks, so she made up for it with her swift thrusts and stabs. Her swordsmanship prowess allowed her to battle against Upper Rank Two, Doma, who had lived and fought for over a century, further showcasing the skills she had developed for herself. Kimetsu no Yaiba Movie: Infinity Castle - Part 1: Akaza Returns. [18] Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 141 (Pages 11-14). [33] Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 142 (Pages 6-7). [52]


Breathing Style

Insect Breathing (蟲むしの呼こ吸きゅう Mushi no kokyū): A Breathing Style created by Shinobu, who designed it to compensate for her lack of physical strength to cut off a demon's head. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 142 (Pages 9-10). [4] Combined with the lethality of her poison, this Style allowed her to defeat countless demons, and even battle Upper Rank Two, Doma.


Techniques

  • Butterfly Dance: Caprice (蝶ちょうノ舞まい   戯たわむれ Chō no mai: Tawamure) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga and Anime: Chapter 41 and Episode 20. [62] - The user leaps into the air and charges towards the opponent, stinging them multiple times with their blade to inject poison in just seemingly one strike.
  • Dance of the Bee Sting: True Flutter (蜂ほう牙がノ舞まい   真ま靡なびき Hōga no mai: Manabiki) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 141 (Page 14). [56] - The user dashes at their target at blinding speeds and uses the momentum of their speed to then strengthen a single thrust into their opponent and inject poison into them.
  • Dance of the Horsefly: Enticing Slash (虻もう咬かノ舞まい   切せっ裂さくの誘さそい Mōka no mai: Sessaku no Sasoi) Kimetsu no Yaiba Movie: Infinity Castle - Part 1: Akaza Returns. [18] - The user leaps off a vertical surface and delivers a forward thrust toward their opponent, before surrounding them with a condusing swarm of stabs from all sides. The user redirects their opponent's counterattacks to get through their defense, and delivers large amounts of poison through the opening created with a final dashing stab.
  • Dance of the Dragonfly: Compound Eye Hexagon (蜻せい蛉れいノ舞まい   複ふく眼がん六ろっ角かく Seirei no mai: Fukugan Rokkaku) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 142 (Pages 6-7). [52] - The user rushes forward and unleashes six rapid, powerful thrusts, aiming to strike and inject their target with multiple doses of poison at the target's weak spots (such as the neck, heart, torso, etc.) in order to kill them with a large dose of poison.
  • Dance of the Centipede: Hundred-Legged Zigzag (蜈ご蚣こうノ舞まい   百ひゃく足そく蛇じゃ腹ばら Gokō no mai: Hyakusoku Jabara) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 142 (Pages 15-16). [45] - The user uses their full speed to run a zigzag pattern that confuses their opponent in order to create an opening for them to directly stab their opponent's neck and inject poison to kill them, with the stab's force and power being further enhanced by the momentum of their speed. Doma noted that the sheer speed and footwork of this technique is strong enough to destroy an entire wooden bridge.

Game Exclusive Techniques

  • Butterfly Dance: Caprice, Illusory Light (蝶ちょうノ舞まい   戯たわむれ・幻げん光こう Chō no mai: Tawamure - Genkō) Kimetsu no Yaiba Game: The Hinokami Chronicles [63] - Shinobu starts with a backflip before lunging at her opponent. Upon connection, Shinobu unleashes a barrage of straight short thrusts with her sword, ending with one powerful thrust that launches her opponent away and onto their back. She then leaps into the air while transforming into a gigantic butterfly of light, before diving from it and stabbing her blade straight through her opponent's chest and into the ground, cracking the surrounding terrain and creating a massive crater in its wake.
  • Butterfly Dance: Caprice, Fluorescence (蝶ちょうノ舞まい   "戯たわむれ"・燐りん光こう Chō no mai: Tawamure - Rinkō) Kimetsu no Yaiba Game: The Hinokami Chronicles 2 [64] - Shinobu starts with a backflip kick, that sends her opponent upward. Upon connection, she blades hidden in her sandals to slash them mid-air, before kicking them downwards. She then performs Butterfly Dance while homing in on the opponent from above, although instead of a single strike, it's a constant barrage of poisonous thrusts from all directions, so fast that it forms into a ball of energy and erupts into a double-helix shape consisting of butterflies.
  • Sixfold Thrust (六ろく連れん突つき Rokuren Tsuki) Kimetsu no Yaiba Game: The Hinokami Chronicles [63] - The user unleashes five short but very quick thrusts towards their opponent, before briefly pausing and then unleashing a sixth, more powerful thrust.
  • Swift Thrust (速はや足あし突つき Haya'ashi Tsuki) Kimetsu no Yaiba Game: The Hinokami Chronicles [63] - The user spins before kicking off the ground and quickly dashing towards their opponent with enough speed to simulate teleporation, then does a powerful, singular thrust that hits twice.
  • Rising Thrust (昇のぼり突つき Nobori Tsuki) Kimetsu no Yaiba Game: The Hinokami Chronicles [63] - The user launches a thrust towards their opponent and then releases an upwards thrist that launches their opponent high up into the air.
  • The Brilliant and the Brave (柱はしらと庚かのえ Hashira to Kanoe) Kimetsu no Yaiba Game: The Hinokami Chronicles 2 [64] - A collaboration between an Insect Breathing user and a Water Breathing user, although unlike Dancing Waters' Allure, it is not required for the Water Breather to have a particularly high skill set. The Insect Breather takes the lead by kicking their opponent back several times before unleashing two thrusting attacks similar to Dance of the Butterfly. After the opponent falls to the ground, the Water Breather joins in and attacks them several times, albeit much more unrefined. The Water Breather then rushes forward and uses a move similar to Water Surface Slash. However, there's a minor chance that the Water Breather's uniform is damaged in the chaos.
  • Dancing Waters' Allure (水すい艶えん乱らん舞ぶ Suien Ranbu) Kimetsu no Yaiba Game: The Hinokami Chronicles 2 [64] - A collaboration between an Water Breathing user and a Insect Breathing user. After the two deliver a crosscut, the Water Breather attacks the opponent several times in a winding fashion similar to Striking Tide. The Insect Breather then joins in and delivers a spinning thrust similar to Dance of the Butterfly. The Water and Insect Breather then bombard their opponent with several attacks from their respective Breathing Styles; the former attacking from several directions, the latter somersaulting around their opponent and stabbing them several times. After the Water Breather slashes them, the Insect Breather stabs and kicks them in the air. As the opponent falls down, the Insect Breather charges towards them while the Water Breather executes a move similar to Dead Calm. When contact is made, the opponent is struck by a constant and multidirectional barrage of Water Breathing and Insect Breathing attacks ending in a spectacular combination.
  • Chemistry Class Concoction (科学実験かがくじっけん Kagaku Jikken) Kimetsu no Yaiba Game: The Hinokami Chronicles [63] - A technique exclusive to the Kimetsu Academy version of Shinobu Kocho. As President of the Pharmacology Club, she uses her knowledge in chemistry to give her opponent an Erienmeyer flask containing a strange pink chemical. She then pouts in another chemical from a test tube, which reacts violently and results in an explosion, causing Shinobu to sarcastically lament, "oh dear, another failure". She then proceeds to lightly bonk herself on the head and give a sarcastic "oops".

Equipment

Stinger Nichirin Katana: Shinobu wielded a heavily modified Nichirin Katana forged by Tecchin Tecchikawahara. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Volume 16 (Page 12). [65] It was the same as any katana from the handle to slightly above the hilt, but above that, the weapon lost its blade and was replaced by a rod. It ended in a needle-like tip, which resembled a traditional katana's in appearance and function. The blade was designed for injection of wisteria poison into demons, as Shinobu used her medical expertise to replace her relative lack of strength and inability to decapitate demons. Her katana's tsuba was designed to bear resemblance to a butterfly, being a four-petaled flower shape that was in an aqua color with tree branches, bushy leave motifs and trimmed with an orange border. The handle was orange and wrapped in crystal teal cords, and capped with an orange kashira. The blade was black in color, with the bottom hilt and the needle-like tip of the blade had a lavender-blue outline. The words Evil Demon (惡あっ鬼き Akki) were engraved on the right face, while the words Destruction (滅めっ殺さつ Messatsu) were engraved on the left face due to her sword's structure. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 41 (Page 19). [2]


Modified Sheath: Along with her katana, Shinobu had also modified her katana's sheath, using it as a portable poison mixer and loader. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 141 (Page 18). [66] The sheath was in white with a butterfly motifs. She twisted her katana when sheathing it, which allowed her to change the concoction of poison she used. Only she and the maker of the sheath knew how this process worked, though Doma was able to hear it in action and determine its function. Kimetsu no Yaiba Movie: Infinity Castle - Part 1: Akaza Returns. [18] She used it several times when fighting him in an attempt to poison him in the most effective way she could muster.

Trivia

  • Shinobu's surname contains the Kan-on'yomi reading of the kanji for "butterfly" (胡こ蝶ちょう kochō). Her first name is written in hiragana and thus doesn't have any semantic values, but it's usually written with the kanji for "stealth, endurance, patience" (忍しのぶ shinobu).
    • Since her first name uses three hiragana characters, it's notably different to Kanae and Kanao's names, as they're both written with three katakana characters instead.
  • Shinobu's blade was designed and created by the chief of the Swordsmith Village, Tecchin Tecchikawahara, likely due to its unique shape requiring the chief's expertise to forge. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Volume 16, Extra Pages. [28]
  • Shinobu's haori resembling a monarch butterfly's wings is notable as in real life, the milkweed diet of the butterflies causes them to be poisonous, which is a defense against predators. This both is represented through Shinobu's usage of poison, and mirrors how her habit of consuming wisteria poison caused Doma to be severely poisoned after absorbing her.
  • Shinobu's Demon Slayer uniform's jacket is originally designed to leave the center of the chest area open, similar to Mitsuri Kanroji's, but, as Shinobu disliked this aspect of its design, she burned the original uniform right in front of the perverted Kakushi who had just given it to her and requested a fully buttoned version of the uniform instead. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Volume 12, (Extra Pages) [67]
  • Although Shinobu wouldn't admit it, she was a natural airhead. The names she came up with for Kanao were so bizarre that upon seeing them, Kanae broke down into such an "EH?!" expression thta even the efficient Aoi Kanzaki wasn't able to smoothen things out. Kimetsu no Yaiba Second Fanbook. [58]
  • Shinobu's overall level of openness was ranked 85%. Kimetsu no Yaiba Second Fanbook. [58]
  • Shinobu's hobby is telling ghost stories. Kimetsu no Yaiba First Fanbook. [68]
  • Shinobu's favorite food is Ginger Tsukudani Kimetsu no Yaiba First Fanbook. [68]
  • Shinobu and Mitsuri get along quite well due to them having been the only two female Hashira after Kanae's death. Kimetsu no Yaiba First Fanbook. [69] Mitsuri sometimes teaches Shinobu western cooking. Kimetsu no Yaiba First Fanbook. [69]
  • According to Giyu, Shinobu can't stand furry animals. Instead, she has a pet goldfish named Fugu. Kimetsu no Yaiba Second Fanbook. [58] Oddly enough, Fugu is the Japanese name for a pufferfish, which is renowned for its lethally poisonous body.
  • There was some tension during the cooperative research time between Shinobu and Tamayo. Shinobu held some distrust towards Tamayo due to her being a demon and Tamayo's assistant, Yushiro, wished to kill Shinobu as a result of it. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Volume 19. [70]
  • Shinobu views the other Hashira as follows: Kimetsu no Yaiba Second Fanbook. [58]
