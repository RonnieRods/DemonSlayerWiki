Gyomei Himejima (悲ひ鳴め嶼じま 行ぎょう冥めい Himejima Gyomei) is a major supporting character of Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba. He is a Demon Slayer of the Demon Slayer Corps and the Stone Hashira (岩いわ柱ばしら Iwa Bashira) of the Taisho era. Gyomei also serves as the mentor of Genya Shinazugawa.

Appearance

Gyomei is a hulking figure in the Demon Slayer Corps, standing as one of the tallest characters in the series and easily towering over his fellow Hashira. He is a powerfully built, extremely muscular man, with a slightly tanned skin tone. He has medium, spiky black hair that is shaved along the sides of his head and a prominent scar running horizontally across his forehead. Having been blind since childhood, he has white eyes with no visible irises or pupils. His eyes frequently tear-up when he gets emotional.


Gyomei wears a standard black Demon Slayer Corps uniform, tinted dark gray in the anime. His uniform consists of a button-up jacket with a stand-up collar and long sleeves, featuring gold buttons, a left breast pocket, and the kanji for Destroy (滅めつ Metsu) engraved on the back of the uniform. However, Gyomei's jacket is heavily customized; it is worn with only the last button fastened, and the sleeves of his jacket and undershirt rolled up. His uniform is secured at the waist with the standard white belt and paired with matching tattsuke-hakama pants. Beneath the uniform, he wears a purple kimono, complete with a prominent white yuban collar. Around his lower legs, he wears white bandages wrapped around his shins in place of traditional kyahan. For footwear, Gyomei wears white zōri sandals with gray straps, notably without tabi socks. Draped over his shoulders is a distinct olive-green happi, which features religious iconography. The collared frame of the happi bears the kanji for the Japanese Nianfo/ Shin Buddha chant (南な無む阿あ弥み陀だ仏ぶつ Namu Amida Butsu) from the top-down, along with the separate kanji all over at the back. Around his neck, he sports a large red-beaded necklace and carries a set of red ojuzu beads when not in battle.


During his time as a monk and caretaker, Gyomei wore a similar hairstyle to his current appearance. His simple attire consisted of a olive green yukata and pants, tied with a lighter green sash. Physically, he was noticeably shorter and slimmer due to malnutrition brought on by his unfavorable living situation and his own willingless to go hungry and let the children he cared for eat instead. He sustained his signature scar from a demon attack during this period.


He sustains a scar across his forehead after being slashed by a crescent moon blade from Kokushibo. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 169 (Page 17). [5] His left leg from the knee below was later severed during the battle against Muzan. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 191 (Page 14). [6]

Personality

Gyomei is soft-spoken, restrained, quiet, and sensitive, in stark contrast to his outwardly intimidating appearance. Stoic and proud, yet alert and perceptive, he speaks with an air of both imposing authority and strength, and of genuine care and gentleness. He is also shown to be quite pious, carrying his prayer beads at all times and offering prayers in poignant situations. Most notably, Gyomei easily cries over the most insignificant things, accentuating his soft nature. This could be due to his love for all living creatures brought about by his religious upbringing, and insinuated when he felt to disgusted by beating up a demon that he would never forget that feeling for the rest of his life.


Like the rest of the Hashira, Gyomei has a great deal of respect for Kagaya Ubuyashiki, of which no small part was due to the Oyakata saving his life. He is also one of the most respected individuals among the Demon Slayer Corps, not only due to being a member of the Hashira but because he serves as a symbol of hope due to his immense strength: Shinobu Kocho had once stated that when Gyomei fights, it puts everyone around him at ease. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 142 (Page 9). [7] Furthermore, Gyomei serves as a leader for them, as seen when he sat at the front during Hashira meetings and was often looked to for his word and opinion. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 128 (Page 12). [8] He was entrusted by Kagaya to give out orders to them, due to both Gyomei being the oldest and because Kagaya had known him for the longest out of anyone outside his own family.


Though he maintains a fairly reserved disposition, Gyomei harbors a very objective and somewhat suspicious view of the people around him, owing to his experiences before becoming a Demon Slayer. He is ever cognizant of what others, particularly children or those younger than him, are capable of if given the opportunity. By reason of his doubtful nature, it can take a while for Gyomei to completely accept and trust others. When he does open up and let others in, he is unwavering in his commitment to them.


Gyomei greatly values the principles that allowed him to achieve his status as a Hashira, rejecting Kokushibo's notion that he must become a demon in order to preserve the physical abilities and skills he had devoted himself to, and retorts his statement by saying that being a Demon Slayer means accepting the fact that seeing tomorrow is not guaranteed and that no one can become a Hashira with Kokushibo's half-hearted resolution. Like with Kyojuro Rengoku, the Stone Hashira also believes that living and dying as humans preserve one's dignity, and he even took the Upper Rank's statement as an insult, demanding the demon does not force his self-righteous belief onto him, further accentuating how dearly he holds onto his values.


Additionally, despite his generally reserved and polite disposition, in that moment, angered by Kokushibo's outright rejection of his humanity, Gyomei taunted him for lying about the fate of those who have gained the Demon Slayer Mark, prodding the demon for hiding the fact that someone has likely loved past the age of 25 and laughing at his visible apprehension. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 170 (Pages 10-12). [9]


Gyomei deeply cares for his comrades in the Demon Slayer Corps. When he heard about Kyojuro's death in battle, he began to tear up and chanted the Nianfo in response. During his stage of Hashira Training, despite the immense difficulty of his tasks, Gyomei emphasized perseverance and succeeding for the sake of their comrades who had fallen in battle, encouraging the trainees to push on. In particular, his reassurance of Tanjiro Kamado, whom he had initially wanted to have killed for his harboring of a demon against the Corps' rules, and his later resolution to keep him on the right path after proclaiming he was a genuine soul, demonstrated a true willingness to protect and see the people around him succeed.


This guidance and attention towards those around him was best put on display during the Corps' last campaign in the Infinity Castle, a time when everyone put their lives on the line for the ultimate victory. When he noticed Muichiro Tokito's boiling anger towards Muzan Kibutsuji for causing Kagaya to resort to committing suicide for his final campaign against the demons, he reassured the Mist Hashira that he and everyone else was equally enraged as well when he vowed to make Muzan suffer. He also acknowledges and commended Tanjiro and Giyu Tomioka for their defeat of Upper Rank Three. In the aftermath of the battle against Kokushibo, Gyomei tearfully thanked Muichiro for his perseverance and pivotal role in the demon's defeat at such a young age, and he gently but firmly encouraged Sanemi Shinazugawa, who had just witnessed his younger brother, and his own apprentice, Genya succumb to his wounds, to push aside his grief momentarily and focus on the coming battle against Muzan. In his last moments following the battle against Muzan, Gyomei, knowing he was not going to survive and accepting that his life was complete, ordered the Kakushi to forgo attempting to treat him, and to instead turn their attention to the other, younger Demon Slayers who need their immediate aid.


Abilities

Overall Abilities

As the oldest, most experienced, and most physically capable Hashira of the Demon Slayer Corps, Gyomei is an extremely powerful and skilled combatant. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 45 (Page 2). [10] He is stated by both Tanjiro Kamado and Inosuke Hashibira to be the strongest Demon Slayer in the entire Demon Slayer Corps. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 134 (Page 8). [11] Additionally, Tengen Uzui had once described Gyomei as a "complete enigma" which can either refer to his incredible physical prowess or his tremendous skill despite his blindness. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 87 (Page 6). [12] This is further given credence by Kokushibo, who noted that Gyomei's natural physical abilities have been trained to their absolute peak and had stated that in the last three hundred years, he has not encountered a warrior of Gyomei's caliber. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 169 (Page 3). [13] Whereas a Marked Muichiro Tokito and Genya Shinazugawa had been cut down in short order, and Sanemi was eventually overpowered despite briefly contending him due to his Marechi blood and exceptional endurance, Gyomei was the only fighter unmarked to challenge Kokushibo without being completely overwhelmed. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 169. [14] Moreover, the demon had noted that Gyomei and Sanemi were two of the more highly skilled combatants, even among the ranks of the already powerful Hashira. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 171 (Page 5). [15] After the fight, Gyomei, who sustained the least physical damage in the aftermath, was able to contribute greatly in the battle against Muzan Kibutsuji, as well as rescuing Mitsuri Kanroji and Giyu Tomioka when they were about to be struck by the demon.


Tactical Intellect: Accrued over many years of battling demons, Gyomei has displayed keen intellect and judgement abilities in combat. During his fight against Kokushibo, he realized that the Upper Rank was overpowering him because he was somehow able to anticipate and retaliate against his incoming attacks seemingly with nothing to base on. He immediately knew this method of perception was supernatural but still could be obtained or countered somehow. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 173 (Page 4). [16] When he later gained access to the Transparent World, he surmised that his eyes gave him the ability to access this special world as well and instinctively learned how to use this to his advantage, even going as far as to manipulating his own blood flow and breathing to disrupt Kokushibo's pattern of attacks, shocking Kokushibo with how quickly he managed to skilfully use the Transparent World in combat. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 173 (Page 16). [17] Upon seeing Muzan's special constitution, he immediately deduced that he needed to attack all twelve of his vital organs simultaneously to deal significant damage to him. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 191 (Pages 10-11). [18]


Demon Slayer Abilities

Demon Slayer Mark: Later during his battle against Upper Rank One, Gyomei displayed that he too, has awakened his mark, appearing as cracked earth fissure-like patterns on both of his forearms. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 169 (Page 18). [19] When manifested, it drastically increases his physical abilities, such as his strength, speed, and stamina, allowing him to fight the Upper Rank for an extended period of time. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 170-176. [20] Kokushibo had even stated that his immense rate of growth was more comparably to that of a demon than that of a human.

  • Bright Red Nichrin: During the battle against Kokushibo, Sanemi was able to strike Gyomei's spiked flail with his sword, turning both of them briefly bright red allowing him to destroy Kokushibo's head. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 175 (Page 21). [21] Later in the battle against Muzan, Gyomei turned his weapons bright red again by striking them together. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 190 (Page 15). [22]
  • Transparent World: Despite being blind, Gyomei gained the ability to access the Transparent World. He can predict his opponent's movements and sense them naturally, and his already keen perception was heightened even further. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 173 (Page 6). [23]


Repetitive Action: Created by Gyomei, this is a technique which maximizes concentration, in a similar way to Total Concentration Breathing, by remembering and focusing on a painful memory. Gyomei is shown to use this extensively during his training, and presumably while in combat too.


Physical Abilities

Immense Speed & Reflexes: Despite Gyomei's large build, he possesses tremendous speed and reflexes. He is said to be the third fastest runner among the Hashira, Gyomei's incredible speed was put on display during his fight against Kokushibo, where he was able to easily move his body around to avoid a majority of his attacks, with the Upper Rank even remarking that the belying ease and grace that he moved with was almost too absurd to believe. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 169 (Page 14). [24] Gyomei was also able to keep up with Kokushibo for the entire duration of the fight, putting pressure on him with the help of Sanemi, Muichiro, and Genya. Later, he was capable of keeping up with the blistering movement speeds of the Demon King himself, Muzan, for a good portion of the fight.


Immense Stamina & Endurance: Gyomei possesses tremendous levels of physical endurance and stamina, so much so that his training during Hashira Training focused on strengthening the body for improved endurance and defensive capabilities. He was able to nonchalantly stand on ground that was on fire while carrying two massive logs being further weighted down by four large rocks, even calmly explaining the details of his training to Tanjiro and Zenitsu while doing so. He also displayed his superhuman levels of stamina and endurance during the fight against Kokushibo, where he was able to hold off the Upper Rank for the entire duration of the fight without significantly tiring on top of sustaining deep cuts all over his body. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 169 (Pages 17-18). [25] Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 171 (Page 15). [26] Even after getting one of his legs slashed off by Muzan, Gyomei was able to persist and continue contributing to the fight against the Demon King.


Immense Strength: Gyomei possesses the greatest level of physical strength in the entire Demon Slayer Corps, most notably showcased when he is able to easily lift and swing his massive spiked flail and axe constructed from pure iron at speeds faster than the eye can see incessantly to thwart the attacks of Kokushibo. He was also able to push a large boulder bigger than him through a town with his bare hands. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 134 (Page 9). [27] Gyomei also nonchalantly endured the weight of two massive logs on his back further weighed down with four large rocks. According to Gyomei, he was naturally born with the gift of his incredulous physical strength, as shown when he subdued a demon that attacked him when he was a skinny teenager by beating it to a bloody pulp with nothing but his bare hands prior to him joining the Demon Slayer Corps and undergoing training, but he did not know he had it in himself until he was forced to fight for his life. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 135 (Pages 15-16). [28]


Supernatural Abilities

Enhanced Hearing: Due to being blind, Gyomei has compensated and alternated his senses to adapt to his condition, effectively developing an extremely heightened sense of hearing. His sense of hearing was so acute that Gyomei can accurately discern and interact with his surroudings normally, even engaging in high-speed combat with demons without trouble. More impressively, Gyomei was able to hear the sound of Muzan Kibutsuji's flesh regeneration during their brief confrontation and used it to determine that he is unlike any demon he has ever faced based solely on the sound the Demon King gave off. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 139 (Page 6). [29]


Later during his battle with Upper Rank One, Kokushibo noted that Gyomei uses the sound of his chains rattling to tell the exact position the chain is in and uses that to guide his axe and flail during combat. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 171 (Page 6). [30] As seen during his conversation with Kokushibo regarding the Demon Slayer Mark, Gyomei was able to accurately tell that ht was lying by detecting the slight changes of the sound of his body, with Gyomei noting that he heard the latter shake after revealing his deception.


Fighting Style

Kusarigamajutsu: Unlike ordinary Demon Slayers who use swords in comabt, Gyomei uses a spiked flail and axe reminiscent of a Kusarigama, a weapon consisting of a scythe and heavy iron weight connected with a metal chain, and thus would uses a similar fighting style known as Kusarigamajutsu. As a Hashira, Gyomei is extremely proficient in combat and one of the most skilled combatants in the entire Demon Slayer Corps, particularly in this fighting style which can briefly match the likes of Upper Rank One, Kokushibo, a demon swordsman who has nearly five centuries of swordsmanship experience. Moreover, he was also shown to be able to hold off Muzan and his blisteringly fast whips for an extended period of time, using his spiked flail to deliver powerful blows to the Demon King.


Breathing Style

Stone Breathing (岩いわの呼こ吸きゅう Iwa no kokyū): A Breathing Style that Gyomei either mastered himself or learned from a cultivator. In his own version of this Breathing Style, Gyomei incorporates his superhuman sense of hearing and physical strength to utilize a spiked flail and axe to use its techniques. As the Stone Hashira, Gyomei is his generation's most skilled Stone Breathing user. He displayed mastery of Stone Breathing in his battle against Kokushibo, where he was able to unleash attacks that were able to match the demon's Moon Breathing.


Techniques

[31] - The user throws both the flail and axe towards their intended target and manipulates the chain by rotating it thus causing the axe and flail to also rotate in order to drill and grind right through their target.

  • Fourth Form: Volcanic Rock, Rapid Conquest (肆しノ型かた   流りゅう紋もん岩がん・速そく征せい Shi no kata: Ryūmongan - Sokusei) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 169 (Pages 15-16). [34] - The user manipulates both the flail and axe weapons at both ends of the chain by swinging the chain on both sides in order to strike their opponent using a two pronged wide ranged attack.
  • Fifth Form: Arcs of Justice (伍ごノ型かた   瓦が輪りん刑ぎょう部ぶ Go no kata: Garin Gyōbu) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 176 (Page 7). [35] - The user utilizes a powerful barrage with their flail and axe, doing a great amount of damage. The axe is destined to hit first with the trajectory of the flail following from the opposite side and slamming into the opponent.

Game Exclusive Techniques

  • Third Form: Stone Skin, Deva (参さんノ型かた・金こん剛ごう   岩がん軀くの膚はだえ San no kata: Ganku no Hadae - Kongō) Kimetsu no Yaiba Game: The Hinokami Chronicles 2. [36] - An offensive variant of Stone Skin. After knocking their opponent with their flail, the user sends both flail and axe towards them, leaps in the air, similar to Serpentinite Bipolar, and performs Stone Skin, but instead of the omnidirectional barrage of attacks deflecting attacks from the user, it instead is concentrated in the direction of their target, striking them from multiple angles. They then finish by hurling their axe next to the now grounded opponent, and diving down in their direciton with the flail at their feet, crushing the target with their own weight.
  • Earth-Shattering Morningstar (地じめ鳴いき強ょう投とう Jimei Kyōtō) Kimetsu no Yaiba Game: The Hinokami Chronicles 2. [36] - The user hurls their flail at the target, and then immediately pulls it back and hurls their axe at them.
  • Raging Boulder (巌がん突とつ転てん進しん Gantatsu Tenshin) Kimetsu no Yaiba Game: The Hinokami Chronicles 2. [36] - The user moves towards their target, swings their flail at them, jumps up and spins around to hurl the flail towards them again, and finally twirls their weapon around 360° horizontally to hit them with both flail and axe, all in quick succession.
  • Dance of Divine Protection (仁に王おう舞まい Niō Mai) Kimetsu no Yaiba Game: The Hinokami Chronicles 2. [36] - A move virtually identical to Stone Skin. The user rapidly twirls and swings the chain aroud them to unleash an omnidirectional flurry of attacks from all directions at once, and bombard the opponent with a flurry of blows.
  • Demolishing Blow (撥はね潰つぶし Hane Tsubushi) Kimetsu no Yaiba Game: The Hinokami Chronicles 2. [36] - Similar to Upper Smash, the user hits their opponent with their flail and axe several times, before twirling the flail around and hurling it downwards, smashing the target.
  • Seismic Burst: Pincer (岩いわ砕くだき・挟きょう撃げき Iwakudaki - Kyōgeki) Kimetsu no Yaiba Game: The Hinokami Chronicles 2. [36] - The user charges forward before cleaving their opponent's torso with their axe and swinging them into the air, sending them flying alongside multiple stones which hit the target in their path. The user then twirls the flail clockwise with enough speed and strength to create a suction force which drags the opponent and the various debris back, before hurling the flail at the opponent, violently hitting them by the spiked ball midair.
  • Master and Disciple (師し匠しょうと弟で子し Shishō to Deshi) Kimetsu no Yaiba Game: The Hinokami Chronicles 2. [36] - A technique exclusive to Gyomei and his protégé Genya Shinazugawa, Genya shoots their opponent using his shotgun before Gyomei hurls his axe and launches them skywards. After the axe flies back towards Gyomei, Genya grabs hold of the chain and is sent upwards as Gyomei hurls his flail at the opponent, with the Demon Slayer shooting his target as the chain circles around them mid-air. Gyomei then strikes the ground with his axe and tosses up a massive boulder, which Genya grabs hold of and positions directly on top of their target. As they fall to the ground, the target is crushed by both the boulder and Gyomei's flail shattering it.

Equipment

Nichirin Chained Spiked Flail and Axe: Instead of a Nichirin katana, Gyomei wields a weapon similar to a Kusarigama, consisting of an Ono (axe) attached to a Chigiriki (a massive iron ball with numerous piercing spikes) via a long chain. It is in a dark gray color, with the axe having a dull gray outline. Still, the weapon is forged from the same steel used in the creation of Nichirin swords. It's craftsmanship in particular is noted for being incredibly high-class, being made of pure iron that has absorbed so much sunlight, it was considered even superior to the iron used to forge swords during the Sengoku period, when swordsmithing was at the peak of development. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 169 (Pages 10-11). [37] Even Upper Rank One, Kokushibo, could not cut through the chain and was forced to instead resort to long-range combat. As noted by Kokushibo, this weapon is very well thought out, as the vibrations created by the chain can be picked up by Gyomei's acute sense of hearing to accurately grasp the space and movements around him, further enhancing its effectiveness in battle. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 171 (Page 6). [30] On the hilt of his axe, the words Destroyer of Demons (惡あっ鬼き滅めっ殺さつ Akki Messatsu) are carved. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 169 (Pages 10-11). [38]


In the final battle against Muzan, the chain was used by Gyomei and several Kakushi to restrain Muzan as he tried to flee from the sunlight in the form of a giant fleshy infant, snapping in two in the ensuing struggle.

Trivia

  • Gyomei is blind, thus explaining the reason for the lack of pupils in his eyes.
    • He was blinded due to a fever he had when he was a child. Kimetsu no Yaiba Second Fanbook. [39]
  • His given name, Gyomei, is composed of the Go-on'yomi reading of the kanji for "Saṅkhāra, meaning formations of the mind" (行ぎょう gyō) and the Kan-on'yomi reading for "dark, depressed" (冥めい mei).
  • His surname, Himejima, can be read in one-go as "screaming island". It is composed of the Kan-on'yomi reading of the kanji for "Karuṇā" (悲ひ hi). along "to cry, scream" (鳴め me). and the Kun'yomi Rendaku for "islet, island" (嶼じま me).
  • Sayo carries immense guilt over Gyomei being mistakenly arrested. Even after roughly ten years go by, she still wants to apologize to Gyomei for the misunderstanding. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Volume 16, (Extra Pages). [40]
  • Gyomei is one of the current Hashira who doesn't use a Nichirin Sword.
  • According to Tengen Uzui, Gyomei is a complete enigma. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 87 (Page 4). [41]
  • "Namu Amida Butsu", also known as Nembutsu (念ねん仏ぶつ Nenbutsu), is a Nianfo chant from Shin Buddhism. A common translation is: "Bow for the sake of Amitābha Buddha."
  • Gyomei is one of the few Hashira besides Muichiro and Shinobu to have a personal connection to an Upper Rank, that being Upper Rank Six, Kaigaku. Kaigaku was one of the orphaned children under Gyomei's care at the temple and would later be directly responsible for the demon attack that resulted in the death of all the children, except Sayo, and his wrongful imprisonment.
  • Gyomei is often depicted with tears streaming down his face. It is later revealed that he cries extremely easily at almost anything, even crying for something as minor as when there is litter on the ground.
  • Gyomei loves cats, a trait he has in common with the Love Hashira, Mitsuri Kanroji. Kimetsu no Yaiba First Fanbook. [42]
  • One of Gyomei's hobbies is playing the shakuhachi, a type of flute. Once, when he played for too long, his grandmother hit him with a broom. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Volume 13, (Extra Pages). [43]
  • Gyomei shares his Japanese and English voice actors as Kyon from The Melancholy of Haruhi Suzumiya.
  • Gyomei viewed the other Hashira as follows: Kimetsu no Yaiba Second Fanbook. [39]
    • "She's sensitive and pushes herself too hard. She likes talking to Tomioka," - Shinobu Kocho
    • "He talks big, but he's clearheaded. He has an accurate grasp of his own abilities, so he isn't arrogant." - Tengen Uzui
    • "She's a good girl with a pleasant nature. Apparently, she has a crush on Iguro." - Mitsuri Kanroji
    • "He has a strong spirit. He's sensitive on the inside, so he easily gets embarrassed. Apparently, he likes Kanae." - Sanemi Shinazugawa
    • "He's dark and not much of a talker, but apparently, he enjoys chatting with Kocho." - Giyu Tomioka
    • "He's straightforward, optimistic, and never complains." - Kyojuro Rengoku
    • "He has a mature spirit and speaks up when he should." - Muichiro Tokito
    • "He's a serious, sensitive boy. And he has a crush on Kanroji." - Obanai Iguro
  • Gyomei's level of openness was ranked as 90%. Kimetsu no Yaiba Second Fanbook. [39]
  • Gyomei's father died in an epidemic, while his mother died in childbirth. Some time later he was blinded due to a fever. In spite of his blindness, Gyomei had a keen sense of "sight" and was able to avoid malicious actions that some people around him do (trying to make him trip, throwing stuff at him etc.). This made people around him call him a liar, stating that no blind person would be able to do what he does. Kimetsu no Yaiba Second Fanbook. [39]
  • Gyomei once beat Inosuke headfirst into the ground, which happened because Inosuke one-sidedly decided to challenge Gyomei to a match. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Volume 15, (Extra Pages). [44] Kimetsu no Yaiba Anime: Episode 61. [45]
  • In Kimetsu Academy, Gyomei is a civics teacher and first-year homeroom teacher. Since he's so muscular, students were afraid of him at first. But because his personal belongings generally have cats on them, everybody quickly became aware that he's a nice person who likes cats. There have been many incidents of people calling him "dad" by accident. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Volume 20, (Extra Pages). [46]
  • Gyomei's favorite food is Takikomi-Gohan. But since he is a monk, he probably doesn't use meat. Kimetsu no Yaiba First Fanbook. [42]
