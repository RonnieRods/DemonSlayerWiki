Quick Answers

What led to Gyomei Himejima being spared from execution?
The intervention of Kagaya Ubuyashiki saved Gyomei Himejima from execution. He had been falsely accused of killing the children he looked after at a temple. Following his acquittal, he joined the Demon Slayer Corps.
How did Gyomei Himejima become a member of the Demon Slayer Corps?
Gyomei Himejima's induction into the Demon Slayer Corps isn't detailed in the given context. He is acknowledged as the strongest Demon Slayer by his peers, Tanjiro and Inosuke, due to his peak physical abilities and combat skills. As the Stone Hashira, Gyomei also mentors Genya Shinazugawa.
Why is Gyomei Himejima known as the Stone Hashira?
Gyomei Himejima earned the title of Stone Hashira after being rescued from a false murder charge by Kagaya Ubuyashiki. He then joined the Demon Slayer Corps, serving under Kagaya's command. As the Stone Hashira, Gyomei is one of the nine most formidable swordsmen in the Corps.
What is the nature of Gyomei Himejima's relationship with Kagaya?
Gyomei Himejima and Kagaya Ubuyashiki have a profound relationship. Kagaya saved Gyomei from a wrongful execution, leading to Gyomei's induction into the Demon Slayer Corps as the Stone Hashira. Their relationship is marked by mutual respect and trust, with Gyomei serving as Kagaya's trusted confidant.
How did Gyomei Himejima earn Kagaya's trust?
At 14, Kagaya Ubuyashiki saved Gyomei Himejima, a falsely accused monk, from execution, recognizing a demon was the real murderer. Gyomei then became a Demon Slayer and the Stone Hashira, serving under Kagaya and acting as his trusted confidant. Kagaya's trust in Gyomei was so deep that he shared his self-sacrifice plan against Muzan Kibutsuji exclusively with him.

Relationships

Kagaya Ubuyashiki

Kagaya is the one who petitioned for Gyomei to be spared from execution after he was wrongfully assumed to have been the killer of the children he cared for at their temple. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 135. [1] Shortly afterward, Gyomei became a member of the Demon Slayer Corps and became the Stone Hashira in just two months. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 87 (Page 4). [2]


Gyomei is grateful to Kagaya and greatly respects, especially when he spends time to know him a little more personally.


It can also be inferred that Kagaya trusted Gyomei and Gyomei was Kagaya's confidant, as the Corps leader only shared with Gyomei about his plans about sacrificing himself.


Tanjiro Kamado

At first, Gyomei found Tanjiro to be weak and pitiful, even suggesting that he should be mercifully killed for being lost to his obsession with a demon. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 45. [3] When he overheard Tanjiro's brash vow to slay Muzan Kibutsuji prompting Kagaya to respond that he should first start with the Twelve Kizuki, Gyomei was amused and stifled his laughter.


However, after Tanjiro continues to impress with his feats, Gyomei comes to hold him in better regard. It is not until the Hashira Training that Gyomei sees Tanjiro's character for himself and becomes impressed with the boy's honesty and character. He comes to fully accept Tanjiro and state that he would be there to prevent Tanjiro from making any mistakes no matter what anyone else said. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 135. [1] The two later fight alongside each other in the final battle against Muzan.


Genya Shinazugawa

In the data book, it says that Gyomei took Genya in as his apprentice. It is revealed that Genya was rescued by Gyomei when he went out of control after eating a demon. Genya stubbornly decided to follow Gyomei and continously asked the latter to take the former in as his Tsuguko. Although Genya wasn't blessed with talent for Breathing Styles and had a bad temper, he got along well with Gyomei, the latter acknowledging him for his honesty and willpower.


Due to his closeness with Gyomei, he is aware of things about him that no one else knows, such as Gyomei's tendency to be a poor explainer and his mannerisms in training. Upon Genya's death, Gyomei was visibly saddened by the loss of his beloved apprentice, but he kept his composure and told his distraught brother Sanemi that the battle was not done until Muzan is killed.


Kaigaku

Kaigaku was one of the children Gyomei cared for at his temple. After he was caught by the other children for stealing temple money, he was chased out at night and encountered a Demon, which he then let in to the temple in exchange for having his life spared. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Volume 17, Extra Pages. [4] Gyomei was at first not aware of this, being told by the other children that Kaigaku was with the rest of them. It wouldn't be until the demon had told him what Kaigaku did, and he put two-and-two together.


Gyomei ended up killing the demon after punching its face until daybreak, however he ended up getting blamed for the deaths of the children as the demon disintegrated in the sunlight which left no trace. This incident as a whole was responsible for Gyomei becoming heavily paranoid of other people. However, Gyomei has also stated that were it not for this incident, he would have never learned that he was strong, showing that he has come to terms with it in a way. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 135. [1] It's unknown if Gyomei was ever aware that Kaigaku would later join the very organization he was apart of, the Demon Slayer Corps, and it was also unknown if he knew he had defected to the demons.


Shinobu Kocho

Gyomei saved Shinobu and her older sister Kanae from a Demon when she was fairly young, and, despite not remembering much about it, Shinobu holds a massive respect for Gyomei as a result. Shinobu used to remind Gyomei of the children from the temple where he grew up. After the Hashira Meeting, Gyomei shows a deep concern for Shinobu and tries to tell her the words "Don't go dying on me", but fails to get the words out.
