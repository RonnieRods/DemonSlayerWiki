Jigoro Kuwajima (桑くわ島じま 慈じ悟ご郎ろう Kuwajima Jigorō) was the man who trained Zenitsu Agatsuma and Kaigaku. He was formerly the Rumble Hashira (鳴なり柱ばしら Nari Bashira) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Volume 17 (Page 5). [1] in the Demon Slayer Corps. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 34 (Page 5). [2]

Appearance

Jigoro was a short, elderly man with pale skin complexion, visible forhead wrinkles, and straight scar with six prongs running along his left cheekbone. He has large, slanted eyes with small irises. He had notably bushy eyebrows and a large moustache, as well as slick white hair that he wore smoothed down to puff out around the base of his head, with what would've been a fringe also swept back away from his face. Jigoro was also missing his right leg, with a wooden peg taking its place, this feature was made more prominent in the anime than the manga.


Jigoro wore a jinbei kimono like the one worn by Sakonji Urokodaki, only with a yellow-brown base color and a pale triangle pattern in the stead of the blue and water-patterned theme Sakonji sports. He also carried a thick wooden cane with him to support his body due to age and his missing leg.


Although Jigoro's tenure as an active Hashira is never shown, a back-view cameo in Kinaza no Kiseki showcases that Jigoro looked more or less the same to his current appearance, with the exception of having black hair in his youth.

Personality

Not unlike Sakonji Urokodaki, Jigoro had a steadfast devotion to his students, showing himself to be strict, rigid, and stubborn for their sake. Owing to his experience as a past Hashira and his position as a trainer, he possessed an unwavering dedication towards raising the next generation of Demon Slayers. Due to this passionate devotion, he was filled with grief after discovering that his former pupil, Kaigaku, betrayed the Demon Slayer Corps and joined the Twelve Kizuki, Zenitsu Agatsuma noted that, despite how often he whined and ran away, Jigoro never gave up on him once. Having saved him from a lifetime of debt by taking him in as a student, he showed patience in the face of his lesser moments, and was even happy just to see Zenitsu master the first form of Thunder Breathing.


He also held his belief in training one's skill to perfection through repetition, comparing it to forging and purifying the steel of their swords, and it was this ideology that allowed Zenitsu to extend the first form to new heights and even create his own. This strong love for his students was also evident when he was shown to be both flattered and touched by Zenitsu's declaration of his love and gratefulness for all of his efforts put into forcing him to improve himself, even after everyone else had abandoned and deemed him worthless. Jigoro was also extremely fair, treating his students with the same level of attention regardless of their level of training; when Kaigaku accused him of favoring Zenitsu by teaching him an unknown form, Zenitsu defended him as someone who would never do such a thing.


History

In his prime, Jigoro had served as the Thunder Breathing Hashira, although he was knwon as the Rumble Hashira. When he was 35, he lost his leg in a battle that forced him to retire. Jigoro would continue to serve the Demon Slayer Corps however, teaching students how to utilize Thunder Breathing. After taking in a young man with a troubled past named Kaigaku, Jigoro stumbled across a boy named Zenitsu Agatsuma. Zenitsu was being beat up after incurring a massive debt from an unfaithful ex-girlfriend, and taking pity on him, Jigoro paid off his debt and took him in as his student. Kaigaku managed to master all of the Thunder Breathing Forms except for the first form, meanwhile Zenitsu had only managed to master the first form and none of the others due to his inexperience.


In training the two, Kaigaku managed to master all of Thunder Breathing's forms except for the first, whereas Zenitsu had only managed to master the first and none of the rest due to his inexperience. Zenitsu proved to be a very defiant student due to his own cowardice, frequently crying and attempting to run away, much to Jigoro and Kaigaku's chagrin. However, the former was a lot more patient with him, even being flattered when Zenitsu referred to him as "gramps" as a sign of affection. In one incident, Zenitsu climbed a tree and threw a tantrum, ranting that alrhough he loved Jigoro and genuinely tried to improve his swordsmanship, he had nothing to show for it and was going to die. Zenitsu was then struck by lighting, much to Jigoro's horror.


Zenitsu survived, though his hair turned yellow as a result, and Jigoro continued to train him. Although he frequently beat Zenitsu as discipline, and foiled his attempts to escape training, Jigoro never lost faith in Zenitsu and even went so far as to encourage him to hone the only form he had mastered. Unbeknownst to him however, his other student Kaigaku would grow jealous of Jigoro's care for Zenitsu, viewing it as favoritism for someone unworthy of it. His envy further grew when Jigoro decided to make both boys his successors, as they covered each others' weakness.


Synopsis

Mount Natagumo Arc

Jigoro debuted prominently through Zenitsu's flashbacks when he was fighting the Son Spider Demon. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 33 (Page 5). [3] He also thought of his master when on the brink of death from the demon's poison, controlling his breathing to resist the poison.


Hashira Training Arc

Some time before or during Hashira Training, Jigoro committed seppuku when learning that his former protégé Kaigaku defected to gain the powers of a demon. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 144 (Pages 9-12). [4] According to Zenitsu, his death proved to be slow and agonizing since no one could cut off his head while he bled out. Zenitsu was informed of this, and was filled with a vengeful drive to avenge Jigoro by confronting Kaigaku.


Infinity Castle Arc

When Zenitsu finally met Kaigaku inside the Infinity Castle, he angrily confronted Kaigaku over his betrayal leading to Jigoro's suicide. Kaigaku callously disregarded his death, claiming that Jigoro deserved it for not recognizing him as his sole successor, and calling him a senile old man. Zenitsu rebuked this, pointing out Kaigaku's hypocrisy as both were incapable of fully mastering Thunder Breathing, and claiming to have pitied Jigoro for having "pathetic successors". Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 144. [5]


When the two engage in battle, Kaigaku continues to mention Jigoro, stubbornly holding on to his belief that Jigoro favored Zenitsu and made a mistake making him a successor. After being shocked and wounded by Kaigaku's Thunder Breathing, Sixth Form: Rumble and Flash and hearing him bluster that he is special, Zenitsu remembers Jigoro ordering him to follow Kaigaku's example, lamenting the path he took. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 145 (Pages 10-11). [6] Kimetsu no Yaiba Movie: Infinity Castle - Part 1: Akaza Returns. [7]


After Zenitsu managed to kill Kaigaku, Zenitsu and Jigoro briefly meet in the afterlife. His student apologized for not being able to get along with Kaigaku and for not being able to become a Hashira while he was still alive. Before Zenitsu forcefully returned to the land of the living, Jigoro declared that Zenitsu was his pride and joy with tears in his eyes. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 146 (Page 7). [8]


Sunrise Countdown Arc

While fighting Muzan Kibutsuji, Zenitsu thought of Jigoro, the memory encouraging him to fight further. After the battle, Zenitsu wore Jigoro's haori to replace his own, which was destroyed from his fight with Kaigaku. Jigoro was also cremated, as shown by Zenitsu carrying his ashes when traversing to live with the Kamado siblings in their old house; he grew angry when Inosuke asked if he could eat them.


Abilities

Overall Abilities

As a former Hashira of the Demon Slayer Corps, Jigoro was known to have been a very powerful and talented swordsman. His combat style was Thunder Breathing, which he was well-versed in. Throughout his active time in the organization, he faced various demons. After losing his leg in battle, he decided to retire from his position as a Hashira.


Fighting Style

Breathing Style

Thunder Breathing (雷かみなりの呼こ吸きゅう Kaminari no kokyū): Jigoro is the primary instructor of this Breathing Style. He was able to teach Zenitsu and Kaigaku how to use all original 6 forms, although Zenitsu could only master the first form and Kaigaku only mastered the second through sixth.


Note: The second to sixth forms of Thunder Breathing have only been displayed by Kaigaku, who uses actual lightning in conjunction with his swordsmanship. While the forms are presumably still similar to the original style, it is currently unknown how a normal swordsman would perform these techniques, or if mastery of the first form would alter how the others are executed.


Techniques

  • First Form: Thunderclap and Flash (壱いちノ型かた　霹へき靂れき一いっ閃せん Ichi no kata: Hekireki Issen) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 23 (Page 9). [9] - The user unsheathes their katana, dashes forward at blinding speeds and decapitates their opponent faster than they can react, before sheathing it back. This technique is extremely reminiscent of laijustsu and its movements are described to be akin to "lightning ripping through the air". Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 125 (Page 6). [10]
  • Second Form: Rice Spirit (弐にノ型かた　稲いな魂だま Ni no kata: Inadama) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 145 (Page 7). [11] - The user delivers five, consecutive, arching lightning slashes in quick succession in a single breath.
  • Third Form: Thunder Swarm (参さんノ型かた　聚しゅう蚊ぶん成せい雷らい San no kata: Shūbun Seirai) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 145 (Page 8). [12] - The user rapidly circles around the enemy and assaults them with a series of wide, arched, lightning slashes from all directions, surrounding them in a series of afte../..rimages.
  • Fourth Form: Distant Thunder (肆しノ型かた　遠えん雷らい Shi no kata: Enrai) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 144 (Page 17). [13] - The user charges forward at high speeds and releases a multitude of electric slashes in a single sword strike.
  • Fifth Form: Heat Lightning (伍ごノ型かた　熱ねつ界かい雷らい Go no kata: Netsu Kairai) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 145 (Page 9). [14] - The user takes a stance and swings their sword upwards, electrocuting their opponent with lightning.
  • Sixth Form: Rumble and Flash (陸ろくノ型かた　電でん轟ごう雷らい轟ごう Roku no kata: Dengō Raigō) Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Chapter 145 (Page 10). [15] - The user unleashes a horizontal slash, which releases a series of powerful long-ranged lightning attacks. The multitude and range ot these bolts makes the form difficult to evade.

Relationships

Zenitsu Agatsuma

Zenitsu was Jigoro's last known student, adopting the boy as his apprentice after rescuing him from severe debt. Jigoro was aggravated by Zenitsu's cowardice nd tantrums, frequently hitting him as discipline, but he never gave up on his student, despite his frequent attempts to run away. He even allowed Zenitsu to call him "gramps" instead of "master", after Zenitsu told that he calls him gramps becasue he loved him.


Even Zenitsu was briefly on the brink of death after defeating Kaigaku, he reunited with Jigoro who tearfully told him that he was his pride and joy. Zenitsu would later take his haori as a memento and carry his ashes to the Kamado household.


Kaigaku

Jigoro had adopted Kaigaku before Zenitsu. Due to his talent, he viewed Kaigaku as a potential role model for Zenitsu, and told his younger student to follow his senior's example. At first, Kaigaku was satisfied with being the student of a former Hashira, although he grew resentful when Jigoro began to train Zenitsu. Despite him valuing both his students and even gifting Kaigaku a triangle-patterned haori, Kaigaku thought that Jigoro was wasting his time training someone as cowardly as Zenitsu and was infuriated when he decided to make Zenitsu his co-successor. Kaigaku reasoned that this was due to favoritism and as such was not regretful of Jigoro's grief-filled suicide after he became a demon.


Trivia

  • Jigoro's given name contains the Go-on pronunciation for "mercy, benevolent" (慈 ji), the Ka-on reading for "enlightenment, realize" (悟 go), and the common male suffix of "male child, son" (郎 ro). Meanwhile, his surname contains the Kun reading for "mulberry" (桑 kuwa) and "island" (島 shima/jima).
  • Jigoro's title as a Hashira was revealed to be unique, as he was not called the Thunder Hashira, instead bearing the name of the "Rumble Hashira" (鳴なり柱ばしら Nari Bashira). Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Volume 17 (Page 5). [1] This title was derived from the Izumi old word for "thunder" (神鳴 kaminari), as in "God's Roaring", rather than the modern radical for "thunder" (雷 kaminari), which is used to write the current Thunder Breathing and it's styles. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Volume 17. [16]
  • Jigoro lost his right leg from the knee down in the middle of a battle when he was 35 years old, which is also the reason why he retired from being a Hashira. Kimetsu no Yaiba First Fanbook [17]
  • Jigoro gave Kaigaku a kimono with the same patternas his, but he never wore it. Kimetsu no Yaiba Manga: Volume 17. [16]
  • Unlike Sakonji and Shinjuro Rengoku, Jigoro is the only retired Hashira to never be shown in his prime. However, he makes a cameo as a younger man in Kizuna noi Kiseki, although only through a back view.
